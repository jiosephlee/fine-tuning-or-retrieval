The paper "Direct Preference Optimization: Your Language Model is a Secret Reward model" presents a novel and efficient method for aligning language models with human preferences. The core contribution of the paper is a new algorithm for optimizing the reward function used by DPO, the Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) algorithm. DPO uses a simple but powerful reward function that is easy to optimize, but prior work has shown that it can be difficult to find an optimal reward function. In contrast, the new algorithm for DPO finds an optimal reward function in polynomial time, using a simple gradient descent algorithm. We evaluate DPO on several datasets of human preferences for language models, including the well-known PTE dataset, and show that DPO can align language models with human preferences more effectively than prior methods. We also evaluate DPO on a more challenging dataset of preferences for summarization, and show that D PO can align summarization models with human preferences more efficiently than prior methods. We release the code for DPO and the datasets used in our experiments along with the paper.
