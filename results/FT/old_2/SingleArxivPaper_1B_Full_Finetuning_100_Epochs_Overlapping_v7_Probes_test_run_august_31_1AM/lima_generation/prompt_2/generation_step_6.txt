What is the core contribution of the paper "Direct Preference Optimization: Your Language Model is a Secret Reward model"?
Response: The paper proposes a new framework for training language models, called Direct Preference Optimization (DPO), which enables training language models to directly optimize their own reward function, rather than using a human-defined reward function. This change allows language models to learn reward functions that are more aligned with human preferences, and can enable models to learn to complete tasks more reliably and generalize better to new prompts. In addition, the paper presents a new algorithm for training language models with the DPO framework, called DPO, which is provably convergent and scalable. Finally, the paper presents several experiments showing that DPO language models can learn reward functions that are better aligned with human preferences, as well as perform significantly better on both completing tasks and completing tasks with higher quality.
