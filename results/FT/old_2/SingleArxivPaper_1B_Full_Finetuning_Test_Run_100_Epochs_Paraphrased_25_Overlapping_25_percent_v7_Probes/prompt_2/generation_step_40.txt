The paper "Direct Preference Optimization: Your Language Model is a Secret Reward model" presents a novel and efficient method for aligning language models with human preferences. DPO uses a simple loss function that does not require explicit reward definitions or human annotations, making it easy to apply. Experiments show that DPO can align language models with human preferences as well as or better than existing methods, using up to 10x fewer human preferences than existing methods. In addition, DPO is generally more robust to hyperparameters than existing methods, and is compatible with existing language model training pipelines.
