The paper "Direct Preference Optimization: Your Language Model is a Secret Reward model" presents a novel and efficient method for aligning language models with human preferences. The core contribution of the paper is a new algorithm for optimizing the reward function used by language models, called Direct Preference Optimization (DPO). DPO is an end-to-end training procedure that leverages the power of reinforcement learning to learn a reward function from human preferences without requiring explicit human feedback or a preference dataset. We evaluate DPO on several preference benchmarking tasks, including sentiment analysis, fact verification, and movie recommendation. On all tasks, DPO outperforms existing methods, including baselines that fine-tune large pre-trained language models with manual instructions on how to answer questions and methods that optimize a preference dataset derived from human preferences. Our experiments also show that DPO can learn rewards that are more aligned with human preferences than rewards learned by existing preference models.
