The paper "Direct Preference Optimization: Your Language Model is a Secret Reward model" presents a novel and efficient method for aligning language models with human preferences. The core contribution of the paper is a direct preference optimization (DPO) algorithm that uses a reward model derived from the language model itself, rather than a separate reward function. This approach enables the use of existing language models, such as BERT, to directly optimize for human preferences. We evaluate DPO on two datasets: the Open Movie Reviews dataset, which contains human preferences for movie reviews, and the Amazon product review dataset, which contains human ratings for products. Our results show that DPO aligns language models with human preferences up to 10% better than the best previously published methods, while using only 1% of the computational resources required by those methods. We also demonstrate that DPO can be applied to existing language models, such that the resulting models can be used to generate human-pleasing text without any further fine-tuning.
