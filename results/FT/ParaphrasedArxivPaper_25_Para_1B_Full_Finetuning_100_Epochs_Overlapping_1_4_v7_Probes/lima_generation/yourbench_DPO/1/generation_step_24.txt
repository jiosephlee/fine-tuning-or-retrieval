Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE):
The primary objective of MLE is to find the parameter values that make the observed data most probable. Mathematically, this involves finding the value of the parameter that maximizes the likelihood function, denoted as $p(\mathbf{x}|\theta)$. Here, $\mathbf{x}$ represents the dataset, and $\theta$ denotes the vector of unknown parameters. By applying mathematical optimization techniques, the MLE solution can be obtained analytically, providing a straightforward way to determine the optimal parameter estimates.
