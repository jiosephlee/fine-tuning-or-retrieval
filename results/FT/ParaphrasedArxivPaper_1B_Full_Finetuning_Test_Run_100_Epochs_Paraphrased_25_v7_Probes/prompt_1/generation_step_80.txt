The paper introduces a new method for training language models, called Direct Preference Optimization (DPO). DPO is based on the idea that we can learn a reward function from the preferences of humans over generated text. This reward function can then be used to train a language model. The paper also introduces a new loss function, called the DPO loss, which is a variant of the KL-regularized cross-entropy loss. The DPO loss is defined as follows:

\begin{equation} \mathcal{L}_D(P, y) = -\mathbb{E}_{p(y\mid x)\mid\text{DPO}}[\log p(y\mid x)] + \beta \mathbb{E}_{\pi\mid x}[y\log\pi(y\mid x) - \log\sigma(\beta\mid x)] \end{equation}
