What is RLHF?

RLHF is a framework for training language models with reinforcement learning. It stands for "Reinforcement Learning from Humans". The basic idea is to view the language model as a reward model, that is, a model that produces a reward value for every completion of a dialogue. The reward can be thought of as the "goodness" of the dialogue. The RLHF framework provides a way to learn the reward model from human preferences. That is, given two completions of a dialogue, one produced by the language model and one produced by a human, the framework can determine how much better the human completion is and use this information to update the language model.
