What is Direct Preference Optimization?

Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) is a method to train language models to generate text that satisfies human preferences. It is a simple and effective method. It is also a general framework that can be used to train language models to satisfy any human preferences.

DPO is based on the following idea: We can train a language model to generate text that satisfies a human preference by maximizing the probability of the human preference and minimizing the probability of the human dispreference.

The following is the mathematical expression of DPO:

$$\mathop{max}\limits_{x \in \mathcal{X}} \mathop{min}\limits_{y \in \mathcal{T}} \mathop{log}\limits_{\theta} \frac{P_{\theta}(y|x)}{P_{\theta}(\neg{y}|x)}$$

Where $\mathcal{X}$ is the set of all possible texts, $\mathcal{T}$ is the set of texts that satisfy the human preference, $P_{\theta}(x)$ is the probability of generating text $x$ with language model $\theta$, $P_{\theta}({\neg{y}}|x)$ is the probability that the human dispreference ${\neg{y}}$ is generated after the text $x$ is generated.

The following is the process of DPO:

  1. Generate a large number of texts $x$ with the language model $\theta$.
  2. For each text $x$, find the set of texts $\mathcal{T}$ that satisfy the human preference.
  3. For each text $y$ in $\mathcal{T}$, calculate the probability $P_{\theta(y|x)}$ that $y$ is generated after $x$ is generated.
  4. For each text $z$ in $\mathcal{X}\backslash\mathcal{T}$,calculate the probability $P_{z|x}$ that $z$ is generated after $y$ is generated.
  • If $P_{\theta(z|x)} > P_{\theta(y|x)}>P_{\theta(\neg{y}|y)}$, then $z$ is the human dispreference of $y$.
  • If $z$ is the dispreference of $x$, then $P_{\theta(\text{dispreference of }x|x)}$ is the probability that the dispreference of the text $x$ will be generated.
  • If the probability $P_{y|x}$ is large, then $y$ is the preference of $x$.
  • If the preference of $x$, $y$, is large, then $P_{\text{preference of }x\text{ and }y|x}$ is the probability that the preference of $x\text{ and}y$ will be generated.
