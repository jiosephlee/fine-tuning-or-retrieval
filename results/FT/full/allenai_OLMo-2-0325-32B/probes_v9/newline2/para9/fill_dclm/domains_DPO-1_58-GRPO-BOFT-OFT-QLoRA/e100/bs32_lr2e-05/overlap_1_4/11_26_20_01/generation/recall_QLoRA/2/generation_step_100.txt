### What is Quantization?

Quantization is a technique used to reduce the amount of memory required to store and process data by converting data from a representation that uses more bits to one that uses fewer bits. In the context of neural networks, quantization typically refers to the process of converting weights and activations from 32-bit floating point numbers to 8-bit integers or even lower precision formats. This can significantly decrease the memory footprint of a model, making it possible to run larger models on devices with limited resources.
