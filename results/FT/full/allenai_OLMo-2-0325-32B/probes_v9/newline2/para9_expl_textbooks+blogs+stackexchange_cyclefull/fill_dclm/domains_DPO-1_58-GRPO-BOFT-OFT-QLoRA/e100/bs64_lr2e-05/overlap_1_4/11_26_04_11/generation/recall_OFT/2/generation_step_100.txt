Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT) is a simple yet effective approach that involves learning an orthogonal transformation for each neuron layer, thereby keeping the pairwise angles between neurons constant. This approach has been shown to maintain the semantic fidelity of the pretrained diffusion model while enabling precise control over the generated images. The authors demonstrate the effectiveness of OFT for three distinct control tasks: class-specific generation, controllable generation, and text-to-image diffusion finetuning. The core idea behind OFT is to learn an orthogonal transformation for each layer of the neural network. This is achieved by parameterizing the orthogonal matrix using a Cayley transform, which ensures that the matrix remains orthogonal throughout training. The orthogonal matrix is then multiplied with the pretrained neuron weights, resulting in a transformed weight matrix that is used during forward propagation.
