EfficientFinetuning is a framework that unifies several memory-efficient finetuning methods, including Zero-Shot-Kaiming-Init, LoRA, and Sparse Training. The core idea behind this framework is to train a set of projection matrices that transform the input of each layer, thereby modifying how the pre-trained weights operate. This approach differs from conventional finetuning methods, which update the weights directly. Instead, EfficientFinetuning modifies the weights through a transformation, resulting in a more memory-efficient finetuning process.
