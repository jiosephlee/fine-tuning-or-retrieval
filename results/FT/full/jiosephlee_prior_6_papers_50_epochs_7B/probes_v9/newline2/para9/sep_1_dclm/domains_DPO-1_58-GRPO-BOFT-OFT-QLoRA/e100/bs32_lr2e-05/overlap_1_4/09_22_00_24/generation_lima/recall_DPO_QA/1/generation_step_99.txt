By providing additional preference data during training, we can teach the model to align with these preferences.

The DPO approach involves two main steps. First, the model is trained on a large corpus of human-written text, which includes implicit preferences. This means that DPO does not require the use of a separate reward model, as it can be applied directly to the preference data.
