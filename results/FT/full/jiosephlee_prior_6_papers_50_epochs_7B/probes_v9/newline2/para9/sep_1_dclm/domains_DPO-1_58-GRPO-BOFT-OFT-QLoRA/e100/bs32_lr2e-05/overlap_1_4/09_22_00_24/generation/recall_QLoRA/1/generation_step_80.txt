Introduction

Large language models (LLMs), particularly those based on the Transformer architecture, have become increasingly powerful. # Prompt tuning

Prompt tuning is a technique for adapting a pretrained LLM to a specific downstream task by adjusting the way inputs are phrased. The central idea is that the model’s outputs depend heavily on the input prompt; thus, by optimizing the prompt, we can steer the model’s responses toward greater accuracy on the target domain. This approach is sometimes referred to as “input-output tuning” because it involves finding the most effective combination of input and desired output.
