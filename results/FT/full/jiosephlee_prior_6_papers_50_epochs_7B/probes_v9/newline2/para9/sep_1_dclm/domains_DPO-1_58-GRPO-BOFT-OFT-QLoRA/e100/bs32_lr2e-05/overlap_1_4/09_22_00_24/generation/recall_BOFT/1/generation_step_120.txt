Introduction

Model quantization is a widely used technique to compress LLMs for deployment. Quantization converts model weights and activations from floating-point representations to lower-bit formats, such as int8 or int4. A critical challenge in quantization is preserving the accuracy of the original model. To address this, calibration techniques are employed to map the distribution of values in the model to the target quantization range.
