# Tokenization, word embeddings, recurrent neural networks (RNs)

Tokenization is the process of converting raw text into discrete units called tokens. Tokens can be individual words, character n-grams, or subword units depending on the chosen strategy. Word-level tokenization is simple but leads to very large vocabularies, which can be inefficient for training and inference. Character-level tokenization addresses this by using a small vocabulary but often results in long sequences that make learning difficult.
