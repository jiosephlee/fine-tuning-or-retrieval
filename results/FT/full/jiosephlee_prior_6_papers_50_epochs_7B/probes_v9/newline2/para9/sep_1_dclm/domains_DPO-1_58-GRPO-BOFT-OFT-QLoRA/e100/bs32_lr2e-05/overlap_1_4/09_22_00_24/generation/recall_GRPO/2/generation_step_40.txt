# DeepSeekMath: Pushing Mathematical Reasoning in Open-Source Language Models

The DeepSeekMath project aims to push the limits of mathematical reasoning in open-source language models. The project introduces a new method for training language models on mathematical tasks called Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRP), which is a memory-efficient alternative to Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO). The GRPO method is used to fine-tune DeepSeek-Coder, a 7B-parameter open-source language model, on the MATH dataset, a collection of 1.5 million mathematical problems from high school and college competitions. The resulting model, DeepSeek-Coder-Math, achieves a score of 80.2% on the MMLUs-STEM benchmark, which includes questions from high school and undergraduate mathematics competitions. This result surpasses the scores of proprietary models such as GPT-4, which achieves a score of 77.9% on the same benchmark.

The DeepSeek-Coder-MATH model is made available to the public, along with the code and data used to train it. The authors hope that this will encourage further research into mathematical reasoning in language models and facilitate the development of more advanced models.
