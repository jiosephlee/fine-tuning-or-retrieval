It aims to address the issue that, when training on GPU with the original training recipe, the quantized model quickly degenerates. 

The main idea behind QLoRA is to selectively dequantize the model: during the first training epoch, the algorithm reverts the quantization of neurons one by  one, based on their importance as determined by their influence on the generation process. This way, the most important neurons benefit from the higher precision of FP16, while the less important ones are kept in INT8, where their values have been stored in the first place.
