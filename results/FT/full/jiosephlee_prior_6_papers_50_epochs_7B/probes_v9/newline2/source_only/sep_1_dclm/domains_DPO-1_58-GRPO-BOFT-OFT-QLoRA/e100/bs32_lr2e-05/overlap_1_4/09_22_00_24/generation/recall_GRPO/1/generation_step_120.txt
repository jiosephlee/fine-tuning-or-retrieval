# What is DeepSeekMath?

DeepSeekMath is an open-source project that aims to push the limits of mathematical reasoning in open language models. It is based on DeepSeek-Coder-v1.5B, which is a 1.5B parameter model pretrained on code and large-scale web text. The project is developed by DeepSeek, a Beijing-based AI company.

DeepSeekMath introduces a series of innovations in training objectives, training data, training recipes, and evaluation protocols. These innovations are designed to improve the model's ability to perform mathematical reasoning tasks, such as multiple-choice problems from competitions like MATH and GSM8K, as well as more complex tasks like GSM7K and MMLU.

# Training objectives

The training objectives of DeepSeekMath are mainly focused on improving the model's ability to solve mathematical reasoning tasks. The main objective is to maximize the probability of the correct answer choice given a mathematical problem and its context. In addition, the model is trained to minimize the probability of wrong answer choices and to produce step-by-step solutions.

Another important objective is to enhance the model's ability to generate high-quality natural language explanations for answers. To achieve this, the model is trained on a dataset of math questions with human-generated explanations, and it is encouraged to produce its own explanations when answering questions.

Furthermore, DeepSeekMath improves the model's ability to produce step-by-step reasoning traces using a trace reconstruction objective. The model is trained on a trace dataset generated from human demonstrations, and it is optimized to reproduce the correct traces when answering questions.

Finally, DeepSeekMath also optimizes the model to produce concise and accurate final answers, and to generate solutions efficiently. All these objectives are combined into a single loss function, which is used during the fine-tuning stage.
