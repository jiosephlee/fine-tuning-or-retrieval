It aims to address the issue that, when training on GPU with the original LLM finetuning strategy, the trained model can still have significant quantization error. This is particularly noticeable for models quantized at 4-bit.

The main idea behind QLoRA is to first train with a large learning rate (e.g., 5e-4) for several epochs to recover performance. After that, the learning rate is reduced and trained further with a standard 5e-5. This approach helps the model to converge faster and stabilize training. In addition, it effectively addresses the problem of vanishing gradients during quantization error backpropagation.

In practice, we can apply QLoRA in finetuning 4-bit LLMs as follows:

1. Use a learning rate of 5e-4 for the first 10 epochs to allow the model to correct quantization errors.
2. Reduce the learning rate to 5e-5 for the following training process.
