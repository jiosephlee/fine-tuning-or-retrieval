# SmoothQuant

SmoothQuant is an iterative re- quantization algorithm that improves the weight distribution after quantization. It is designed to reduce the outliers in the weight distribution caused by the absolute deviation from zero. The algorithm works by iteratively re-quantizing the weights with a new scale factor after smoothing the weights. The key idea behind SmoothQuant is to first smooth the weight distribution by subtracting an offset that minimizes the squared error, and then rescaling the smoothed weights to bring more weights closer to zero. The algorithm can be summarized as follows:

1. Initialize the weight tensor $W$ and a smoothing mask $M$ with size $C\times H\times W$ where $C$ is the number of channels, $H$ is the height, and $W$ is the width.
2. For each element $w$ of $W$, compute the centroid $\bar{w}$ of its $2^q$ neighboring elements using $M$.
3. Subtract the centroid from $w$ to obtain the deviation $\delta=w-\bar{w}$.
4. Compute a smoothing coefficient $\sigma$ that minimizes the squared deviation of $\delta$.
5. Update $W=\bar{w}+\sigma\cdot \delta$.
6. Rescale $W$ with a new scale factor to bring more weights closer zero.
