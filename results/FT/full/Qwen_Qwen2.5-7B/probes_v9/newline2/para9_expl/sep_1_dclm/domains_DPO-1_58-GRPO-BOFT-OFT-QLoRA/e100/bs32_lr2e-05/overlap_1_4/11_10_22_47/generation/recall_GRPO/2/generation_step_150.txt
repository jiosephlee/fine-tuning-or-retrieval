The paper "DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models" presents a novel and efficient method for improving mathematical capabilities in language models, Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO), instead of the standard memory-intensive PPO algorithm. Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) is an adaptation of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO), which is a reinforcement learning technique used to train language models. GRPO reduces the computational burden by eliminating the need for a critic network and replacing it with a group-based baseline. This modification allows GRPO to be applied to larger model sizes without requiring excessive memory resources.

The authors apply GRPO to train DeepSeekMath-Base 7B, a foundational model that has been pre-trained on a diverse dataset including 500B tokens from Common Crawl, with an emphasis on mathematical content. The training process focuses on improving mathematical reasoning skills through a curriculum of tasks ranging from basic arithmetic to advanced competition-level problems. The evaluation of DeepSeekMath-Base includes assessments on established benchmarks such as MATH, GSM8K, and CMATH, demonstrating strong performance compared to other open-source models like Mistral 7B and Llemma 34B. Additionally, the model’s ability to solve mathematical problems is showcased by its successful integration into a theorem prover, where it assists in formalizing proofs.

One of the key innovations in this work is the development of MathTrainingDataset, a carefully curated collection of 120K chain-of-thought formatted questions and answers sourced from the GSM8K and MATH datasets. This dataset serves as a valuable resource for evaluating and advancing mathematical reasoning in language models.

DeepSeekMath-Instruct 7B builds upon DeepSeekMath-Base by further training it using 500K instruction-tuning examples derived from platforms like GitHub and English-Chinese bilingual instruction corpora. These examples are selected based on their mathematical relevance, resulting in a model that excels in both Chinese and English mathematical instruction tasks. The research also introduces a new Chinese mathematical instruction benchmark called CMATH, which consists of 75K questions spanning multiple-choice and free-response formats. DeepSeekMath-Instruct achieves top results on this benchmark, outperforming leading systems such as Gemini-Ultra, GPT-4, and others.

In summary, the paper presents a practical approach to enhancing mathematical reasoning in large language models by employing Group Relative Policy Optimization (a streamlined version of PPO) and demonstrates the effectiveness of these methods through rigorous evaluations and novel datasets. The resulting models, DeepSeekMath-Base and DeepSeekMath-Instruct, establish new standards in open-source mathematical reasoning and provide robust support for automated proof generation.
