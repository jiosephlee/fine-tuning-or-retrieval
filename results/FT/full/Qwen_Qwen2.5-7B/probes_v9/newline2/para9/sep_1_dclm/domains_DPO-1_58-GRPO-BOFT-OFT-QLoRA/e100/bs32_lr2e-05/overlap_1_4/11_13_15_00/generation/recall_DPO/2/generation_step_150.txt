Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) is a straightforward approach that modifies the binary cross-entropy loss to directly optimize for human preferences without relying on reinforcement learning or explicit reward modeling. DPO is theoretically grounded in a reparameterized Bradley-Terry model, which allows it to be viewed as a direct optimization of an implicit reward function derived from preference data.
