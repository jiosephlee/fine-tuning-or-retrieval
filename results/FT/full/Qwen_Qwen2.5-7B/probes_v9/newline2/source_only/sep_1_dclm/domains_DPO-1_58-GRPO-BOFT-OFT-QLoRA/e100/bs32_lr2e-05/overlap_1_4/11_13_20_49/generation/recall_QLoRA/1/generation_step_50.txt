## 1. Introduction to Q LoRA

The primary goal of QLoRA is to address the memory bottleneck issue in finetuning large language models. Traditional methods, such as full finetuning, involve training the entire model from scratch using gradient-based optimization techniques. This approach is computationally expensive and requires substantial memory resources, making it impractical for many applications.

To overcome this limitation, QLoRA introduces a Parameter-Efficient Finetuning (PEFT)-based method that focuses on fine-tuning only a small weight matrix within the LLM. By doing so, QLoRA significantly reduces memory requirements while still achieving comparable performance to full finetuning.

The core idea behind QLoRA is to target the low-rank component of the attention weights, which are the most frequently used during inference queries. By efficiently updating this specific component, QLoRA effectively adapts the pre-trained LLM to new tasks without altering the majority of its weights.
