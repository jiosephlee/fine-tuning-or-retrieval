Reinforcement learning (RL) is a subfield of machine learning that deals with learning policies from interactions with an environment. At its core, RL seeks to find a policy that maximizes expected cumulative reward, given the current state and action. The policy π is then defined as the mapping from states to actions, i.e., π : S → A.
