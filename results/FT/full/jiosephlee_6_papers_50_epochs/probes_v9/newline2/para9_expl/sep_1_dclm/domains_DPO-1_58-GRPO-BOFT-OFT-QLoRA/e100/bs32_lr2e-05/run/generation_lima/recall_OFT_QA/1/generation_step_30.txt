According to the paper "Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning", what is Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT)?<|EOT|>

The OFT method is a finetuning method that uses an orthogonal matrix $Q$ to transform the pretrained weights of a model. The orthogonal matrix $Q$ is chosen so that the transformation is invertible, and the resulting weight matrix is orthogonal. This ensures that the learned parameters are not affected by the transformation, which is important for preserving the original model's behavior.
