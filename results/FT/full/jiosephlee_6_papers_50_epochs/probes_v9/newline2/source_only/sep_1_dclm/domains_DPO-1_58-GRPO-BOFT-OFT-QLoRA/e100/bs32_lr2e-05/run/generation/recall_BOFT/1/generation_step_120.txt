Motivation
The goal of finetuning is to fine-tune a pretrained model to improve its performance on a downstream task. However, finetuning is usually very expensive because it requires retraining the entire model from scratch. To address this problem, researchers have proposed parameter-efficient finetuning (PEFT) methods. PEFT methods reduce the number of trainable parameters in the finetuned model while preserving its performance. The most widely used PEFT method is finetuning from scratch (F2S), which is simply retraining the entire model. However, F2S has a few limitations. First, it requires a large amount of memory and computational resources. Second, it is sensitive to the initialization of the model weights. Third, it is not parameter-efficient. Therefore, researchers have proposed many parameter-efficient finetuning methods.
