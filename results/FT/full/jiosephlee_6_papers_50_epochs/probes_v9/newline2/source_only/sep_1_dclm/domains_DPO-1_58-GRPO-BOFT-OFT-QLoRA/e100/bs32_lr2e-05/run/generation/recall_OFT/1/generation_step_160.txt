The text-to-image diffusion model

Before we talk about OFT, let's first understand the text-to-image diffusion model. The text-to-image diffusion model is a generative model that can generate images from textual prompts. It is trained by minimizing the cross-entropy loss between the generated image and the reference image. The generative model is based on the principle of text-to-image diffusion. The text-to-image model can be roughly divided into two parts: encoder and decoder. The encoder maps the text prompt into a high-dimensional representation, and the decoder then generates an image from the representation. The encoder and decoder are usually convolutional neural networks. The text-to-image generation process is shown in Figure 1.

Figure 1: The process of text-to-image generation

Text-to-image diffusion models are usually trained by minimizing the cross entropy loss between the generated image $x$ and the reference image $y$:

$\min \frac{1}{2}\mathbb{E}_{x,y\sim P_{\theta}}[H(x; \theta) - H(y; \theta)]$

where $P_{\theta}$ is the training dataset, and $H(x; \theta)$ is the generative model given the text prompt $x$ and the parameters $\theta$.

Catastrophic forgetting

Catastrophic forgetting refers to the phenomenon that a model trained on a certain task loses its ability to perform other tasks. In the context of text-to-image diffusion model, the model is trained to generate images from text prompts, but it is not optimized to generate images from different text prompts. Therefore, the model tends to forget the ability to generate images from other text prompts during the training process. This phenomenon is also called "catastrophic forgetting". The reason for this phenomenon is that the training data of the model is limited, and the model cannot explore a large number of data to train itself. Therefore, the model is unable to learn the generalization ability of the model. In other words, the model is unable or unwilling to learn new knowledge.

Orthogonal finetuning

To solve the problem of catastrophic forgetting, the authors propose a finetuning method called orthogonal finetune. OFT is a finetting method that uses orthogonal transformation. The basic idea of OFT is to transform the input data before finetuning, so that the finetuning process can be carried out without causing catastrophic forgetting. The OFT finetuning method is shown in Algorithm 1.

The OFT finetuning algorithm is simple and effective. It can prevent the model from forgetting the ability to generate images by transforming the input data before finetting. The OFT finetting method is also called orthogonal finetuning. The OFT fineting method is based on the idea of orthogonal transformation. The orthogonal transformation is a linear transformation that preserves the inner product of vectors. In other words, orthogonal transformation does not change the angle between two vectors, and the length of the vector remains unchanged. Therefore, orthogonal transformation can preserve the information of the input data, and it is suitable for data augmentation. In the text-to-image diffusion context, OFT can be applied to the encoder and decoder of the text-to-image diffusion models to prevent the model from forgetting its ability to generate images from different prompts.
