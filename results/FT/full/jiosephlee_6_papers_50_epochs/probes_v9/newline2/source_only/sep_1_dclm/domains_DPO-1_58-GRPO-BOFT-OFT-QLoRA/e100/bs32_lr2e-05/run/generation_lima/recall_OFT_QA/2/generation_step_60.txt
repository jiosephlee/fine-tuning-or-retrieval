The key idea behind OFT is to use orthogonal matrices to rotate the input data before it enters the network. This rotation preserves the angles between neurons, which is crucial for preserving the spatial layout and the orientation of neurons in the network. By rotating the input data, OFT can effectively control the directionality of neurons without changing their magnitude, which is why it is particularly effective for preserving the orientation of neuron directions.
