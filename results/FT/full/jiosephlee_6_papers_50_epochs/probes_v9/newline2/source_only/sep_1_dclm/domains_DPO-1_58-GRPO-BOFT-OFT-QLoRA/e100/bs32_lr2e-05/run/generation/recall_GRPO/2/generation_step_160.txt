Introduction
The ability to perform mathematical reasoning is an important capability for language models (LMs). LMs have already demonstrated impressive capabilities in natural language reasoning (NLP), but the ability to reason with numbers and symbols is still relatively underdeveloped. This is particularly true for LMs trained on large open-source datasets, such as GPT-4 (Brown et al., 2022), which have not been specifically designed to improve mathematical reasoning. As a result, LMs trained on such datasets often struggle with mathematical tasks, such as solving equations, proving the existence of solutions, and checking proofs.
