Theoretical background

Zero-shot language model finetuning relies on the principle of transfer learning, which states that a model that has already been trained on a large dataset can be effectively retrained on a new dataset without losing its initial performance. Transfer learning is based on the assumption that the model has learned generalizable representations that can be reused across different tasks. When applied to language models, transfer learning enables us to leverage the knowledge acquired during the initial training phase and apply it to a new task without retraining from scratch.
