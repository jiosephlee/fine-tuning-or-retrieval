According to the paper "Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning", what is Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT)?<|EOT|>

The OFT method is a finetuning method that uses an orthogonal matrix to transform the pretrained weights. The idea is to use a low-rank orthogonal matrix to change the direction of the weight vectors, which can effectively reduce the influence of the original weight matrix on the final result. In this way, the original weight matrix can be used to control the final result, and the final result can be controlled by changing the orthogonal matrix.
