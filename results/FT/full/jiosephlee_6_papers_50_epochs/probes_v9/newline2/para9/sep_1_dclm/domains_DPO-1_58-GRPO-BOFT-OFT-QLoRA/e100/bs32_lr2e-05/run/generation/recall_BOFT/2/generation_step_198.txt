Introduction
Large language models (LLMs) have become increasingly powerful and widely used in various applications. However, these models are often trained on very large datasets, which can lead to memory and computational issues when deploying them in production. To address this, many researchers have proposed methods to finetune LLMs on smaller datasets while maintaining their performance. One such method is Orthogonal Finetuning, which uses an orthogonal matrix to transform the pretrained weights of a model. This approach has been shown to be effective in improving the performance of LLMs on downstream tasks without requiring additional data.
However, Orthogonal Finetuning has limitations. For example, it requires the use of a full orthogonal matrix, which can be computationally expensive.
