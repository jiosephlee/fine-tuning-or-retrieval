## What is LoRA?

LoRA (Low-rank Adapter) is a technique for finetuning large language model (LLM) parameters without changing the original model weights. Instead, LoRA adds a small set of extra parameters (called adapters) that are multiplied by the original model outputs. This makes the training process much faster and uses much less memory than full finetuning.
