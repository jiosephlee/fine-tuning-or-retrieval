## What is the difference between a quantile and a percentile?

A quantile is a value that divides a group of data points into equal parts. A percentile is a value that represents the percentage of data points that fall below it.

For example, if we have a dataset of 100 values, the 25th percentile would be the value that is greater than or equal to 25% of the data points, and less than or equal to 75% of the data points. The 50th percentile, also known as the median, would be the value that separates the upper half of the data from the lower half.

On the other hand, a quantile is a value at a specific position in the sorted data. For example, the 25th quantile would be the value that has 25% of the values below it and 75% above it. The 50th quantile would be equal to the median.

In summary, a quantile is an exact position in the sorted data, while a percentile is a percentage of the data that falls below a certain value.
