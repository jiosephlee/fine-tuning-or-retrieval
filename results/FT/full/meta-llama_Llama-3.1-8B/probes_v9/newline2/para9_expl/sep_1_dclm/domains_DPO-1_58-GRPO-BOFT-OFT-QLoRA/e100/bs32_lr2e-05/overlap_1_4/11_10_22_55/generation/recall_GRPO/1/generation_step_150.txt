# DeepSeekMath: Pushing Limits of Mathematical Reasoning

## Abstract

Large language models (LLMs) have demonstrated remarkable capabilities in mathematical reasoning, yet their proficiency in solving complex problems remains limited. This work presents DeepSeekMath, a model that substantially advances mathematical reasoning in open-source systems. DeepSeekMath is built upon DeepSeek-Coder- Base-v1.5  7B, undergoing pre-training over 500B tokens, with a portion of the data comprising mathematical content sourced from Common Crawl, ArXiv, and AlgebraicStack. We conduct an extensive evaluation of DeepSeekMath’s mathematical reasoning skills across eight distinct benchmarks—spanning English and Chinese datasets, ranging from elementary to collegiate-level challenges—and compare its performance against leading open- and closed-source models, including Gemini-Ultra, GPT-4, Gemini Pro, and Inflection-2. DeepSeekMath consistently achieves top results, outperforming all other open-source alternatives by at least 10 percentage points on the M ATH benchmark. Notably, on the MATH dataset, DeepSeekMath attains an accuracy of 51.9%, surpassing the previous best open-source system by more than 10 percentage points. Additionally, we investigate strategies to enhance mathematical reasoning within LLMs. Our findings indicate that self-consistency sampling, when applied to a single model, can boost performance by up to 6 percentage points on MATH. Furthermore, we introduce Chain-of-Thought Reasoning (CoT), a straightforward approach that involves LLMs generating stepwise natural language explanations for mathematical problems, thereby improving both accuracy and robustness in mathematical reasoning. We also present Program-of-Thought (PoT), an extension of CoT wherein models produce executable programs to solve mathematical tasks. Experimental results demonstrate that PoT enhances the effectiveness of DeepSeekMath on benchmarks such as GSM8K and MMLU- STEM, even when compared to state-of-the-art code- augmented models like DeepSeek-Coder- base -v1.5. Lastly, we propose a unified framework that encompasses CoT, PoT, and Tool-Augmented Reasoning (TA R ), facilitating a deeper understanding of their respective strengths and limitations.
