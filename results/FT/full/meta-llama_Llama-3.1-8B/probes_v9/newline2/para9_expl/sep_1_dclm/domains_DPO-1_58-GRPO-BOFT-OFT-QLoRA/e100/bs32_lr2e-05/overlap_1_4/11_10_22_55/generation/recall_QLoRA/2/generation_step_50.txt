# QLoRA: Efficient Finetuing of Quantized LLM

## Abstract

Finetuning large language models (LLM) on downstream tasks has become an essential step to achieve state-of-the-art performance. However, the memory demands of 16-bit finetuing make it challenging to train models larger than 6B parameters on a single consumer GPU. To address this, we introduce QLoRA, a method that enables finetuning of quantized pretrained models using Low-rank Adapters (LoRA). QLoRA leverages 4-bit NormalFloat (NF4) quantization for the pretrained weights, combined with 32-bit storage for the Low-rank Adapter parameters. We demonstrate that QLoRA achieves parity with 16-bit finetued models across a range of tasks, including natural-language understanding (MMLU), reasoning (BBH), and code generation (HumanEval). Notably, QLoRA can finetune a 33B parameter model on a single 24GB GPU, while 16-bit finetue requires at least 80GB of GPU memory. Our results indicate that QLoRA offers a practical and effective solution for finetuing large language models on limited hardware resources.
