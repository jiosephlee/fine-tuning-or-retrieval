Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) is a reinforcement learning technique that uses a group of sampled outputs from the current policy to compute advantages, which are then used to update the policy. This approach reduces memory usage by eliminating the need to store baselines for each output, making it more suitable for large-scale training with limited resources.
