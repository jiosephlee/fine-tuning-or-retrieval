# Controlling Text-to-Image Generation with Orthogonal Finetuning

## Abstract

Recent advances in text-to-image diffusion models have enabled high-fidelity image synthesis from textual descriptions. However, these models often lack the ability to control the generated images effectively. To address this limitation, we propose a general framework for controlling text-to-image diffusion models based on orthogonal finetuning (OFT). Unlike conventional finetuning methods, OFT enforces an orthogonality constraint on the weight matrices of the neural network, ensuring that the pairwise neuron angles are preserved. This constraint not only maintains the semantic information learned during pretraining but also enhances the stability of finetuning. We apply OFT to two types of control settings: subject-driven generation, where the goal is to generate images of a specific subject based on a few reference images and a text prompt; and controllable generation, where the objective is to produce images conditioned on both text and additional control signals. Experimental results demonstrate that our OFT approach achieves superior controllability and generates higher-quality images compared to existing methods.
