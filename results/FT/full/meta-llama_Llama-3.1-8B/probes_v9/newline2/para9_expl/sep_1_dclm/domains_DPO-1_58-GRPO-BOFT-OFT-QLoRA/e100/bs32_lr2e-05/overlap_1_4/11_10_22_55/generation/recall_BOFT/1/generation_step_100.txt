# Parameter-Efficient Orthogonal Finetuning via Factorization

## Abstract

We introduce a new approach to parameter-efficient finetuning (PEFT) of large language models (LLM) called Orthogonal Finetuing (OFT). OFT adapts the pretrained weights of an LLM through multiplication by a trainable orthogonal matrix. This simple modification maintains the pairwise angles between neurons, ensuring that the pretrained semantic information is preserved. However, standard OFT requires a large number of trainable parameters due to the dense nature of the orthogonal matrix. To address this, we propose a novel parameterization of the orthogonal matrix based on butterfly structures, which enables us to construct a dense orthogonal matrix with far fewer trainable parameters. Our method, termed Butterfly Orthogonal Finetuing, achieves strong empirical results while requiring significantly fewer parameters than previous PEFT techniques. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach by finetuning large-scale models on various downstream tasks, including MMLU, ARC-Easy, ARC-Challenge, Hellaswag, Winogrande, PIQA, OpenbookQA, and BoolQ, with results showing that our method surpasses current state-of-the-art PEFT approaches.
