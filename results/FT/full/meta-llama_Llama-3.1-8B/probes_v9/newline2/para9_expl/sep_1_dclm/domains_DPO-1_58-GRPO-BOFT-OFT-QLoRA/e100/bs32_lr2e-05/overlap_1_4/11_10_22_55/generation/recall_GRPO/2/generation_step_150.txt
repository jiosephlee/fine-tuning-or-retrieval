# DeepSeekMath: Pushing The Limits Of Mathematical Reasoning In Open Language Models

## Abstract

Recent advances in mathematical reasoning within open language models have been largely driven by reinforcement learning (RL) fine-tuning on problem-solving datasets. However, the scalability of RL methods remains limited by their substantial memory demands, particularly during the sampling of candidate solutions. To address this challenge, we present Group Relative Policy Optimization (G RPO ), an efficient RL algorithm that eliminates the need for per-example baselines, thereby reducing memory consumption. G RPO leverages a group of samples generated from the current policy to estimate advantages, which are then utilized to update the policy. We apply G RPO to fine-tune a 7B parameter model, DeepSeekMath, which is further evaluated on a range of mathematical benchmarks. Remarkably, DeepSeekMath-Base attains 88.6% accuracy on GSM8K and 51.4 % on MATH, surpassing all existing open-source models as of August 2023. Furthermore, through a series of experiments, we demonstrate that the effectiveness of math-focused pre-training hinges critically on the quality of the underlying math dataset. To facilitate future research, we introduce MMLU-STEM—a new benchmark comprising multiple-choice questions from MMLU spanning science, technology, engineering and mathematics disciplines. Our analysis reveals that math pre-training not merely enhances mathematical reasoning but also contributes to improvements in broader STEM knowledge.
