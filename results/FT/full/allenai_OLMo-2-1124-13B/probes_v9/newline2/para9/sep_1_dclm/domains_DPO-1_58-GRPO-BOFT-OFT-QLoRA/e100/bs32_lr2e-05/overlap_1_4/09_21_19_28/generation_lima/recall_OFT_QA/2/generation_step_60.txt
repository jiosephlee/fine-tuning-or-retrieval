The motivation behind OFT is that it can preserve the crucial geometric properties of neurons that are lost during standard finetuning. 

During finetuning, the parameters of the model are updated to adapt the model to a new task. In the case of text-to-image diffusion models, the pretrained model is finetuned to generate images based on textual descriptions. The standard approach to finetuning is to use a small learning rate to minimize the loss function. However, this approach does not guarantee that the updated parameters will remain close to the pretrained parameters in terms of their geometry. This can lead to the loss of important information that was learned during pretraining.
