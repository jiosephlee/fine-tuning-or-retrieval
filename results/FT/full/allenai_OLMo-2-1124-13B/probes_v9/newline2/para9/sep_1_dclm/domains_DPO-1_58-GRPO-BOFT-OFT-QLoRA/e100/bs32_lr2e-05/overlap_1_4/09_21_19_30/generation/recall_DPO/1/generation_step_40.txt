## What is Direct Preference Optimization?

Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) is a method for training language models that uses human preferences as the loss function. This is in contrast to traditional methods that use maximum likelihood estimation (MLE) or cross-entropy loss.

The basic idea behind DPO is to train a language model to generate completions of a given prefix that are preferred by humans over other completions. This is done by presenting humans with pairs of completions and asking them to choose the one they prefer. The language model is then updated to increase the probability of the preferred completion and decrease the probability of the non-preferred completion.

This approach has several advantages over MLE and cross-entropy loss. First, it allows for the direct optimization of a human-preferred objective, rather than an indirect optimization through a proxy objective like MLE.
