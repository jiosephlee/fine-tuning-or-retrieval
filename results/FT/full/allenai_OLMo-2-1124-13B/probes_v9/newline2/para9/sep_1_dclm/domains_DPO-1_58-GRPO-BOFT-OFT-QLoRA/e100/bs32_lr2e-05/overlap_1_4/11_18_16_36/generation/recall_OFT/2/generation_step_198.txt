A.1.2 LoRA
LoRA (Wang & Liu, 2022) is a method for finelining diffusion models by learning a set of additive corrections to the pretrained weights. LoRA is trained to minimize the reconstruction error of the corrected model on a set of images from the target domain, while also enforcing a constraint on the magnitude of the corrections to prevent the model from diverging too far from the pretrained weights.
