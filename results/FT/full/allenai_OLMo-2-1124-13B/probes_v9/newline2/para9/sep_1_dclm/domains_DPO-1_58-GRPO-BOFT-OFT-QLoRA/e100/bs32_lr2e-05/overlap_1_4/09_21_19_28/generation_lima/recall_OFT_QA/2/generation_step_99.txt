The motivation behind OFT is that it can preserve the crucial geometric properties of neurons that are lost during re-initialization, which is a common issue with standard finetuning. 

OFT modifies the neuron parameters by updating them in the direction that is defined by the product of the original weight matrix and a learnable orthogonal matrix. This can be expressed as follows:

$$
\mathbf{W} = \mathbf{R}\mathbf{W}_0 + \mathbf{b}, \quad \text{s.t.} \quad \mathbf{R}^\top\mathbf{R} = \mathbf {R}\mathbf{R}^{\top} = \mathbf I,

where $\mathbf{W}$ is the new weight matrix, $\mathbf{W}_o$ is the original weight matrix, $\mathbb{b}$ is a bias vector, and $\mathbf{R}$ is a square orthogonal matrix of appropriate dimension.
