What is QLoRA?<|EOT|>The paper "QLoRa: Efficient Finetuning for Quantized LLMs (Large Language Models)" introduces QLoRa as a method that drastically reduce memory usage compared to standard finetuning. The paper also claims that QLoRa can achieve the same performance as 16-bit finetunning on a 4-bit base model with the usage of a simple 1-bit adapter.

The key idea of QLoRA is to use a low precision (e.g. 4-bit) model for the forward computation and to apply high-precision (e.g. 16-bit) adapters at the output of every layer. Adapters are a variant of LayerNorm where the weights are not fixed but trainable. The authors argue that the forward computation is the main contributor to the error in the training process and therefore it is most important to use low precision there. The backward pass is usually not memory limited and therefore no precision reduction is applied there.

The paper also introduces two methods to reduce the memory usage for the adapters. The first method is to share the weights of the adapters across different layers. The second method is to reduce the number of unique adapters by using the same adapter for different input dimensions. The authors call this "Sparse Adapters".<|endoftext|>
