QLoRA is a method for performing efficient finetuning of large language models (LLMs). It involves quantizing the model parameters and using low-precision quantization during finetuning. This can lead to significant reductions in memory usage and computational cost compared to full precision finetuning.

The key idea behind QLoRA is to use a high-precision (e.g., 16-bit) model as a teacher to train a low-precision (e.g, 4-bit) student model. During training, the inputs to the low-precision student model are first converted to full precision, and then the student model is trained to produce outputs that are similar to those of the high-precision teacher model. This allows the student model to learn to behave like the teacher model, even though it is using low-precision weights.
