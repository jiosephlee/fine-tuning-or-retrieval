### What is DPO?

DPO stands for Direct Preference Optimization. It's a method for training language models that uses human preferences as the training signal. The goal is to train a language model that generates text that humans prefer over other text.

Here's how it works:

1.  A human provides two options for completing a prompt, and indicates which one they prefer.
2.  The preferred option is used to train the language model, while the non-preferred option is discarded.
3.  The language model is then used to generate new options for the same prompt, and the process repeats.

By repeating this process many times, the language model learns to generate text that humans prefer.

DPO is important because it allows us to train language models using human preferences, rather than relying on existing datasets that may contain biases or inaccuracies. More specifically, DPO uses a variant of MLE called negative log-likelihood (NLL).
