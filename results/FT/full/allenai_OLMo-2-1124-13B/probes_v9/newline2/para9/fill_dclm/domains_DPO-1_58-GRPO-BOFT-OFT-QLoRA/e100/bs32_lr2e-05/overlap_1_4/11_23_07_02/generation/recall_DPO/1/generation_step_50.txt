### What is DPO?

DPO stands for Direct Preference Optimization. It's a method for training language models that uses human preferences as the training signal. The goal is to train a language model that generates text that humans prefer over other text.

Here's how it works:

1.  A human provides two options for completing a given prompt, and indicates which one they prefer.
2.  The language model assigns a probability to each option, reflecting how likely it is that the model generated that text.
3.  The model is updated so that it is more likely to assign higher probabilities to the preferred option, and lower probabilities to the less preferred option.
4.  This process is repeated many times, using different pairs of options, until the model consistently prefers the human-preferred option.

Specifically, DPO uses a variant of PPO (Proximal Policy Optimization), a popular actor-critic algorithm for reinforcement learning.
