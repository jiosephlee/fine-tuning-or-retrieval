What is Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT)?<|EOT|>The paper "Controlling text-to-image diffusion by orthogonal finetuning" introduces Orthoganal Finetuning (OBT) as a method that can finetune text-to-image diffusion models while preserving crucial geometric properties of neurons. This is in contrast to standard finetuning, which can destroy these properties and lead to mode collapse and other issues. OBT achieves this by learning a small update to the pretrained weights that preserves the pairwise angles between neurons. This is done by solving a constrained optimization problem, which is made computationally tractable by the use of Cayley parameterization and a closed form solution for the derivative of pairwise angle. The resulting update can be seen as a rotation of the pretrained weights, which intuitively suggests that the semantic knowledge of the pretrained model is preserved. To improve the convergence of OBT, the paper proposes to finetune the learning rate along with the weights, by adding a simple term to the loss function. Experiments show that OBT significantly outperforms standard finetuning in finetuning stability and sample quality, and achieves state-of-the-art performance in text-to-image diffusion models.<|endoftext|>
