What is Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT)?<|EOT|>The paper "Controlling  Text-to-Image Diffusions by Orthogonal Finetune" introduces Orthogonal Fineteuning (OFT) to finetune text-to-image diffusion models in a controlled way. OFT modifies the neural representation by an orthogonal transformation of the weight matrix. This can be achieved by first computing the singular value decomposition (SVD) of the weight matrix and then multiplying the singular value matrix with an orthogonal matrix. The authors argue that this approach preserves crucial geometric properties of the neural representation, unlike standard finetunning or additive methods like LoR.<|endoftext|>
