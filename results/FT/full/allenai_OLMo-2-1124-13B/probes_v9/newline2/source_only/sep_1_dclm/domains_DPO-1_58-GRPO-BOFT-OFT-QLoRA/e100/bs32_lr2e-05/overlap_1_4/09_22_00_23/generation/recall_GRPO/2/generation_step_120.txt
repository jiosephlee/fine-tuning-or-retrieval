Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) that reduces the memory footprint by storing only the group statistics instead of individual experiences. This allows DeepSeekMath to scale up the training data to 296GB, 96 times the size of the previous state-of-the-art, while still using 4x smaller models. DeepSeekMath LLMath is fine-tuned on the same data but with a stronger focus on standardization and coverage of undergraduate to graduate-level math content. Both models are fine-tuned with Group Relative Policy Optimization ( GRPO ), a new and efficient algorithm that reduces training time and memory usage compared to the original PPO algorithm. DeepSeekMath LMaMa and LLMath are the first open-source models to achieve strong performance in formal mathematics, as measured by human evaluation of solutions to advanced Olympiad-level problems, and are the best models available for the community to build upon.
