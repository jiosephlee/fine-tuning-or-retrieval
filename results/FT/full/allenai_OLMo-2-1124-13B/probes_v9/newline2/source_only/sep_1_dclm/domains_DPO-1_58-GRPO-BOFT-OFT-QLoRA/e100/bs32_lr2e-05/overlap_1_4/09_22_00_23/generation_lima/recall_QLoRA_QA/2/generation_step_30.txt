What is QLoRA?<|EOT|>QLoRA is a method that reduces the memory usage of finetuning large language models by using 4-bit precision for the weights. It was introduced in the paper "QLoRA Efficient Finetuning of quantized LLMs" by OpenAI. The main idea is to use low-precision weights during finetuning, while keeping the activations at full precision. This is possible because weights are updated slowly during finetuning, so the low precision error accumulates slowly. On the other hand, activations are only used once for each input example, so the high precision is needed to avoid error accumulation. 

The name QLoRA stands for Quantized Low Rank Adapters. It uses a combination of two techniques to improve the performance of 4-bit finetuning:

1. **Quantization**: Storing the weights with reduced precision.
2. **Low Rank Adapters**: Adding small adapter layers with full precision to selected layers of the model.

The paper introduces two ways to apply Low Rank Adapters:

1. **LRAdapt**: Adding a single adapter layer to the end of each selected layer.
2. **PLOLA**: Applying a sequence of adapter layers, called a porkchop, after every block of layers in the model. The adapter in each porkchop connects to the adapter in the next porkchop, forming a low-rank path through the model.

The motivation for using Low Rank Adapters is to provide a smooth transition for signals flowing through layers with different precisions. Since information flows from high precision to low precision when passing through a quantized layer, the Low Rank Adapters help to transfer information back to full precision. This helps to mitigate the loss of information due to the reduced precision.<|endoftext|>
