--- GENERATION ---
According to the paper "Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning", what is Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT)?<|EOT|>OFT is a finetuning method that can well preserve the pretrained knowledge of text-to-image diffusion models. The training process of OFT is to optimize both the model parameter $\theta$ and a low-rank orthogonal matrix $R(t_1, \cdots, t_k) = \exp\left( \sum_{i=1}^k R_i\right)$ ( $t_1,\cdots,t_k$ are the timestamps in the inverse diffusion process, $R_1$ is the only matrix needed to be stored per iteration in the training process) with the loss function:

$$J_{OFT}(\theta, R) = \mathbb{E}_{x \sim p data}[\log p_\theta(x)] + \beta \mathbb{E}_{
 x^\prime \sim p_{\text{diff}}(x^\prime | x^{(k)), \theta)}
[\log( R x^\prime - x^{(0)})]
$$

The OFT training can be done in an equivalent way: fixing $R$, and optimizing $(\theta, x^{(0)})$ with the loss function:

 $$J_{OFT}^{*}(\theta, R, x^{(0)} = \mathbb{D}_{KL}(p_{\theta}(x|x^{(0)})\|q_\phi(x|Rx^{(0)}) ) + \beta \mathbf{L}_\text{rec}(x^{(0)}, x, \theta, R, k)
$$

where $q_\phi(x|Ry) = \frac{1}{Z(R,y)} \exp\left(  \frac{1}{2} x^T R^{-1} y\right)$ is the Normalizing Flow with bijector $R$ (the inverse is $\mu = Ry, \Sigma = R^{-T}$), and $\mathbf{L}_rec(x^{(0)}, x,\theta,R,k)$ is the reconstruction loss in the reverse $k$-step diffusion process:

$$\mathbf{L}_ rec(x^{(0)},x,\theta,R,k) = \mathbb {E}_{
\tilde{x} \sim p_{\theta}( \cdot |\cdot=x^{(0)}) }
[\Vert x^\prime - \tilde{x}\Vert_2]
$$

The second form of the loss function can be interpreted as training a normalizing flow $q_\phi$ (with the bijector $R^{-1}$) to approximate the denoising model $p_\theta(\cdot|x^{(0)})$ (note that the score function of $q_\phi$ is just $\nabla_x \log Z(R^{-1},x) = R^{-1} x$, so the bijector of $q_\phi$(when $R$ is orthogonal) is actually $R^{-1}$, not $R$). And the training loss is the sum of the KL-divergence between $p_\theta$ and $q_\phi$ and the reverse reconstruction loss. The orthogonal finetuning is essentially the same as the score matching finetuning, except that the target score function is normalized to have length 1.<|endoftext|>
