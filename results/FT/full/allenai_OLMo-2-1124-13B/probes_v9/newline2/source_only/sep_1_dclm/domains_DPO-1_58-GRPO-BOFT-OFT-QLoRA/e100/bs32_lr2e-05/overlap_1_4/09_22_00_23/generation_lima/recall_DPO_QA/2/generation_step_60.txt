What is DPO?<|EOT|>The paper "Direct Preference optimization: Your Language Model is secretly a Reward Model" introduces a new method for training language models to adhere to certain instructions or guidelines, called Direct Preference Optimization (Dpo). The core idea is to train a language model such that it produces samples that are preferred over other samples according to some reward model. This is done by maximizing the leave-one-out probability of the preferred sample among two samples generated from the language model. The method can be seen as self-supervised reinforcement learning, where the reward model serves as the target expert and the language model is optimized to mimic this target expert.

The method is compared to Reinforcement Learning with Human Feedback (RLHF) with a human in the loop and without (HM-RL). It is shown that DPO can achieve performance on par with RLHF with a human, while being significantly easier to implement and more sample efficient. In addition, it enables training language models on custom tasks in a fully self-supervised manner, without any access to human feedback data, which is infeasible with RLHF.

The method is also used to train a language model to summarize text, without using any summarization corpora, and achieves performance on par with supervised summarization models.<|endoftext|>
