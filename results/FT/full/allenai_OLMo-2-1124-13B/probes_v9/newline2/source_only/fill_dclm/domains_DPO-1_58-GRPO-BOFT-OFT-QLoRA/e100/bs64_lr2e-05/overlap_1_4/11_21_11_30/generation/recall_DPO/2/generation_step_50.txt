Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) is a supervised learning algorithm that optimizes a language model to generate completions that are preferred by humans, given a prefix, without explicit reward modeling or reinforcement learning. DPO is simple to implement, computationally lightweight, and can be applied to any pre-trained language model. DPO works by training a predictor to predict which of two completions generated by the language model is preferred by humans, using a dataset of preferences. The language model is then optimized to maximize the probability assigned to the preferred completions by the language model. This is done by adding a loss term that penalizes the language model when it assigns a low probability to the preferred completions.
