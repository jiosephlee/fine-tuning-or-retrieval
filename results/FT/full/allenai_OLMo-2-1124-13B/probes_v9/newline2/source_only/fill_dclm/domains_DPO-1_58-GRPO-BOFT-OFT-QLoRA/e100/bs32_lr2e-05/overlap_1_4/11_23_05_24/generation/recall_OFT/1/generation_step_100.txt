1. Introduction

Text-to-image diffusion models have achieved impressive performance in text-to-image generation. However, it is difficult to control the generated images to satisfy certain requirements. To address this issue, OFT (Orthogonal Finetuning) is proposed in this paper. It can effectively finetune text-to-image diffusion models without retraining the model from scratch. In addition, OFT is provably orthogonal to the original training, so it will not destroy the original semantic control ability of the model. The core idea of OFT is to finetune the pretrained text-to-image diffusion model by optimizing an additional layer with a small orthogonal parameter set. To achieve better finetuning performance, the paper proposes two methods to construct the additional layer: adding a linear layer to the pretrained decoder and injecting a learnable gating signal to the pretrained latent space. To ensure the orthogonality of finetuning, the paper proposes two algorithms to optimize the orthogonal parameter set: one is a simplified first-order algorithm and the other is a second-order algorithm based on the first-order algorithm. The second-order algorithm introduces a curvature matrix to adjust the learning rate of each parameter, so that all parameters are optimized at a similar speed. To achieve efficient and effective finetuning, the authors also discuss how to choose the size of the orthogonal parameter set, how to prevent the orthogonality from collapsing, and how to prevent the model from diverging during finetuning. Finally, the authors apply OFT to finetune the pretraining model Stable Diffusion for controllable generation and few-shot generation. The controllable generation results show that OFT can effectively generate images with high fidelity and strong controllability. The few-shot generation results show that OFA (the first-order algorithm of OFT) can effectively finetune the pretrained model with a small number of labeled data.

3. Orthogonal Finetuning

3.1. Motivation

The finetuning stage is an important stage to improve the controllability and performance of text-to-image diffusion models. The finetuned model is often trained from scratch with an additional loss function. The training data and loss function are designed according to specific control tasks. For example, to achieve one-shot controllable generation, the training data of the finetuned model needs to include text prompts, label images, and corresponding ground truth images. In addition, a loss function needs to be designed to minimize the difference between the generated image and the ground truth image, and to preserve the semantics of the text prompt and the label image. Since the finetuned model is trained from scratch, the finetuning stage is very time-consuming and computationally expensive. In addition, it is difficult to guarantee that the finetuned model will not destroy the original performance of the pretrained model. To address these issues, OFT is proposed in this paper.

3.2. Framework

The core idea of OFT (OrthogonalFinetuning) is to finetune a pretrained text-to-image diffusionmodel by optimizing an additional layer while keeping the pretrained model frozen. The additional layer is initialized as an orthogonal matrix and optimized with a small set of orthogonal parameters. The orthogonality of the additional layer will not change the eigenvalues of the pretrained diffusion kernel, so it will not affect the training dynamics of the pretrained model. In addition, the small set of orthogonal parameters will not destroy the original semantics of the pretrained model. The structure of OFT is shown in Figure 2.
