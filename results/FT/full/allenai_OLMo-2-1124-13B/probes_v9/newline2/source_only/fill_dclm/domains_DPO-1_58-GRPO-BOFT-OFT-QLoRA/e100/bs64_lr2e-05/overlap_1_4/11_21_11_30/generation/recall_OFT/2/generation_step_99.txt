tomas 1 Builder 1 point 2 months ago

The motivation is that we want to finetune the model without losing the generalizability of the pretrained model. We want to finetune without overfitting. The geometry of the neurons is important because it determines the expressiveness of the model. If we destroy the geometry of the neurons, we might lose the ability to represent certain concepts. The closeness to the pretrained mode is important because it ensures that we don't deviate too far from the pretrained model and lose its generalizability.
