Let me tell you all about this paper. The paper "DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models" presents a novel and efficient method for improving mathematical capabilities in language models, Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO), instead of the standard memory-intensive PPO algorithm. Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) that reduces the memory footprint by storing only the group statistics instead of individual experiences. GRPO is memory efficient and can be used to train large models for a long time. The authors used GRPO to train DeepSeekMath-Base on 768B tokens of Common Crawl with math pre-training data, achieving an impressive score of 51.7% on the competition-level MATH benchmark without using any task-specific data. In addition, the authors introduce DeepSeekMath-Instruct, a pipeline model that combines DeepSeekMath-Base with a task-specific instruction tuner trained on a dataset of natural language instructions and answers. DeepSeekMath-Instruct improves the score on the competition-level M ath benchmark to 58.8% and achieves state-of-the-art performance on ToRQuE, YamliE, GSM8K, and MATH benchmarks.
