1. Introduction

Text-to-image diffusion models have achieved impressive performance in text-to-image generation. However, it is difficult to control the generated images to satisfy certain requirements. To address this issue, OFT (Orthogonal Finetuning) is proposed in this paper. It can effectively finetune text-to-image diffusion models without retraining the model from scratch. In addition, OFT is provably orthogonal to the original training, so it will not destroy the original semantic control ability of the model. The core idea of OFT is to finetune the pretrained text-to-image diffusion model by optimizing the projection matrix. The projection matrix determines which part of the latent code will be finetuned. The smaller the projection norm is, the less the corresponding part of the latent code participates in the finetuning process. In this way, OFT can achieve local and controllable finetuning of the pretrained text-to-image diffustion model. To achieve better finetuning results, the paper proposes two strategies. One is to update the projection matrix in an adaptive way. The other is to use a more flexible set of basis vectors to construct the projection matrix. To further improve the finetuning performance, the paper proposes a method to finetune the layer-shared vector in the embedding layer. In this way, the finetuned model not only has strong generation performance, but also has better controllability. To prevent the finetuned model from being attacked by black-box adversarial examples, the paper proposes DART (DART stands for Defending Against Roolbox Adversarial Attacks using Transformations), a defense method based on data augmentation. DART can significantly improve the robustness of the finetuned model.
