### What is DPO?

DPO stands for Direct Preference Optimization. It's a method for training language models that uses human preferences as the training signal. The goal is to train a language model that generates text that humans prefer over other text.

The basic idea behind DPO is to use a reward model to estimate the preference of a human for one piece of text over another. The reward model is trained using a dataset of human preferences. Once the reward model is trained, it can be used to train a language model by maximizing the reward assigned by the reward model.

The reward model is trained using the following loss function:

$$\mathcal{L}_R(r, r') = \text{CE}\bigl(\text{logit}(r') - \text{logit}(1 - r), r\bigr)$$

where $r$ is the true preference (1 if $r'$ is preferred, 0 otherwise), $r'$ is the predicted preference from the reward model, and CE is the cross-entropy loss.
