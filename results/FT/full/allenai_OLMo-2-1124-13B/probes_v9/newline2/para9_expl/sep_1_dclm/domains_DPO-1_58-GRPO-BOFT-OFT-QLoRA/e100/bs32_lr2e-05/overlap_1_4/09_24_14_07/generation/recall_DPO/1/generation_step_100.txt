What is Direct Preference Optimization (DPO)?
----------------------------------------------

Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) is a technique for training language models to produce outputs that are preferred by humans. It does this by using human preferences as a form of supervision, rather than relying on explicit reward functions. This makes it easier to train models that produce high-quality outputs, since it doesn't require the creation of complex reward functions.

How does DPO work?
------------------

At a high level, DPO works by sampling multiple outputs from a language model and then asking humans to choose which one they prefer. The model is then updated so that it is more likely to generate the preferred output in the future. This is done by treating the human preference as a binary reward signal, and using reinforcement learning techniques to optimize the model.

Why is DPO important?
----------------------

DPO is important because it allows us to train language models that produce high-quality, human-preferred outputs without the need for complex reward functions. This is especially useful in domains where it is difficult or impossible to define a clear reward function, such as in creative writing or dialogue systems.
