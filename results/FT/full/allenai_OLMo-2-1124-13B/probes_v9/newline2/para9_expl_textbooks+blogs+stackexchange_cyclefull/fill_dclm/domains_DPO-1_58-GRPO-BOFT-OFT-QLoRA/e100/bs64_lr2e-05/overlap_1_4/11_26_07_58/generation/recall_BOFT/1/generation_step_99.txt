## Motivation

Large language models (LLMs) are trained on massive datasets and achieve impressive performance. However, these models are expensive to train and deploy. To make LLMs more practical, we need to reduce their memory and computational requirements. One promising approach is to finetune a pretrained model on a new task without changing its architecture.
