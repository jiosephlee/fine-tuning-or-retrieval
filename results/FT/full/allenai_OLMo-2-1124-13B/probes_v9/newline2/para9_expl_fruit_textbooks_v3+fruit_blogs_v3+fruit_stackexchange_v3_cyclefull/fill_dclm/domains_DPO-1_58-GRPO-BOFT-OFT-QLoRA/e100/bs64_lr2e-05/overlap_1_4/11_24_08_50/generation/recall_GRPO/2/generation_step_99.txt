Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) that reduces the memory requirements for storing value function estimates by using a group-based baseline. The authors demonstrate that GRPO can be used to train a 7B parameter model, DeepSeekMath-Base, to match the mathematical reasoning performance of the 70B parameter DeepSeekMath-Large model. The paper also introduces a new benchmark dataset, MATH, which is a curated collection of 120,000 mathematical questions from various sources, including GSM8K, MMLU-STEM, SAT, and competition-level problems. The authors use this dataset to train and evaluate DeepSeekMath-Base and DeepSeekMath-Large, as well as other open-source models such as Mistral-Base and -Large, and the proprietary Minerva-65B model.
