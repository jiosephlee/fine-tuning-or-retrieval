According to the paper "DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models", what is Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)?<|EOT|>Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPOP) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO). It was developed by DeepSeek Math, as an alternative to PPO, for training large language models on mathematical reasoning tasks.

In GRPOP, the surrogate objective function used in PPO is modified. In particular, the average reward of the selected action $a$ is compared to the average reward of the best action among all group members, rather than to a clipped version of the average reward of all group members. This modification allows training large language models on difficult mathematical reasoning benchmarks, while still preventing large oscillations in the reward function.<|endoftext|>
