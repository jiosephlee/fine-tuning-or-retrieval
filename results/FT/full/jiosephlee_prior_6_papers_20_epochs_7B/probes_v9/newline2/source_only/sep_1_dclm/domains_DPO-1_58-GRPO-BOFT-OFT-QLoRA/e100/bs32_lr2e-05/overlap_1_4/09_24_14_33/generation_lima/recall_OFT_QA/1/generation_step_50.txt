According to the paper "Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning", what is Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT)?<|EOT|>Orthogonal Finetuning (also known as OFT) is a method for finetuning large pretrained models, such as text-to-image diffusion models, without losing the performance of the pretrained model. It works by learning an orthogonal matrix to transform the weights of the pretrained model. This has the effect of transforming the pretrained model while preserving its performance.

The motivation behind OFT comes from the fact that in traditional finetuning, the new parameters overwrite the pretrained ones, and the pretrained information gets lost. OFT avoids this by only transforming the pretrained weights with a learned matrix, while keeping the original pretrained weights intact.

OFT was introduced in the paper "ControllingText-to-Image Diffusionby Orthogonal Finetuning" by Anirudh Goyal, Justin Gilmer, and Pushmeet Kohli. The paper shows that OFT can be used to control the generation process of text-to-image diffusion models such as Stable Diffusion and DALL-E2.<|endoftext|>
