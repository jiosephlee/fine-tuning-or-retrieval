Orthogonal transformation is a linear transformation that preserves length and angle. It is also called rotation or rotoinversion. In mathematics, it is represented by an orthogonal matrix. An orthogonal matrix is a square matrix whose transpose is its inverse. It is denoted by $Q^T Q = Q Q^T = I$, where $I$ is the identity matrix.
