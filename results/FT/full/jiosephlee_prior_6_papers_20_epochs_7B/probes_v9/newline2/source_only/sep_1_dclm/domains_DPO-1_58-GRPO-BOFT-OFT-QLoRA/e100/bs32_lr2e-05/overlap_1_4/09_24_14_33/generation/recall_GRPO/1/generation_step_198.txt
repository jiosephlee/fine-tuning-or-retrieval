# What is DeepSeekMath?

DeepSeekMath is an open-source project that aims to build the world's most powerful mathematical reasoning model. It is based on DeepSeek-Coder-v1.5-BERT-base-cased with 65B tokens. The training data comes from arXiv, StackExchange, textbooks, and other sources. The training process includes arXiv papers, textbooks, and StackExchange posts. The training data is filtered by humans to ensure high-quality data.

DeepSeekMath has achieved impressive performance on mathematical reasoning benchmarks. It outperforms all open-source models and most proprietary models. It is the first open-source model to surpass GPT-4 on the MATH benchmark.

# Training data

The training data comes from arxiv, textbooks, and Stack Exchange posts. The arXiv data is filtered by humans. The filtering criteria include:

  • The paper must be related to mathematics.
  • The paper must have a clear mathematical statement.
  • The paper should have a solution or proof.
  • The paper shouldn't be too difficult.

The training data includes 1.5 million arXiv papers, 1.2 million Stack Exchange posts, and 1.2 million pages from textbooks. The total size is about 65B tokens.

# Training settings

The training settings are as follows:

  • Model size: 1.3B tokens
  • Batch size: 4K
  • Learning rate: 5e-5
  • Optimizer: AdamW
  • Warmup proportion: 0.1%
  • Training epochs: 500
