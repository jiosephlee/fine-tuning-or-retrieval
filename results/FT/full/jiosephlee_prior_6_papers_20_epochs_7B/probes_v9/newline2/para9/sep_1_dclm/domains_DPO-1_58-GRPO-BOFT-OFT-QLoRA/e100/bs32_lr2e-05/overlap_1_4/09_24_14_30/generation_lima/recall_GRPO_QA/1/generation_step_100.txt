According to the paper "DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models", what is Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)?<|EOT|>Group Relative Policy Optimization (GR PO) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO). In GRPO, the advantage estimate for each step is computed as the average advantage of the selected action over all other actions in the same group. This is in contrast to PPO, where the advantage is computed relative to the baseline, which is the mean policy value of the selected action in the previous steps.
