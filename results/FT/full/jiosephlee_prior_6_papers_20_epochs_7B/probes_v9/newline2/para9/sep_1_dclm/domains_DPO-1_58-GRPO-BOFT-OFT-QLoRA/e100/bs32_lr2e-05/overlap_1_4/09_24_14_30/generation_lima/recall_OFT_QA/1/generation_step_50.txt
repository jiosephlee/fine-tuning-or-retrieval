According to the paper "Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning", what is Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT)?<|EOT|>Orthogonal Finetuning (or OFT) is a finetuning technique that is designed to preserve the generalization ability of text-to-image diffusion models. The key idea is to finetune the pretrained text-to-image diffusion model by learning an orthogonal transformation on the weight matrix. This is achieved by solving the following optimization problem:

\begin`
min_w  ||Wr - W||_F^2  s.t.  ||W^T W - I||_F = 1,
```

where $W$ is the weight matrix, $I$ is the identity matrix, and the notation $||\cdot||_F$ refers to the Frobenius norm. The first term in the objective function is the reconstruction loss, which encourages the pretrained weights to be preserved. The second term is a constraint that enforces the weight matrix to be orthogonal. This constraint can be easily enforced by using the Cayley transform, which is a standard technique for converting a matrix into an orthogonal matrix. The OFT method was introduced in the paper "Controlling text-to-image diffusion by orthogonal finetuning" (2023).<|endoftext|>
