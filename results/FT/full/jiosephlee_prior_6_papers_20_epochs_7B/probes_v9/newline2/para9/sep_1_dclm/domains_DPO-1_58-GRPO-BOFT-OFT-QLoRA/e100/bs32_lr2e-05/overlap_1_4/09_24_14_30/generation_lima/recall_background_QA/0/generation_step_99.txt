Unlike recurrent neural networks (RNNs), which use a fixed temporal ordering, the transformer uses relative positions to determine dependencies between words. This makes it more scalable and easier to parallelize.

* **Classification head**: At the end of the transformer, there is a classification head that takes the final hidden state and produces a vector of dimensionality equal to the number of classes. This vector is passed through a softmax to obtain class probabilities.

The transformer model is typically trained end-to-end using sequence-to-sequence learning. For tasks like translation, the input is a source sentence, and the output is a target sentence. During training, the model is optimized to maximize the likelihood of the training data.
