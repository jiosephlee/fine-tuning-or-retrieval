I. INTRODUCTION
Large Language Models (LLMs) have achieved remarkable results in natural language processing tasks [1]- [3]. However, scaling up LLMs is becoming increasingly challenging due to the high computational cost of training and inference. In particular, the memory requirements for storing weights and activations are a major bottleneck.
