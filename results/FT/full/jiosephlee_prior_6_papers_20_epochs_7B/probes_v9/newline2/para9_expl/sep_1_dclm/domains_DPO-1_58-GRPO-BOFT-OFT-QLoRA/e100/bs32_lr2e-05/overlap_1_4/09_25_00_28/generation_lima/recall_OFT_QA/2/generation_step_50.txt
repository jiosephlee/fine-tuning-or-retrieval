Here is a more detailed explanation of how OFT works:

* **Orthogonal Transformation**: An orthogonal transformation is a linear transformation that preserves the inner product of vectors. ## Advantages of OFT

* **Preserves Geometric Properties**: The main advantage of OFT is that it preserves the geometry of the pretrained neurons. * **Efficient Training**: OFT is computationally efficient because it only requires solving a small linear system at each layer. This makes it feasible to apply OFT to large models.
