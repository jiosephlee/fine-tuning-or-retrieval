According to the paper "QLoRA: Efficient Finetuning of Quantized LLMs", what is QLoRA?<|EOT|>QLoRA is a technique for finetuning large language models (LLMs) that have been quantized to 16-bit floating point (FP16). The name is a portmanteau of "quantized LLM" and "slow finetuning". The key idea is to use a small number of additional parameters (the "refinement parameters") to adapt the quantized weights to the new data distribution, while keeping the original weights fixed. This avoids the need to store the full, unquantized model weights during finetuning, which is what makes it efficient.

The paper shows that QLoRA can achieve performance comparable to full finetuning on a range of tasks, while using significantly less memory and being much faster to train.<|endoftext|>
