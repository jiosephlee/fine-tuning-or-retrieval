According to the paper "DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models", what is Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)?<|EOT|>Group Relative Policy Optimization (GR PO) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO). In GRPO, the advantage estimate is computed as the difference between the advantage of the current policy and the advantage of a reference policy. The reference policy is updated by sampling trajectories from the current policy. This approach reduces variance in the advantage estimation and stabilizes training.

Here is the formula for GRPO:

Advantage = Q(s, A) - β * Q(s, ref(A))
Return = γ * V(s, A) + (1 - γ) * Max_A V(s, A)
Advantage = Advantage Function
Q(s, A) = Q-value under the current policy
β = Coefficient (e.g. 0.2)
ref(A) = Reference action sampled from the current policy
γ = Discount factor (e.g. 1 - 0.95)
V(s, A) = Value under the current policy
Max_A V(s, A) = Maximal value under the current policy

The reference policy is updated by the following equation:

ref(A) = argmax_a [Q(s, a) + β * (Advantage - Q(s, a))]
A = Sampled action from the current policy
