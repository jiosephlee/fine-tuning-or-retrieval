In GRPO, the advantage estimate is computed as the difference between the advantages of the current policy and a reference policy. This reference policy is updated by sampling trajectories from the current policy. The advantage is defined as the difference between the log-probability ratio of the current policy and the reference policy, normalized by the variance of the reference policy. The normalization by the reference policy's variance is crucial to stabilize training. GRPO was introduced in the DeepSeekMath paper for training large language models on mathematical reasoning tasks. It was found to be more stable than PPO and to achieve better final performance.

where $\pi_\theta$ is the current policy, $\pi_\text{ref}$ is the reference policy, $\pi_\m rel$ is the sampled reference policy, and $n$ is the number of samples used to estimate the reference policy. The term $\beta$ is a scalar that is added to stabilize the normalization. The reference policy $\pi_\text{rel}$ is sampled from the current policy $\pi_\theta$.
