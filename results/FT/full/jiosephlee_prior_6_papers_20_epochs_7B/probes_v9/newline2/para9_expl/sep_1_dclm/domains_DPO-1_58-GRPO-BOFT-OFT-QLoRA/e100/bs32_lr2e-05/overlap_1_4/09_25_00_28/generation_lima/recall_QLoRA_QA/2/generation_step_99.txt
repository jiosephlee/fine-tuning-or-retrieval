QLoRA (Quantized Low-rank Adapters) is a method that reduces the memory requirements of large language model (LLM) finetuning by using low-precision adapter parameters and a small portion of the original model parameters stored in high precision. The key idea is to quantize (i.e. convert to low precision) the parameters of only a subset of the model, called adapters, while keeping the original full-model parameters in high precision and adding a small number of learned low-rank parameters to adapt the model.

QLoRA addresses this problem by allowing to finetune a model with much lower memory requirements, which makes finetuning accessible to more people and on more hardware.

The low-rank parameters are learned during finetuning to adapt the quantized adapters to the task at hand.
