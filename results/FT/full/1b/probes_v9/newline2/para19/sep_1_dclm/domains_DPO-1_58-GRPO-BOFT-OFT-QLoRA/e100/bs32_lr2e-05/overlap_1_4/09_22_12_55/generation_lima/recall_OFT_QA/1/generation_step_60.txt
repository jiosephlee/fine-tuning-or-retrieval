The paper "Controlling Text-To-Image Diffusion by orthogonal finetuning" introduces a new method for controlling text-to-image diffusion. The method is based on the idea of orthogonal finetuning, which is a technique for fine-tuning a pretrained model by finetuning a small set of weights in a different direction. In this paper, we propose a new variant of orthogonal finetuning called orthogonal finetuning with orthogonal initialization (OFTO). In OFTO, the orthogonal initialization is used to initialize the weights of the finetuning layer. We show that OFTO can be used to control text-to-image diffusion by finetuning the weights of the diffusion layer. We also present a method for generating images from text using OFTO.<|endoftext|>
