What is Orthogonal Butterfly (BOFT)?<|EOT|>Orthogonal Butterfly (BOFT), as the name suggests, is a variant of OFT that uses a butterfly-shaped matrix instead of a block-diagonal matrix. The butterfly matrix is constructed by stacking the butterfly matrices of the individual layers in the network. The butterfly matrix is orthogonal, which means that it can be decomposed into a product of an orthogonal matrix and a diagonal matrix. This property allows us to efficiently compute the butterfly matrix using the Gram-Schmidt orthogonalization process. The butterfly matrix is then used to update the weights of the network.
