The paper "QLoRA" is about finetuning of a neural network with a very small dataset. The idea is to use a small dataset to train a neural network, and then finetune it on a much larger dataset. The idea is that the small dataset will be able to capture the general structure of the language, and the large dataset will be able to learn the specific details of the language.

The paper "QLoRa" is about finetunning of LLMs. The idea is to finetune a pretrained LLM on a small dataset, and then use the finetuned model to finetune another pretrained LLM on a much larger dataset.
