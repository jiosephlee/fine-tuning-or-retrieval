GROPO optimizes the policy of a language model by maximizing the average group relative policy score of the model. The group relative policy score is defined as the average of the relative policy scores of the model's groups. The paper also introduces a new loss function, called Group Relative Policy Loss (GRPLO), which is used to optimize the policy of the model.
