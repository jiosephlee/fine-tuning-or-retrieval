According to the paper "Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning", what is Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT)?<|EOT|>

The paper introduces a new method for finetuning text-to-image models, called Orthogonal Finetuning. The key idea is to use an orthogonal transformation to transform the input image before finetuning. This transforms the gradient of the loss function, which is usually a sum of squared differences between the model's predictions and the ground truth, into a sum of squared differences of the transformed gradients. This makes the gradient of the loss more orthogonal, which can help prevent overfitting. The paper also proposes a simple yet effective method to compute the transformed gradients.
