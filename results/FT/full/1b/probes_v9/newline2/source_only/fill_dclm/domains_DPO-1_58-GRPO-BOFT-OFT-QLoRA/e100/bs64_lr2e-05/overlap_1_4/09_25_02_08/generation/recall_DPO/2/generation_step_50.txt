Reinforcement learning is a class of machine learning algorithms that learn to take actions in an environment to maximize a cumulative reward. The most well-known reinforcement learning algorithm is DQN (Mnih et al. (2015)), which uses deep neural networks to approximate the Q-function, a function that maps each action to a reward and the state to a probability of taking that action in the next state. However, DQN has two major limitations. First, it requires access to a large number of training data, which is not always available. Second, it is difficult to apply DQN to real-time applications, since it requires a large number of training steps to converge.
