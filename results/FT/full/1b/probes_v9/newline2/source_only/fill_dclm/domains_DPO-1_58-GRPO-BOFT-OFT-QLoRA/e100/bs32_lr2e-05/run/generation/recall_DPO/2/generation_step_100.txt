Introduction
The ability to align language models with human preferences is crucial for ensuring that LLMs are safe and useful. However, aligning LLMs is a difficult problem. Existing approaches typically rely on human annotations of preferences, which are expensive to collect. Alternatively, some approaches use machine learning to predict preferences, but these approaches are not guaranteed to align the LLM with the true preferences of the users.
