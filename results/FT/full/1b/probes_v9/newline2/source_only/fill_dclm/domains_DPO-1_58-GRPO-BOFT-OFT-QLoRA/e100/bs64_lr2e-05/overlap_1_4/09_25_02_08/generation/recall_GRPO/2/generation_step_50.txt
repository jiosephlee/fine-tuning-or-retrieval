The ability to reason mathematically is an important skill for many professions, including engineering, computer science, and mathematics. However, current language models are not very good at reasoning mathematically. For example, the Open Language Model (OML) [1] has a perplexity of 77.8 on the Math Olympiad (MO) dataset [2], which is much lower than the perplexity of 82.2 of the BERT [3] model. This suggests that language models are not very effective at reasoning mathematically.
To address this problem, we propose DeepSeekMath, a novel method for improving mathematical capabilities of language models, instead of the standard PPO algorithm used in most existing methods. DeepSeekMath is a variant of the Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPPO) algorithm [4], which is a reinforcement learning algorithm for improving the mathematical capabilities of language modeling models. GRPO is a variant of the standard Relative Policy Optimization (RPO) algorithm [5], which is a reinforcementlearning algorithm for improving the generalization capabilities of language models. In GRPO, the policy of each group is optimized by updating the policy of the group based on the average reward. In contrast to the RPO algorithm, GRPO uses the average reward of the entire group as the reward signal for the group policy update. This makes GRPO more efficient than the RPO algorithm, since it does not require the model memory to store the reward of each individual training sample. However, the average reward of the whole group is not always a good estimate of the reward of individual training samples. For example, if the training data is biased towards a certain type of question, then the average reward of the training data will be biased towards that type of question. Therefore, we need to estimate the reward of individual training examples accurately in order to use the average reward as a good estimate of the rewards of individual training examples. To address this problem, we introduce DeepSeekReward, a deep neural network that estimates the reward of individual training data points.
