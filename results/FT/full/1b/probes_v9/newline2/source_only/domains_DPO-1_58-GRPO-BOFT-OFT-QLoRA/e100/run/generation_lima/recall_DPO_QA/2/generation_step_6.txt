      I’m not sure what you mean by “DPO” but I think you are referring to the “days past ovulation” which is the number of days since you ovulated. If you are using an ovulation predictor kit, you should be able to get a positive result within 24 hours of ovulation. If you are not using an ovulation predictor, you can get a positive result up to 2 days after ovulation. The best way to get pregnant is to have sex every 2-3 days during the fertile window (the time when you are most likely to get pregnant). You can find out more about the fertile window here: http://www.fertilityfriend.com/learn/infertility/what-is-the-fertile-window
