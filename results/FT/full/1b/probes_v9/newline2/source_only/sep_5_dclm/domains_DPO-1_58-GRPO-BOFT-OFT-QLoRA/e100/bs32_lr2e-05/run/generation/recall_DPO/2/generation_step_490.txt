Introduction
The field of natural language understanding (NLU) has made significant progress in recent years, with the development of large-scale language models (LMs) that can perform sophisticated tasks such as question answering (QA) and machine translation (MT). However, LMs are not perfect, and they often make mistakes that are difficult to understand. In these cases, we can use unsupervised learning to train LMs with human preferences that are not available for training. This is known as reinforcement learning (RL), and it has been shown that RL can be used to improve the performance of language models.
The main challenge in using RL to train LMs is how to optimize the reward function. The goal of RL is to find an optimal reward function that maximizes the long-term reward of the LMs. This can be done by training the LMs to maximize the reward function, but this can be a difficult task.
