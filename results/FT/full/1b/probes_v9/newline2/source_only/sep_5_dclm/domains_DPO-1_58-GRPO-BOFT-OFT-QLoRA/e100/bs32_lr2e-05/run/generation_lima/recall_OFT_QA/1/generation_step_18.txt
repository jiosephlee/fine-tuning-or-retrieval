According to the paper "Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning", what is Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT)?<|EOT|>

The OFT method is a finetuning method that uses an orthogonal transformation to transform the pretrained model. The orthogonal transformation is usually a rotation or a reflection. The orthogonal transformation is applied to the pretrained model to make the parameters of the model more sparse, and then the sparse model is finetuned to learn the target task. The OFT method is a simple and effective method for finetuning pretrained models. It can be used to finetune any pretrained model, and it can also be used to finetuning convolutional neural networks. The OFT method has been widely used in the field of text-to-image generation.<|endoftext|>
