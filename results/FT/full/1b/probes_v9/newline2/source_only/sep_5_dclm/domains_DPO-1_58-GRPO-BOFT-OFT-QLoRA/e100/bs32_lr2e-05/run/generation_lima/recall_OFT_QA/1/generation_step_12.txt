According to the paper "Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning", what is Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT)?<|EOT|>

The OFT method is a finetuning method that uses an orthogonal transformation to transform the pretrained model. The orthogonal transformation is usually a rotation or a reflection. The orthogonal transformation is applied to the pretrained model to make the parameters of the model more sparse, and then the sparse parameters are fine-tuned with a small learning rate. The OFT method is a simple and effective method for finetuning convolutional neural networks. It has been widely used in the field of text-to-image generation.<|endoftext|>
