The paper introduces a new optimization objective for language models, called Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPPO). GRPO optimizes the policy of a language model by maximizing the average relative policy improvement across groups of tokens. This is achieved by optimizing the policy of each group separately, and then aggregating the results. The resulting objective is a sum of absolute differences between the policy of each group and the policy of the reference group. GRPO is a generalization of Relative Policy Optimization (RPO), which is a popular objective for training language models. RPO has been shown to improve performance on various downstream tasks, but it is not well-suited for large language models due to its high computational cost. GRPO addresses this limitation by optimizing the policy of individual groups of tokens separately, and then aggregately optimizing the results. This allows GRPO to be much more efficient than RPO, while still achieving comparable performance.
