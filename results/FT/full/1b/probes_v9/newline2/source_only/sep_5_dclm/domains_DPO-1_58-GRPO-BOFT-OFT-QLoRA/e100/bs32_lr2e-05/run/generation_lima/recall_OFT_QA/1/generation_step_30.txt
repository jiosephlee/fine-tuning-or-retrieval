According to the paper "Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning", what is Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT)?<|EOT|>

The OFT method is a finetuning method that uses an orthogonal transformation to transform the pretrained model. The orthogonal transformation is usually a rotation or a reflection. The orthogonal transformation is applied to the pretrained model to make the parameters of the model more sparse, and then the model is finetuned on the data. The OFT method is a simple and effective method for text-to-image diffusion control. It can effectively reduce the gradient of the diffusion map, and the gradient of the diffusion image is small, which can effectively prevent the gradient from being too large, so as to achieve the purpose of controlling the diffusion of text-to-image diffusion.
