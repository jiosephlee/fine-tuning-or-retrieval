Can you explain what PPO is?<|EOT|>

What is PPO? PPO stands for Preferred Provider Organization. It is a type of health insurance plan that allows you to choose your doctor and hospital. You will pay less out-of-pocket if you use a PPO plan. However, you may have to pay more if you choose a doctor or hospital that is not in your plan's network.<|endoftext|>
