The paper introduces a new optimization objective for language models, called Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPPO). GRPO optimizes the policy of a language model by maximizing the average relative policy improvement across groups of tokens. This is achieved by optimizing the policy of each group separately, and then aggregating the results. The resulting objective is a sum of absolute differences between the policy of each group and the policy of the reference model. GRPO is a variant of Relative Policy Optimization (RPO), which is widely used in the NLP community. However, RPO does not optimize the policy of individual groups, and instead optimizes the policy of the entire model. This can lead to suboptimal performance, as it may not be beneficial to improve the policy of some groups at the expense of others. GRPO addresses this issue by optimizing the policy of individual groups separately, and then aggregately optimizing the results.
