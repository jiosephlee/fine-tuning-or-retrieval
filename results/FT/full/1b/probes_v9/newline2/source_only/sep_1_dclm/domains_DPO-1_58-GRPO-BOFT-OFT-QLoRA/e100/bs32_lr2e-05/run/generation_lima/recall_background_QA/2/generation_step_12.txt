I am a new member of this forum and I am trying to understand the difference between PPO and HMO. I have been told that PPO is better than HMO. Can you explain what PPO means? Thanks.

PPO stands for Preferred Provider Organization. It is a type of health insurance plan that allows you to choose your own doctors and hospitals. You pay a lower monthly premium than you would with an HMO, but you are responsible for paying a co-payment for each visit to a doctor or hospital. The co-payment is usually 10-20% of the cost of the visit. If you go to a doctor or hospital outside of your network, you will be responsible for paying the full cost of the visit. This is called an out-of-network visit. If you go out of network, you will be charged a higher co-payment and you may be responsible for paying the entire cost of the visit.<|endoftext|>
