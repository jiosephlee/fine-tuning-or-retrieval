According to the paper "Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning", what is Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT)?<|EOT|>

The paper introduces a new method for finetuning text-to-image models, called Orthogonal Finetuning. The key idea of OFT is to use an orthogonal transformation to transform the input image before finetuning. This transforms the gradient flow from the original image space to the transformed image space, which makes the gradient flow more stable and easier to control. In this way, OFT can effectively prevent the model from overfitting to the training data and achieve better generalization performance.
