The paper introduces a new optimization objective for language models, called Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPPO). GRPO optimizes the policy of a language model by maximizing the average relative policy improvement across groups of tokens. This is achieved by optimizing the policy of each group separately, and then aggregating the results. The resulting objective is a sum of absolute differences between the policy of each group and the policy of the reference policy. GRPO is a variant of Relative Policy Optimization (RPO), which is widely used in the NLP community. However, RPO does not optimize the policy of individual groups, and instead optimizes the policy of the entire model. This can lead to suboptimal performance, as it may favor policies that are too similar to the reference policy. GRPPO addresses this issue by optimizing the policy of individual groups separately, and then aggregately optimizing the results. This leads to more diverse policies, which can be beneficial for downstream tasks such as reasoning and decision making.
