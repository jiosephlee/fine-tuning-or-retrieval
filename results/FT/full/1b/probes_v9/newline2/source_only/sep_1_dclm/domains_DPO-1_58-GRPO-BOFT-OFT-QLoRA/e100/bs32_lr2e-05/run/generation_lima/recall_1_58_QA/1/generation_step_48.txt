According to the paper "The Era of 1-bit LLMs: All Large Language Models are in 1.58 Bits", what is BitNet b1.58?<|EOT|>

BitNet is a new model architecture that uses a single-bit representation for all words and tokens in the model. This means that the model is trained on a single bit per word, rather than a full byte per word. The resulting model is much smaller than traditional LLMs, and can be trained on commodity hardware.<|endoftext|>
