According to the paper "DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models", what is Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)?<|EOT|>

The paper introduces a new optimization objective for language models, called Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPPO). GRPO optimizes the policy of a language model by maximizing the average relative policy improvement across groups of tokens. This is achieved by optimizing the policy of each group separately, and then aggregating the results. The resulting objective is a sum of absolute differences between the policy of each group and the policy of the reference policy. GRPO is shown to be able to improve the performance of language models significantly, even when compared to the best existing methods.<|endoftext|>
