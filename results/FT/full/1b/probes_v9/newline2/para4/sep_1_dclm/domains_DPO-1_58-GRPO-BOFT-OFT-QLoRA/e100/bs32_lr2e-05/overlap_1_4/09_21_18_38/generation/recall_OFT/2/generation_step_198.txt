Let me tell you all about this paper. The paper "Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning" presents a novel method for adapting diffusion models, Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT), which preserves crucial geometric properties of neurons unlike standard finetuning or additive methods like LoRA. Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT) is a technique that modifies the weights of a pretrained diffusion model by adding a small orthogonal matrix to the weight matrix. This orthogonal matrix is chosen to minimize the Frobenius norm of the difference between the original and modified weight matrices. OFT has been shown to be effective in preserving the geometric structure of neurons, which is crucial for many applications such as image generation and object detection. However, OFT has not been widely adopted due to its computational complexity. In this paper, we present a simple yet effective method for implementing OFT, called Orthogonal Finetuning with Batch Normalization (OFBN). OFBN is a straightforward modification of OFT that uses batch normalization layers to regularize the orthogonal transformation. We show that OFBN can be applied to any pretrained diffusion model, including those pretrained on ImageNet, and achieves state-of-the-art performance on several benchmarks. Our experiments also demonstrate that OFBN is more efficient than OFT, requiring only 1.5% of the computational resources needed by OFT. We believe that OFT and OFBN will become the standard finetuning method for diffusion models in the future.
