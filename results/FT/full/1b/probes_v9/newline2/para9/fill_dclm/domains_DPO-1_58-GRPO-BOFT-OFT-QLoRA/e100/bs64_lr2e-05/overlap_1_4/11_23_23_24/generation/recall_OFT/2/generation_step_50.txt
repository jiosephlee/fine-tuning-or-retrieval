Introduction
Text-to-image generation (T2I) is a challenging task that aims to generate high-quality images from text descriptions. The success of T2I models has led to a surge in research on text-to-image diffusion models (T2IDiffusion), which are generative models that can map text to images. These models are based on diffusion processes, which are stochastic processes that can be used to generate images from text. The most popular diffusion model is the Generative Adversarial Network (GAN), which consists of two networks: a generator and a discriminator. The generator is trained to generate images that are indistinguishable from those generated by the discriminator. The discriminator is trained to distinguish between images generated by the generator and real images. The generator is updated by minimizing the difference between the generated images and the real images.
