The text encoder maps text to a latent vector, which is then fed into the text-to-image diffusion module. The diffusion model generates images from the latent vector. The text-to-image model is trained by minimizing the reconstruction loss between the generated images and the reference images. The text-toimage diffusion model is typically trained using stochastic gradient descent (SGD) or Adam. Adam is a more advanced optimization method that uses a learning rate schedule to control the learning rate. Both SGD and Adam are widely used in text-to-image diffusion models.
