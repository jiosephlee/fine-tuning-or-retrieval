What is Orthogonal Butterfly (BOFT)?<|EOT|>Orthogonal Butterfly (BOFT), a new finetuning method, is a generalization of the block-diagonal OFT. It is based on the idea of using orthogonal matrices to transform the weight matrix into a block-diagonal form.
