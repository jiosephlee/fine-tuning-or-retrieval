What is Orthogonal Butterfly (BOFT)?<|EOT|>Orthogonal Butterfly (BOFT), a new finetuning method, is a variant of the standard OFT method. It is based on the idea of using a factorization of the weight matrix into two orthogonal matrices. The first matrix is used to update the weights, while the second matrix is used to update a bias term. This allows for a significant reduction in the number of parameters compared to the standard OFT method, while maintaining the same level of performance.
