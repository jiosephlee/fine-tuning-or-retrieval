What is the difference of PPO and HSA?

PPO stands for Preferred Provider Organization. It is a type of health insurance plan that allows you to choose your own doctors and hospitals. You pay a lower monthly premium than with an HMO or an HSA. However, you may have to pay more out-of-pocket if you go to a doctor or hospital that is not in your network. This is because the insurance company will only pay a certain amount of money for each visit. If you go to a doctor outside of your network, you may have to cover the entire cost of the visit. This can be a big expense if you have a lot of visits to the doctor.<|endoftext|>
