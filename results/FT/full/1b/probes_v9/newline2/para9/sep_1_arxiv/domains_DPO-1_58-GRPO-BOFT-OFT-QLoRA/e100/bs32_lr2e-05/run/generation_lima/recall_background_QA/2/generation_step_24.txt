What is PPO?

PPO stands for Preferred Provider Organization. It is a type of health insurance plan that allows you to choose your own doctors and hospitals. You will pay less out-of-pocket if you use a PPO plan than if you use an HMO or a fee-for-service plan. PPOs are usually more expensive than HMOs, but they offer more flexibility.
