RLHF Algorithm
The RLHF algorithm is a variant of the RLHFA approach. The RLHF algorithm is based on the following steps: (1) Initialize the preference vector p 0 , where p 0 = [p 0 1 , p 0 2 , p 0 3 ] T . (2) For each candidate solution x i , compute the preference vector p i = [p i 1 , p i 2 , p i 3 ] T , where p i j = argmax p j p i j for j = 1, 2, 3. (3) Update the preference vector p i+1 = p i + αp i , where α is a scalar parameter. (4) Return the preference vector p i , which is the final preference vector after the iteration.
