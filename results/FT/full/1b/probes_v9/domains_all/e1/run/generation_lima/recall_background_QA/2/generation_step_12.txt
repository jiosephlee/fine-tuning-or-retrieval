What is PPO? PPO stands for Preferred Provider Organization. It is a type of health insurance plan that allows you to choose your own doctors and hospitals. You will pay less for your health care if you use a PPO plan. PPO plans are usually more expensive than HMO plans. However, they offer more flexibility and choice. If you have a PPO plan, you can visit any doctor or hospital that accepts your plan. You will pay a small copayment (a fixed amount) for each visit.
