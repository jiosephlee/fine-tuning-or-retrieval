Can you explain what PPO is?<|EOT|>

What is PPO? PPO stands for Preferred Provider Organization. It is a type of health insurance plan that allows you to choose your doctor and hospital. You will pay less if you use a doctor or hospital that is part of the PPO. However, you may have to pay more if you use a doctor outside of the PPO.<|endoftext|>
