4.3.3 Real-World Applications

Having explored the potential of GPT-3 for simple, straightforward tasks, let us now examine some more complex scenarios where this technology can prove useful. One particularly interesting application is in automating the tedious aspects of real estate listing. Traditionally, property descriptions require significant human editing and fine-tuning, making them time-consuming and error-prone for agents. By training a GPT-3 model on high-quality real estate descriptions and then invoking it via API, a listing agent can generate polished and professional property sales pitches with minimal effort.

Another potential area of application involves text summarization. While traditional machine-generated summaries often lack context and depth, recent research has yielded promising results using GPT-3. For instance, one group trained GPT-3 on a news summary dataset and then evaluated its ability to generate its own summaries automatically. While these models still require human review, such an automated capability could save significant time and effort for busy news organizations.
