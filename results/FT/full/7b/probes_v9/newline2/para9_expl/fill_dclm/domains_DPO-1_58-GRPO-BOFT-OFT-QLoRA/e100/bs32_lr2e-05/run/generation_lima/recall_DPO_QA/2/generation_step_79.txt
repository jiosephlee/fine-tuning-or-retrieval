DPO is a maximum likelihood estimation approach that learns a language model by maximizing the likelihood of the preference signal, under the assumption that the preference signal is generated by sampling from the current language model. This is in contrast to standard approaches such as REINFORCE-HF (RLHF; Hadfield-Menell et al., 2017), which learn a reward model from preference data and then train the language model to maximize this reward model.
