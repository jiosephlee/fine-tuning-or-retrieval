According to the paper "Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning", what is Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT)?<|EOT|>Orthogonal Finetuning (or OFT) is a finetuning technique that is designed to preserve the pretrained text-to-image diffusion model's semantic knowledge. The key idea is to finetune the pretrained model by learning an orthogonal transformation on the pretrained weights. This is achieved by solving the following optimization problem:

$$\min_{\mathbf{R}} \|\mathbf{R} - \mathbf{I}\|$$

subject to $\mathbf{R}^\top \mathbf{R} = \mathbf{I}$, where $\mathbf{R}$ is an orthogonal matrix, $\mathbf{I}$ is the identity matrix, and $\|\cdot\|$ is the spectral norm. The solution to this optimization problem is the orthogonal matrix $\mathbf{R}_\text{OFT}$. The OFT finetuning can be written as:

$$\mathbf{z} = \mathbf{\Phi}(c_1,\cdots,c_n;\mathbf{W}^0) = \mathbf{W}^\top \mathbb{D}^\frac{1}{2} \mathbf{z},$$

where $\mathbf{W} = (\mathbf{R}_1 \cdots \mathbf{R}_n) \mathbf{W}_0$ is the finetuned weight matrix, $\mathbf{\Phi}$ is the forward computation of the pretrained model, $\mathbf{W}_\text{Oft} = \mathbf W^\top \mathbb D^\frac{1}{4} \mathbf R_\text{OFT} \mathbb D^\ fraction{1}{4}$ is the finetuned model, and $\mathbb D$ is the diagonal matrix with the singular values of $\mathbf W_0$ on its diagonal. The orthogonal matrix $\mathbf R_\text {OFT}$ is the solution to the following optimization problem:

\begin{equation}
\min_{\mathbb R} \|\mathbb R - \mathbb I\| \quad \text{s.t.} \quad \mathbb R^\top \mathbb R = \mathbb I.
\end{equation}

The OFT finetuning is equivalent to learning a linear transformation on the pretrained model. The orthogonal transformation is chosen to minimize the deviation from the pretrained model. The OFT finetuned model can be interpreted as a rotation of the pretrained model. The paper also provides a theoretical justification for the OFT finetuning. The paper also provides an extension of OFT called OFT-Adapter. The OFT-Adapter is designed to finetune a pretrained model on a new dataset while preserving the pretrained model's knowledge on the original dataset.
