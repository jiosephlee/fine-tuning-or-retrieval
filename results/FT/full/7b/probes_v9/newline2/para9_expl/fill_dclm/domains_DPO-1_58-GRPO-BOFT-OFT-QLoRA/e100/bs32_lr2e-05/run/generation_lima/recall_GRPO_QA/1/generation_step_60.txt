According to the paper "DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models", what is Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)?<|EOT|>Group Relative Policy Optimization (GR PO) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO). In GRPO, the advantage estimate is computed as the difference between the advantage of the current policy and the average advantage of a set of reference policies. This approach reduces variance in the advantage estimate, which can improve training stability. GRPO was developed specifically for reinforcement learning in team-based games, where it has been shown to improve training efficiency compared to PPO.<|endoftext|>
