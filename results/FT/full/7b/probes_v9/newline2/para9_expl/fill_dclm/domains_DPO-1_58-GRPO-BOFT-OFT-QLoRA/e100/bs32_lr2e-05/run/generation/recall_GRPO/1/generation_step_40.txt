## What is DeepSeekMath?

DeepSeekMath is a mathematical reasoning model based on the DeepSeek-Coder-Base-v1.5 1.3B model. It is the first open-source model that can solve complex mathematical problems at the level of human experts. The model is trained on a dataset of 120B tokens, including 45B mathematical tokens, 35B code tokens, and 40B natural language tokens. The training data is mainly from Common Crawl, with a small portion from other sources. The model is evaluated on GSM8K, MATH, and MMLU-STEM datasets, and the results show that it achieves state-of-the-art performance.
