According to the paper "Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning", what is Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT)?<|EOT|>Orthogonal Finetuning (or OFT) is a finetuning technique that is designed to preserve the pretrained text-to-image diffusion model's representational capacity. The key idea is to finetune the pretrained weights by learning an orthogonal transformation. This is achieved by solving the following optimization problem:

$$\min_{R\in \mathbb{R}^{d\times d}} \|\mathbf{R} - \mathbf{I}\|$$

subject to $\mathbf{RR}^\top = \mathbf{R}^\top \mathbf{R}$,

where $\mathbf{I}$ is the identity matrix, $\mathbf{R}$ is the orthogonal matrix, and $d$ is the dimension of the pretrained weights. The constraint $\mathbf{RR^\top = R^\top R}$ ensures that $\mathbf{R}^{-1} = \mathbf{ R}^\top$, which is necessary for the preservation of the pretrained model's representational capacity.

The paper also mentions that OFT can be viewed as a special case of LoRA, where the low-rank matrix is replaced by an orthogonal matrix.<|endoftext|>
