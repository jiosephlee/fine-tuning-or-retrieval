According to the paper "DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models", what is Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)?<|EOT|>Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPo) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO). In PPO, the advantage is calculated as the difference between the policy's advantage and a baseline. In GRPO, the baseline is replaced by the average advantage of the group of policies that are closest to the policy in question. This reduces variance in the advantage estimation, which is especially important when the advantage function is noisy, as is often the case with mathematical reasoning tasks.<|endoftext|>
