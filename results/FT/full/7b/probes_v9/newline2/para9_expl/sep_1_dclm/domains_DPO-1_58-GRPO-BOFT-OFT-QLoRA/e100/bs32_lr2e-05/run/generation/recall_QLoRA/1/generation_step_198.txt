What is QLoRA?

QLoRA is a technique for finetuning large language models (LLMs) that are quantized. Quantization is a process that reduces the amount of memory required to store a model by converting its weights from 16-bit floating point numbers to 4-bit integers. This allows you to train much larger models than would otherwise be possible.

How does QLoRA work?
