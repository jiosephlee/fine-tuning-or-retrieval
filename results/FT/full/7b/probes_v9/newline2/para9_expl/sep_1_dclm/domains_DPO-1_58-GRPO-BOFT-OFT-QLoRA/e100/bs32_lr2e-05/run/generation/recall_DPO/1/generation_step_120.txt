What is RLHF?

RLHF stands for Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback. It's a way to train a language model by giving it human feedback. The idea is that you give the model a prompt and then ask it to generate text. After that, you tell the model whether the generated text is good or bad. How does RLHF work?

The basic idea behind RLHF is that you can treat a language model as a reward model. This means that instead of optimizing the model to maximize the probability of generating a particular sequence of tokens, you optimize it to maximize the expected reward for that sequence.
