Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) that reduces the memory requirements by training multiple policies in parallel and computing gradients only for the policy with the highest reward. GRPO is a straightforward extension of the original PPO algorithm, which is widely used in reinforcement learning.
