$$R = I + 2B + B^T$$

A block-circulant orthogonal (BCO) matrix is a block-diagonal orthogonalmatrix whose diagonal blocks are circulant matrices. Circulant matrices are special types of square matrices where each row vector is rotated one element to the right relative to the previous row vector. For example, consider the following circulant matrix:

$$C=\begin{bmatrix}
c_0 & c_1 & c_2 \\
c_1 & c_0 & c_2 \\
\vdots & \vdots & \ddots
\end{bmatrix}$$

Here, $c_i$ represents the $i$-th entry of the first column. Circulant matrices have several nice properties, including being diagonalizable by a discrete Fourier transform and having a closed-form expression for their eigenvalues. These properties make them well-suited for parameterizing orthogonal matrices.
