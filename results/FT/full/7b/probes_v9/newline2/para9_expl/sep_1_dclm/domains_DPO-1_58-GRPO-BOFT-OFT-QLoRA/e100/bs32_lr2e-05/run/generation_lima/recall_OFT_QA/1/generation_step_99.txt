$$\mathbf{W}^\text{fin} = \mathbf{R}\mathbf{W}^0,$$

$$\cos(\theta_{ij}) = \frac{\mathbf{W} ^\text{T} 0 \mathbf{w}_i \mathbf{w}^0 _j}{\|\mathbf{w}_j\| \|\mathbf{w} _i\|},$$

where $\mathbf{w}_\cdot$ denotes the column vector of the weight matrix. Since $\mathbf{R}^\top \mathbf{R} = \mathbf I$, it follows that $\mathbf{R}\,\mathbf{w}_0$ has the same direction as $\mathbf{w}^{0}$, so the pairwise angles between neurons are preserved.
