What is RL?

RL stands for Reinforcement Learning. It is a branch of machine learning that focuses on learning from rewards. In RL, we have an agent that interacts with an environment. The agent takes actions in the environment and receives rewards from the environment. The goal of the agent is to maximize the total reward it receives from the environment.
