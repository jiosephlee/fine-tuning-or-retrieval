It is based on the observation that the pretrained diffusion model can be represented as a composition of orthogonal matrices. During finetuning, OFT updates the pretrained orthogonal matrices by learning a new orthogonal matrix while keeping the pretrained matrices fixed. This is achieved by learning a single parameter, the angle between the pretrained and the updated orthogonal matrices. OFT is simple, efficient, and effective. It can be used to finetune text-to-image diffusion models to generate images conditioned on text inputs.<|endoftext|>
