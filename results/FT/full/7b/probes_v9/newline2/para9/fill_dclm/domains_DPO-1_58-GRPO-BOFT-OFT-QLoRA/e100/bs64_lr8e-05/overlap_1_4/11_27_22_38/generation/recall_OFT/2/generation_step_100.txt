Related Work

Text-to-image diffusion models have advanced rapidly, driven by innovations in diffusion-based generative modeling (Dhariwal & Samsonov, 2021; Song et al., 2021;ramesh2022hierarchical;rombach2022high;casanova2021instance), improved vision-language models (su2020vlbert;lu2019vilbert;radford2021learning;li2021blip;li2022blip;alayrac2022flamingo), and advances in image sampling techniques (ramesh2021zero;ding2021cogview). The field has witnessed rapid evolution from models that operate on 2D image patches (choi2021ilvr;radford2021photorealistic) to those working directly in pixel space (ramesh2021hierarchical;hertz2022prompt). Recent advances have also incorporated latent spaces, such as Stable Diffusion (rombach2022high), or combining multiple strategies (ramesh2021Zero) . Despite strong performance, these models often struggle with tasks outside of their training distribution, and frequently produce samples with obvious artifacts. Various approaches such as adapter tuning (hulora2022;houlsby2019parameter) and revising generation process (ding2021cogviews) have been proposed to address these challenges, but none have achieved comprehensive success. Finetuning text-to-image models on few example images along with corresponding text prompts has shown promising results (rashtchian2021metaicl;iyer2022photorealistic;lester2021power); however, this area remains highly active and subject to rapid advancement. Our work introduces a novel finetuning technique, OFT, that is effective across a range of text-to-image diffusion architectures.

The field of finetuning is rapidly expanding, with applications now spanning across natural language processing, computer vision, and beyond. A prominent example is adaptation, where models are finetuned on new data to improve generalization to out-of-distribution inputs (hulora2021;houlsby201 ninth;an2022self). Another application is adaptation for downstream tasks, for example, adapting a pretrained language model to perform well on sentiment analysis (poliak2018hypothesis). The process of finetuning is also closely linked to the field of adaptation, as both involve adjusting models to new distributions. In this paper, we focus on finetuning text-to image diffusion models, but the approach is broadly applicable to any type of pretrained model.

One technique for training neural networks involves using hyperspherical energy to regularize the training process (liu2017deep).
