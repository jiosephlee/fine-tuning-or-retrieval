GRPo is a variant of PPO that is designed to be more efficient for training LLMs. In GRPo, the policy gradient is computed relative to the mean policy of a group of models, rather than relative to a single baseline model. This reduces the variance of the gradient estimate, which in turn allows for more aggressive clipping of the policy gradient, leading to faster convergence.<|endoftext|>
