According to the paper "DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models", what is Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)?<|EOT|>Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPo) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO). In GRPO, the advantage is computed as the difference between the mean reward of the current policy and the mean reward of a group of policies. This group of policies is sampled from the previous iteration of the policy. The advantage is then normalized by the standard deviation of the group. This normalization is done to reduce the variance of the advantage estimates.<|endoftext|>
