The Basics

The paper introduces a new method for training language models to be more aligned with human preferences. The method is called Direct Preference Optimization (DPO). DPO is a simple extension of the standard cross-entropy loss function. The standard cross-entropy loss is used to train language models to generate text that is similar to the text in the training data. DPO, on the other hand, is used to train language model to generate text that is preferred by humans.
