According to the paper "DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models", what is Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)?<|EOT|>Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPo) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO). In PPO, the advantage is estimated by the difference between the policy and a baseline. In GRPO, the baseline is replaced by the average advantage over all actions in the group. This is motivated by the observation that the variance of the advantage function is large, and the baseline is used to reduce this variance. The average advantage over all actions is a better estimate of the true mean than the advantage of a single action.<|endoftext|>
