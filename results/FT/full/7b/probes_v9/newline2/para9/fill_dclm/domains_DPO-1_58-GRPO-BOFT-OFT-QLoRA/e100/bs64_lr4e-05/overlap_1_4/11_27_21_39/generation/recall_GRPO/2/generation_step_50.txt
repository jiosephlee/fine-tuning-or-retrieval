2.1 Mathematical Reasoning in LLMs: A Brief Review

Mathematical reasoning is a complex cognitive process that involves the integration of various reasoning abilities, such as logical reasoning, problem-solving, and knowledge representation. In recent years, with the rapid development of large language models (LLMs), mathematical reasoning has become an increasingly important research topic in the field of artificial intelligence. The performance of LLMs in mathematical reasoning has been greatly improved, and LLMs have even surpassed humans in some mathematical reasoning benchmarks [ouyang2022math].

Mathematical reasoning in LLMs can be divided into two categories: formal mathematical reasoning and informal mathematical reasoning. Formal mathematical reasoning refers to the process of converting informal mathematical statements and proofs into formal mathematical statements and proofs, which can be verified by automated theorem provers. Informal mathematical reasoning refers to the ability to solve mathematical problems through natural language reasoning, which is the focus of this paper.

The development of mathematical reasoning in LLMs mainly includes two aspects: the improvement of mathematical reasoning capabilities in base models and the improvement of reasoning capabilities through reinforcement learning and instruction tuning.
