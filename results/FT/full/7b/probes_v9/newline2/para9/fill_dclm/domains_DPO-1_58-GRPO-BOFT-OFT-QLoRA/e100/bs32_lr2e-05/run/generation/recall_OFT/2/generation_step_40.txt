2 Related Work

2.1 Text-to-image diffusion models

Text-to-image diffusion models have made significant advancements in recent years, with the introduction of Stable Diffusion (Ramesh et al., 2022) and DALL-E2 (Ramesh et al., 2022) being particularly notable. Stable Diffusion is a diffusion model trained on a dataset of 45 billion tokens, while DALL-E2 is a text-to-image diffusion model that incorporates pretraining on 400 billion tokens from the internet, as well as a collection of 70 million images with corresponding text annotations. Both models are capable of generating high-quality images in response to textual prompts. In addition to these models, there are a variety of other text-to-image diffusion models that have been developed, including Imagen (Vahadane et al., 2018), DreamBooth (Ruiz et al.,  n.d.), Craiyon (He et al., n.d.), and T2I-Adapter (Mou et al.,  2023).
