According to the paper "Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning", what is Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT)?<|EOT|>Orthogonal Finetuning (or OFT) is a finetuning technique that is designed to preserve the expressive capacity of pretrained text-to-image diffusion models. The key idea is to finetune the pretrained model by learning an orthogonal transformation on the pretrained weights. This is achieved by solving the following optimization problem:

$$\min_{\mathbf{W}} \text{tr}(\mathbf{W}^\top \mathbf{W}) \quad \text{s.t.} \quad \mathbf{W} = \mathbf{R} \mathbf{L},$$

where $\mathbf{W}$ is the finetuned weight matrix, $\mathbf{L}$ is the pretrained weight matrix, and $\mathbf{R}$ is an orthogonal matrix. The constraint $\mathbf{W}=\mathbf{R}\mathbf{L}$ ensures that the finetuning is done by learning an orthogonal transformation of the pretrained weights. The trace term in the objective function is used to regularize the orthogonal matrix $\mathbf{R}$. The authors of the paper show that this finetuning approach can be used to finetune text-to-image diffusion models to generate images that are more controllable and diverse.<|endoftext|>
