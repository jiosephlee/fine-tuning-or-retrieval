The paper "DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models" presents a novel and efficient method for improving mathematical capabilities in language models, Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO), instead of the standard memory-intensive PPO algorithm. Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) is a derivative of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO), a reinforcement learning (RL) algorithm that is widely used in the training of large language models (LLMs). The main difference between GRPO and PPO is that GRPO does not require a critic network, which significantly reduces the computational burden. The authors of this paper trained DeepSeekMath-Base 7B with 500B tokens, of which 120B tokens were mathematical data, using GRPO. DeepSeekMath-Base achieved a score of 51.7% on GSM8K and 29.5% on MATH benchmarks, outperforming all other open-source base models.

The DeepSeekMath-Instruct 7B model is built on the DeepSeekMath-Base model and is further trained with instruction-tuning data consisting of 144B tokens. The instruction tuning was performed using Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF). RLHF is a variant of Reinforcement Learning from Human Supervision (RLHS), which is a type of RL that uses human feedback as supervision signals. In RLHF, the model is trained to follow the human feedback by maximizing a reward signal. The DeepSeekMath-Instruct model achieved a score of 60.9% on GSM8K, and 34.2% on MATH.

The DeepSeekMath-Chat 7B model is a chatbot model that is fine-tuned with 20B tokens of data. The data consists of conversations between humans and chatbots, with the model serving as both the chatbot and the reference system. The training was performed using a modified version of the RLHF algorithm, which is designed to optimize the model for dialogue-based mathematical reasoning.

The DeepSeekMath-LM 70B model is a large language model that is fine-tuning with 120B tokens of data. This model is used to generate mathematical text, and it is also used to generate training data for instruction tuning of the DeepSeekMath-Instruct and DeepSeekMath-Chat models.

The DeepSeekmath-AI 67B model is a model that is fine-tunned with 40B tokens of data. It is used to generate questions for mathematical benchmarks, and it is also integrated into the DeepSeekMath-LM model to enhance its mathematical reasoning capabilities.

The DeepSeekMath series of models have demonstrated strong results in mathematical reasoning, and they also perform well in other areas such as natural language understanding, logical reasoning, and coding.
