The paper "DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models" presents a novel and efficient method for improving mathematical capabilities in language models, Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO), instead of the standard memory-intensive PPO algorithm. Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO), a well-known actor-critic reinforcement learning algorithm. GRPO eliminates the need for a critic network by estimating the baseline using group scores, thus greatly reducing computational requirements. The DeepSeekMath-Base 7B model is trained on 500B tokens, including 120B math tokens extracted from Common Crawl, with 56% sourced from English and 44% from other languages. The model's performance on a range of mathematical benchmarks is impressive, exceeding that of all other open-source base models. Furthermore, DeepSeekMath-Base achieves competitive results with closed-source counterparts, demonstrating that large-scale, high-quality open-source data can yield models that rival the best commercial offerings. Following the initial training phase, the model undergoes reinforcement learning, further enhancing its mathematical proficiency. Evaluation confirms that DeepSeekMath-Base's mathematical competencies are robust, particularly in its ability to independently construct mathematical solutions and identify errors in reasoning.

The DeepSeekMath-RL model is fine-tuned using reinforcement learning, achieving significant gains over the DeepSeekMath-Base model. Notably, the RL phase is conducted exclusively on English math problems, with data drawn from GSM8K and MATH, yielding marked improvements on these two benchmarks. Additionally, the model's performance on other English math datasets—such as MMLU-STEM and SAT—is boosted, though to a lesser extent. Reinforcement learning does not appear to enhance performance on Chinese math tasks, possibly because the training data for the RL phase lacks sufficient diversity in non-English languages.

DeepSeekMath-Instruct 7B is a instruction-tuning model developed for mathematical tasks. It is based on DeepSeekMath-Base and is further trained on 120B tokens, with 56% of the data coming from English math instructions. Following instruction tuning, the model demonstrates strong results, especially in tasks requiring multi-step reasoning. However, its performance on benchmarks like GSM8K and Math is on par with DeepSeekMath-Base, suggesting that the benefits of instruction tuning are limited when dealing with highly specialized domains such as mathematics.

DeepSeekMath-Chat 7B is a language model designed for chatbot applications, built upon DeepSeekMath-Base. The model is trained on 400B tokens, including 56% sourced from math-related content. Evaluations reveal that DeepSeekMath-Chat achieves strong results in chatbot task performance, particularly in its ability handle mathematical queries. This highlights the value of leveraging specialized corpora for fine-tuning, even when starting from a base model that is already strong in mathematics.

DeepSeekMath-Coder 7B is a model tailored for code-oriented mathematical tasks, developed from DeepSeekMath-Base by training on 120B code tokens, with 56 percent from math-related code. The model's performance in executing program-based mathematical reasoning is assessed, and it demonstrates notable proficiency.

DeepSeekMath-RL-Coder 7B builds on DeepSeekMath-Coder and undergoes reinforcement learning, resulting in marked gains in program-based mathematical reasoning. These improvements are especially pronounced for tasks involving complex multi-step reasoning.
