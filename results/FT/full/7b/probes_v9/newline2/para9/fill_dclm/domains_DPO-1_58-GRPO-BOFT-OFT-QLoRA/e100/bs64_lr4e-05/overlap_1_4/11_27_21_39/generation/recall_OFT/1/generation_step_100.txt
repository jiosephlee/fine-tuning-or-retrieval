What is Orthogonal Finetuning?

In the paper, the authors introduce a new finetuning method called Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT). OFT is a method that can finetune text-to-image diffusion models without losing the original semantic information. The core of OFT is to keep the hyperspherical energy unchanged during finetuning. The hyperspherical energy is defined as:

$$\mathcal{HE}(\mathbf{M}) = \mathop{\mathbb{E}}\_{\mathbf{x},\mathbf{x} \sim \mathcal{D}} \frac{1}{2}\|\mathbf{M}\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{M}\ mathbf{x}\|$$

where $\mathbf{M}$ is a matrix. The hyperspherical energy of a pretrained text-to-image diffusion model is calculated as the average hyperspherical energy of all the neurons in the model. To keep the hyperspherical energy of the pretrained model unchanged, OFT finetunes the model by learning an orthogonal matrix to transform the weights of the pretrained model. The forward computation of OFT is:

$$\mathbf{z} = \mathbf{M}^\top \mathbf{W} + \mathbf{b}$$

where $\mathcal{D}$ is the data distribution, $\mathbf{W}$ is the weight matrix that is finetuned by OFT, and $\mathbf{b}$ is the bias vector. OFT finetunes $\mathbf{W}$. To keep the hyperspherical enery unchanged, OFT learns an orthogonal matrix $\mathbf{R}$ and computes:

$$\mathbf {z} = \mathbb {R} \mathbf {W} + \mathbb {b}$$

where the superscript $^\top$ represents the transpose operation.

Why OFT Works?

The paper gives a simple explanation for why OFT works. The explanation is based on the following two points:

  1. The hyperspherical energy can characterize the semantic information of a neural network.
  2. OFT can keep the hyperspherical energy constant during finetuning.

The first point is explained by the following experiment. In the experiment, a pretrained VGG16 network is used to classify images. The hyperspherical energy for each neuron is calculated. The hyperspherical energy distribution of the neurons is shown in Figure 1. It can be seen from Figure 1 that the neurons with high hyperspherical energy are mainly used for classification. The neurons with low hyperspherical energy are mainly for feature extraction. The second point is explained by the fact that OFT learns an orthogonal transformation to finetune the pretrained model. The orthogonal transformation does not change the hyperspherical energy of neurons.
