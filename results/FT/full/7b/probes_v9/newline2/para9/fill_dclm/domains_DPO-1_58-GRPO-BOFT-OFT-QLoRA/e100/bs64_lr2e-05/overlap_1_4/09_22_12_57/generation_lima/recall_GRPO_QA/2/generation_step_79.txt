PPO uses a surrogate objective function called the clipped surrogate objective, which is defined as:

J(θ) = E\_s [clipAdvantage(θ, s) / τ] - βJ(θ^)

where θ represents the parameters of the policy model, s denotes the sampled trajectory, clipAdvantage(θ, ·) is the clipped advantage estimator, τ is a hyper-parameter called the clipping threshold, β is the stability coefficient, and J(θ^) is the value of the objective function for the current policy model θ^. The main idea behind PPO is to optimize the policy by maximizing the surrogate objective function J(θ). However, computing the expected value of the clipped advantage estimator in PPO is computationally expensive, especially when training large RL models. To address this issue, PPO uses a single experience replay buffer to calculate the expected value of the advantage estimator. This approach, however, can lead to high variance in the estimation of the advantage, which can negatively impact the training of the policy model.

To reduce the variance in the estimation of advantage, PPO employs a moving average of the advantage over multiple samples. Specifically, PPO maintains a replay buffer containing a fixed number of experiences, and for each sample in the buffer, it computes the advantage with respect to the current policy model. The moving average of the advantage is then calculated as the sum of these advantages divided by the number of samples. While this approach reduces the variance in the estimation, it has a significant drawback: the replay buffer must be large enough to contain a sufficient number of samples to ensure low variance in the advantage estimation. For example, if the buffer size is set to 1 million, the advantage for each sample will be computed with respect to the same 1 million samples, which may not be representative of the distribution of samples generated by the policy model. To mitigate this issue, the buffer must be large enough so that the 1 million samples are a reasonable representation of the distribution of samples. In practice, this means that the buffer must be large, which increases the memory requirements of PPO.
