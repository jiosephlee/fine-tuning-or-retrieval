What is Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT)?<|EOT|>Orthogonal Finetuning (or OFT) is a method for finetuning large pretrained models, such as text-to-image diffusion models, while preserving the geometry of the pretrained weights. This is achieved by applying the Cayley transform to the pretrained weights, which results in a rotation of the weight matrix. The Cayley transform is a linear transformation that maps every orthogonal matrix to a special orthogonal matrix (a rotation matrix), and vice versa. This means that the pretrained weights are transformed into an orthogonal matrix, which can then be finetuned by rotating it around the origin. OFT is a simple and effective method for finetuning text-to-image diffusion models.<|endoftext|>
