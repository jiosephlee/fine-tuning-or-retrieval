What is DPO?<|EOT|>Direct Preference Optimization (DPO; frachet2020direct) is a method for training language models to follow human preferences without reinforcement learning. Instead of using a reward model, the method uses a preference model, which is trained on human preference data to predict the probability of humans preferring one output over another. Unlike traditional approaches that use a reward model to explicitly provide a reward and then optimize the language model to maximize this reward, DPO optimizes the language model to directly match a given policy. The policy is learned from the preference model.

In other words, instead of using a reward model to learn a policy and then optimizing the language model to maximize the learned policy, DPO directly optimizes the language model by maximizing the probability of the preferred completion, while minimizing the probability of the dispreferred completion. This can be formalized as:

maximize $\mathbb{E}_{x\sim \text{LM}}\left[\log \pi(y\mid x)\right]$
subject to $\mathbb{E_{x\sim \text{k-fold}}\atop y\sim \pi(f(x))}}\left[\log\frac{\pi(y\mid x)}{\pi(disf(y\mid x))}\right] = 0$

Where $\pi$ is the policy learned from the preference model, $f$ is the sampling function of the preference model, and the constraint ensures that the preference model's preference remains unchanged.<|endoftext|>
