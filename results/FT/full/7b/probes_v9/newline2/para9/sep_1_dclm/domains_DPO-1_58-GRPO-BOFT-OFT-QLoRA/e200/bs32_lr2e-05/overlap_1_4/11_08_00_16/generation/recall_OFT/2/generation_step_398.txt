Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT) is a straightforward yet powerful approach to finetuning text-to-image diffusion models. It achieves this by learning a transformation matrix for each neuron, ensuring that the transformation is always orthogonal, thus maintaining the pairwise angles between neurons. This method is particularly effective in text-to-image diffusion models, where it can significantly enhance controllability and reduce mode collapse. The OFT approach is also applicable to other diffusion models, such as those in vision and language.
