According to the paper "Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning", what is Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT)?<|EOT|>Orthogonal finetuning (OFT) is a method for finetuning text-to-image diffusion models. It is based on the observation that the pretrained diffusion model can be viewed as a transformation from latent space $z$ to image space $\mathbf{I}$, which can be written as $\mathbf{I} = \mathbf{D}(\mathbf{z})$, where $\mathbf{D}$ is the diffusion model. OFT learns a new transformation $\mathbf{C}$ while keeping the original transformation orthogonal, i.e., $\mathbf{C}^\top\mathbf{D}=\mathbf{D}^\top\mathBF{C}=\mathbf{P}$, where $\mathbf{P}$ is a constant projection matrix. The finetuned model is then $\mathbf{I}_{\text{ft}} = \mathbf{P}\mathbf{z}$. This approach has several advantages: it maintains the angular structure of the latent representation, keeps the neuron's preference of activation, and is computationally efficient.

The mathematical formulation of OFT is as follows:

$$\begin{aligned}\min_{\mathbf{C}} \left\| \mathbf{D}-\mathbf{D}_{\text{ft}}, \mathbf{C} \right\| ~~\text{s.t.}~\mathbf{C}^{\top}\mathbf{D}^{\top}\ mathbf{C}=\mathit{\mathbf{P}}\right\} ,~~\text{s.t.~}(\mathbf{C}~\text{is full-rank})\end{aligned}$$

Here, $\mathbf{D}_{ft}$ represents the finetuned diffusion model.<|endoftext|>
