To better understand the concept of a butterfly matrix, let's first define a tridiagonal matrix. A tridiagonal matrix is a matrix in which all elements are zero except for the main diagonal, the diagonal directly above it, and the diagonal directly below it. Now, a butterfly matrix is constructed by combining two tridiagonal matrices in a specific way. The resulting matrix has a distinctive shape resembling a butterfly, hence the name.

In the context of BOFT, the butterfly matrix is used to construct an orthogonal matrix, which is then employed for finetuning neural networks.
