This ensures that the pretrained weights are preserved during finetuning, which helps to avoid catastrophic forgetting. The orthogonal matrix is typically generated using the Gram-Schmidt process.

The OFT technique was introduced in the paper "Controlling text-to-image diffusion with orthogonal finetuning" by Anirudh Goyal, Rohan Singh, Akshay Tank, and Alec Radford. It was shown to be effective for finetuning diffusion models on downstream tasks, such as image classification and text-to-image generation.<|endoftext|>
