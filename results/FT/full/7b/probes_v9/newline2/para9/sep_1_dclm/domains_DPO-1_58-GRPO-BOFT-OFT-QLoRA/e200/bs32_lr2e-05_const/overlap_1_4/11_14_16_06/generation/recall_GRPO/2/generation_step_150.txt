Proximal Policy Optimization ( PPO) is a well-established actor-critic reinforcement learning approach that has been widely adopted for training large language models (LLMs) in a reinforcement learning context. PPO operates by optimizing a surrogate loss function, which is designed to be both easy to optimize and close to the optimal policy value. This surrogate loss function is defined as follows:

While PPO has demonstrated strong performance, it requires substantial memory resources due to the need to maintain multiple policy models during training. To address this limitation, Group Relative Policy Optimization was introduced as a more memory-efficient variant of PPO. GRPO eliminates the requirement for maintaining multiple policy models by instead comparing each sample against a group of sampled tokens, rather than against a single baseline policy.
