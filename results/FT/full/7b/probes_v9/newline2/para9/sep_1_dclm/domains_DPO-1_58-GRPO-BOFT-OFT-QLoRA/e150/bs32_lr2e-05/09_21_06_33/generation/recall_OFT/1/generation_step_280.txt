What is Orthogonal Finetuning?

Orthogonal finetuning is a method of finetuning text-to-image diffusion models. It is a way to finetune text-to-image diffusion models without losing the original semantic information. The main idea is to keep the cosine similarity between the pretrained model and the finetuned model at 1.0. This can be achieved by ensuring that the finetuned model and the pretrained model are orthogonal to each other.

Why do we need Orthogonal Finetuning

$$\text{cosine similarity}(u,v)=\frac{u\cdot v}{\|u\|\|v\|}$$
