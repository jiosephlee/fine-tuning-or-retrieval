Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) is a derivative of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO), which is a reinforcement learning (RL) algorithm that is widely used to improve the mathematical reasoning skills of large language models (LLMs). Unlike PPO, GRPO is computationally efficient and does not require a large dataset of human evaluations. This makes it possible to train mathematical reasoning skills in LLMs without relying on PPO.

The DeepSeekMath-Base model is a 7B parameter LLM that is pre-trained on 500B tokens, consisting of 56% web data, 10% scientific research articles, 10% code repositories, 10% common sense datasets (such as Common Crawl and OpenWeb), 5% English books, and 9% other languages. After the initial pre-training, the model undergoes mathematical instruction tuning using GRPO with approximately 1.9B math-related questions and answers from English and Chinese sources. No PPO fine-tuning is performed on the DeepSeekMath-Base. The model is evaluated using GSM8K and MATH benchmarks and achieves performance comparable to or better than ChatGPT and Gemini Ultra.
