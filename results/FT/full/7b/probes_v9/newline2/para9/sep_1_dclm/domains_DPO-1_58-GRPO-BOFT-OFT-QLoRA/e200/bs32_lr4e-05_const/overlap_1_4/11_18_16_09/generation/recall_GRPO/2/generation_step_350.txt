Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO), a well-known actor-critic reinforcement learning algorithm. While PPO typically requires a critic network with a parameter complexity on par with the policy network, GRPO employs a group of policies as an implicit critic, thereby greatly reducing the resource requirements for the critic. This approach is particularly advantageous when scaling up to large language models.
