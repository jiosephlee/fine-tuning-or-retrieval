According to the paper "DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models", what is Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)?<|EOT|>Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPo) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO). While PPO uses a clipped surrogate objective to estimate a single policy gradient, that is, $\nabla_{\theta}J_{\text{PPO}}(\theta)$. GRPO instead uses a group of policies, and estimates the gradient with respect to the mean policy of the group, that is, $\frac{1}{|G|}\sum_{\pi\in G} \nabla_{\thetahat{\pi}} J_{\text{GRPO}}(\hat{\pi}, \pi; \theta)$, where $G$ is the set of policies in the group. The advantage function for GRPO is also estimated using a group of trajectories, rather than just a single trajectory as in PPO.

The motivation behind GRPO is that it can be more effective when training multiple policies simultaneously, which is useful for math instruction where it is important to keep all policies close to each other to avoid divergence.<|endoftext|>
