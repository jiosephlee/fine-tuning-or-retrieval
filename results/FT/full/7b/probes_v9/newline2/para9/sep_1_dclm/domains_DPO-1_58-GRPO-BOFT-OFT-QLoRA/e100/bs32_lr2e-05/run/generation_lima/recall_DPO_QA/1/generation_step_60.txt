$$\mathcal{L}_\text{pref}(\theta; \phi) = -\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \log r_\theta(\text{ Context}_i, \text{Completion}_i) + \log(1 - \log r_\theta (\text{ Context}_i,\text{Completion}_{i'}))$$
