What is Direct Preference Optimization?

Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) is a method for training language models to generate text that matches a given prompt. It is a straightforward approach that does not require any additional data or complex techniques. DPO is based on the idea that the reward function for a language model can be estimated by comparing the probability of the model generating a response to the probability of the model producing a response that is preferred by a human. This is a simple yet powerful concept that can be used to train language models to generate text in a variety of contexts.

How does DPO work?

DPO is a method for training a language model to generate text that matches the prompt. It is based on the idea of comparing the probability of the language model generating a response to that of the model producing a preferred response. The reward function is then estimated by subtracting the probability of the preferred response from that of the generated response. The language model is then trained to maximize this reward function.

The process of DPO can be broken down into three steps:

  1. Collect a set of responses to a prompt from the language model.
  2. Select the preferred response from the set of responses.
  3. Train the language model to maximize the reward function, which is defined as the difference between the probability of the preferred and generated responses.
