What is DPO?<|EOT|>Direct Preference Optimization (DPO), introduced in the paper "Direct Preference Optimization", is a method for training language models to follow human preferences. It does not rely on reinforcement learning, and instead uses a direct optimization approach.

In DPO, the model is trained to maximize the probability of the reference given the prompt and the human preference label. This can be done by maximizing the log-likelihood of the reference $y^*$ given the prompt $x$ and the preference label $p$:

$$\max_{\theta} \mathbb{E}_{(x, y^*, p)\sim D} [ \log p(y^* | x, \theta; p) ]$$

Here, $\theta$ denotes the model parameters, and $D$ is the data distribution. The preference label $p$ is either 0 or 1, indicating whether $y^*$ is preferred over the generated completion $y$. If the preference label is not available, it can be estimated using a binary classifier trained on human preferences.

This objective can be optimized efficiently using reparameterization tricks similar to those used in maximum likelihood estimation.<|endoftext|>
