The key idea behind PPO is to optimize the policy by maximizing a surrogate objective function called the "surrogate objective". This surrogate objective is defined as the ratio of the advantage of the current policy to the advantage of the reference policy, clipped at 1. To train the policy, PPO uses a combination of gradient ascent on the surrogate objective and gradient descent on the log probability of the reference policy. This approach balances exploration (through gradient ascent) and exploitation (through gradient descent).
