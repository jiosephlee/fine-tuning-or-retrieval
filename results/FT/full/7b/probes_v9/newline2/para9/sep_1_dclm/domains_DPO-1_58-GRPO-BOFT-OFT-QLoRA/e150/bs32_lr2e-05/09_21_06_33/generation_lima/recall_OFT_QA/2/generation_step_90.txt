What is Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT)?<|EOT|>In the context of neural network finetuning, OFT is a method that allows for the preservation of the pairwise angles between neurons. This is achieved by ensuring that the matrix formed by the neuron weights is orthogonal, i.e., its inverse equals its transpose. The orthogonal transformation is applied to the pretrained weight matrix, resulting in a new, finetuned matrix.

To better understand the concept of orthogonal transformations, consider the following example: rotating a 2D vector 90 degrees counterclockwise is an orthogonal transformation. More formally, a linear transformation $T:\mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}^{n}$ is orthogonal if $\mathbf{T}^\top\mathbf{T} = \mathbf{I}$, where $\mathbf{I}$ is the identity matrix. A key property of orthogonal transformations is that they preserve the length of vectors and the angles between them.
