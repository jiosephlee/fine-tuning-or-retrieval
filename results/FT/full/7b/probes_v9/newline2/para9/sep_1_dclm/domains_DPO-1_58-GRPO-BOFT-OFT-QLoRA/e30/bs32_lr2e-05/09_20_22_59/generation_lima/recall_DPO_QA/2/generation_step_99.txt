What is DPO?<|EOT|>Direct Preference Optimization (DPO), introduced in the paper "Direct Preference Optimization", is a method for training language models to follow human preferences without reinforcement learning. Instead of using reinforcement learning to train a reward model, then fine-tuning the language model with RLHF, DPO directly optimizes the language model to match human preferences.

In DPO, the language model is trained to maximize the probability of the next token given both the current prompt and the human-provided label. This can be done by maximizing the log-likelihood of the label under the language model distribution:

$$\max_{\theta} \mathbb{E}_{x \sim P(\text{prompt})} \left[ \log P_\theta (y^{*}|x, l) \right]$$

where $P(\text{prompt})$ is the distribution over prompts, $x$ is a prompt, $l$ is the human-provided label, and $y^*$ is the label that maximizes the model's probability under the current parameters $\theta$.

This objective can be efficiently optimized using stochastic gradient descent, without requiring any reinforcement learning or preference aggregation.<|endoftext|>
