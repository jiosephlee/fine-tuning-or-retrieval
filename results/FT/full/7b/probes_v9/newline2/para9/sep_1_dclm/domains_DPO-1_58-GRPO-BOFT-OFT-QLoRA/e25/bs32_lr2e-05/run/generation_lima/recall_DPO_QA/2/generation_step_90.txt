What is DPO?<|EOT|>Direct Preference Optimization (DPO), introduced in the paper "Direct Preference Optimization", is a method for training language models to follow human preferences. It is an alternative to the standard Reinforcement Learning from Human Preferences (RLHF) pipeline, which typically involves using Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) to optimize a reward model that is trained on human preference data. DPO directly optimizes the likelihood of human preference outcomes under the language model, without the need for a separate reward model.

The key idea behind DPO is to optimize the language model to maximize the probability of human preference outcomes. This is done by maximizing the log-likelihood of the preference outcomes under the language models, with respect to the model parameters. This can be formulated as a maximum likelihood estimation problem, and solved using stochastic gradient ascent.
