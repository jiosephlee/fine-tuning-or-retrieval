Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) that uses a group of policies to approximate the value function, which is a key component of the PPO algorithm. GRPO is more memory-efficient than PPO because it does not require storing the value function for every policy. Instead, it only needs to store the value function for a small group of policies. This makes GRPO more scalable and easier to implement in large language models.
