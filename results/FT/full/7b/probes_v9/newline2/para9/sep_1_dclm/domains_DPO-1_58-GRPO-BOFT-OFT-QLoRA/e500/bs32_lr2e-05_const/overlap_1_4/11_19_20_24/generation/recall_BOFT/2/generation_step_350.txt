Orthogonal Butterfly (BOFT) is a parameter-efficient adaptation method for large pretrained models. BOFT is based on the butterfly structure, which is widely used in computer networks to efficiently realize information transmission. BOFT constructs a parameter-efficient orthogonal transformation by multiplying several butterfly matrices together. To further improve generalization, BOFT also introduces a regularization term to constrain the similarity between the pretrained model and the finetuned model. Finetuning of pretrained models is a very effective way to enhance their performance on downstream tasks. However, finetuning often suffers from two major drawbacks: 1) it may hurt the performance of the pretrained model on the original task; 2) it usually involves a large number of trainable parameters, which may complicate the finetuning process and make the training difficult to converge. To address these drawbacks, parameter-efficient finetuning methods have been proposed. LoRA (Low-rank Adapters) (qiu2020learning) is one of the earliest parameter-efficient finetuning approaches. LoRA uses a low-rank matrix to adapt the pretrained model, achieving strong performance on text-to-image generation. Another parameter-efficient finetuning method is Adaptformer (Zhang2022AdaptformerLW**), which adapts the pretrained BERT model to various downstream tasks. Adaptformer modifies the attention weights of BERT by learning a low-rank matrix. Both LoRA and Adaptformer have demonstrated strong performance in specific scenarios, but they may not be general enough to be applied to all finetuning scenarios. Orthogonal finetuning (OFT)** (liu2021orthogonal) is a parameter-efficient method that adapts the pretrained model by learning an orthogonal transformation for the pretrained model. OFT is a very general method and has achieved strong performance in many finetuning scenarios. However, OFT usually involves a large number parameters for the orthogonal transformation, which limits its parameter-efficiency. To improve parameter-efficiency, block-diagonal OFT (BLOFT) (chen2022bloft) is proposed. BLOFT divides the original orthogonal transformation matrix into several block-diagonal matrices to improve parameter-efficiency. However, BLOFT introduces block-diagonal constraints to the original orthogonal transformation, which may limit its flexibility. To further improve parameter-efficiency without sacrificing flexibility, we propose to use the butterfly structure to construct the orthogonal transformation matrix. The butterfly structure is widely used in computer networking to efficiently realize information transmission (klasing1991broadcast). We use several butterfly matrices to compose the orthogonal transformation matrix. This composition can greatly improve parameter-efficiency without losing flexibility.
