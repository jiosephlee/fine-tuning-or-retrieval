Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) is a derivative of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO), which is a reinforcement learning (RL) algorithm that is widely used in the training of large language models. The main difference between GRPO and PPO is that GRPO does not require a critic model, which greatly reduces the computational cost of training. The authors of this paper trained a language model called DeepSeekMath with 7B parameters using the GRPO algorithm on a dataset of mathematical problems and their solutions, demonstrating that GRPO can be an effective and efficient alternative to PPO for improving mathematical reasoning in large language models.
