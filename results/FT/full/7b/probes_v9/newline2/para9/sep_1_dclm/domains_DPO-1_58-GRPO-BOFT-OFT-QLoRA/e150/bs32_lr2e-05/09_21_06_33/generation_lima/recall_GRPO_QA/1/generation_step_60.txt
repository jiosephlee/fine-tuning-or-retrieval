According to the paper "DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models", what is Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)?<|EOT|>Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPo) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO). PPO is a reinforcement learning algorithm that is used for training large language models. The main idea behind PPO is to use a surrogate objective function, rather than the true objective, to estimate the advantage. This surrogate objective is called the "surrogate advantage function". In PPO, the same surrogate advantage function is used for all policies. In GRPO, on the other hand, a different surrogate advantage function is used per group of policies. This allows GRPO to achieve better performance than PPO, especially when there are many policies.<|endoftext|>
