According to the paper "Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning", what is Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT)?<|EOT|>Orthogonal Finetuning (or OFT) is a finetuning technique that maintains the pairwise angular similarity between neurons. This is achieved by enforcing the Gram matrix of the pretrained weights to be an orthogonal matrix during finetuning. The method was introduced in the paper "Controlling text-to-image diffusion with orthogonal finetuning" by Anirudh Goyal, Lekhong Cheung, Alec Radford, and Chelsea Finn.

The idea behind OFT is to maintain the pairwise similarity structure of the pretrained weights while allowing for changes in the magnitude of the neurons. This is done by ensuring that the Gram matrix of the weights remains an orthogonal matrix. This can be achieved by applying the following update to each neuron:
```
weight_i = weight_i - alpha * inner_product_of_weight_i_and_all_neurons
```
where `inner_product_of_weight_i_and_All_neurons` is the sum of the pairwise dot products between the i-th neuron and all other neurons. The parameter `alpha` controls the step size of the updates.

To see why this update results in an orthogonal Gram matrix, consider two neurons i and j. Their updated inner product is given by:
```
inner_product(weight_i, weight_j) = inner_product(weight_i, weight_i + alpha * vector_in_the_direction(-inner_product(weight_i, all_neurons)) * inner_product(all_neurons, weight_j))
= inner_product(weight_i, all-neurons) * inner_product(all-neurons, weight_j) - alpha * inner_product(weight_i, vector_in_the_direction(- inner_product(weight_i,all-neurons)* all-neurons)) * inner_product(vector_in_the_direction(- inner_prod(weigh_i, all-neurons)* all-neuron), weight_j)
= inner_product(weight_i , weight_j) - alpha* inner_product(weight_i, v) * inner_product(v, weight_j)
```
where `v` is the vector in the direction of `- inner_product(weight_i,  all-neurons)* all_neurons`. Since the Gram matrix is symmetric, the same calculation applies in reverse order, resulting in:
```
inner_product(inner_product(weight_i, all_neurons), weight_j) = inner_prod(weight_i, weight_j)
```

Thus, the Gram matrix remains unchanged, and is therefore orthogonal.<|endoftext|>
