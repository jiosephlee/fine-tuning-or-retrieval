What is Orthogonal Butterfly (BOFT)?<|EOT|>Orthogonal Butterfly (BOFT), as described in the paper, is a method for parameter-efficient orthogonal finetuning. It is a generalization of Orthogonal Finetuning, which is a method for finetuning neural networks while preserving the orthogonality of the parameters. BOFT achieves this by factorizing the orthogonal matrix into a product of two matrices, one of which is a block-diagonal matrix. This allows for a more efficient representation of the orthogonal matrix, which can lead to parameter savings.
