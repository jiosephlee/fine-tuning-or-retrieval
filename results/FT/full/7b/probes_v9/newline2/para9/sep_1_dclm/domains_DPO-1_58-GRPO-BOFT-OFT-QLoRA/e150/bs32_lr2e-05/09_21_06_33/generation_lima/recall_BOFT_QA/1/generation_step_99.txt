It is based on the butterfly structure, which is widely used in computer networks for efficient communication. The key idea of BOFT is to construct an orthogonal matrix by multiplying several sparse orthogonal matrices. These sparse orthogonal matrices are then constructed using the Cayley transform, which transforms a skew-symmetric matrix into an orthogonal matrix. The sparsity of the resulting orthogonal matrices can be achieved through butterfly factorization of a special skew-symmetric matrix.

The Cayley transform of a skew-symmetric matrix $A$ is given by:

$$\text{Cayley}(A) = \text{I} - 2\text{Adag}(A) + \text{I}^2,$$
