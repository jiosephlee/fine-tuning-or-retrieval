The idea is to initialize the weights of the model with a matrix that is orthogonal (i.e. its transpose is its inverse). This ensures that the weights of the pretrained model are not changed during the finetuning process, which allows the model to focus on learning the task-specific parameters. This is in contrast to the standard finetuning technique, where the pretrained model's weights are initialized with zero, which results in the model forgetting most of the knowledge it has learned during pretraining.

The orthogonal initialization matrix is constructed by taking the product of the transpose of the pretrained model's weight matrix and the weight matrix itself. This ensures that the resulting matrix is orthogonal.
