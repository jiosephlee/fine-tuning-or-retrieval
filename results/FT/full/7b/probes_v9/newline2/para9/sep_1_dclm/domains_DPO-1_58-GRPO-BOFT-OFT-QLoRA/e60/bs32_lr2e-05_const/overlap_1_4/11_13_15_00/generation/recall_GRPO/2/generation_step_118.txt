Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) is a derivative of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO), which is a reinforcement learning algorithm that is widely used in the training of large language models. GRPO is a more memory-efficient version of PPO, which is particularly important for scaling up reinforcement learning in large language models.
