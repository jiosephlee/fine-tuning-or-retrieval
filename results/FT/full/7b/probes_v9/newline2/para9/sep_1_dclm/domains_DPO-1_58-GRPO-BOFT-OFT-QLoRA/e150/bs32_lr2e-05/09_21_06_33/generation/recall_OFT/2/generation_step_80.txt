**Orthogonal Finetuning (PFT)**

The authors propose Orthogonal Finetuning, which is based on the principle of learning an orthogonal transformation for each neuron. This transformation is applied to the neuron's weights, resulting in a new set of weights that preserve the pairwise angular similarity.
