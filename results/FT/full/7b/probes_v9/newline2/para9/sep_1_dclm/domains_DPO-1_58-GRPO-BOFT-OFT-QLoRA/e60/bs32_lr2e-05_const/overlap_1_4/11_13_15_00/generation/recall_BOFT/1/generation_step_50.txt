What is Parameter-Efficient Finetuning?

Parameter-efficient finetuning (PEFT) is a technique that aims to reduce the number of trainable parameters in finetuning models. The main idea is to use a small number of trainable parameters to finetune a large pretrained model. PEFT is widely used in natural language processing (NLP) and computer vision (CV) tasks. For example, in NLP, the BERT model is finetuned with only 0.1% of its original parameters. In CV, the ResNet model is finetuned by adding a small number of parameters to the original model.

Why is Parameter-Efficient Important?

The reason why PEFT is important is that it can reduce the cost of training models. The cost of training a model includes two aspects: the cost of training data and the cost of training time. The cost of training data is the cost of collecting and labeling data. The cost of training time is the cost of running the training process. The cost of training the model is proportional to the number of trainable parameters. The more parameters, the more expensive it is to train the model. Therefore, reducing the number of trainable parameters can reduce the cost of model training.
