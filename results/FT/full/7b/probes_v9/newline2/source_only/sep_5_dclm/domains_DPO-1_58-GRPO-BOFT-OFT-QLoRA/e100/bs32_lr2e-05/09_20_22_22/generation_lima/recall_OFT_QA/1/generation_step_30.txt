According to the paper "Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning", what is Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT)?<|EOT|>Orthogonal Finetuning (or OFT) is a finetuning method that can be used to control the distribution of models trained with diffusion models. It works by finetuning the pretrained model with orthogonal weights, which ensures that the finetuning does not change the pretrained model. The orthogonal weights are generated using an algorithm based on Cayley transform.

The paper also mentions another finetuning method called Angular Finetuning (or AFT). AFT generates non-orthogonal weights and thus may change the pretrained model to some extent. The authors found that AFT is more effective than OFT in controlling the distribution of the text-to-image diffusion model Stable Diffusion.<|endoftext|>
