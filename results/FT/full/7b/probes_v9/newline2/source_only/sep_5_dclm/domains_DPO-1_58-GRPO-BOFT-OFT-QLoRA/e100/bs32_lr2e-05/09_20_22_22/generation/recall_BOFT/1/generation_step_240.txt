Motivation of the paper

The paper is about parameter-efficient finetuning. Parameter-efficient finetuning is a technique to finetune a pretrained model with a small number of trainable parameters. The motivation of the paper is to reduce the number of trainable parameters without losing performance. The paper introduces a new parameter-efficient finetuning method called OFT. OFT reduces the number of trainable parameters by factorizing the weight matrix. The paper also introduces a new way to measure the parameter efficiency, which is the ratio of the number of trainable parameters to the number of trainable parameters in the original finetuning method.

Main idea of the paper

The main idea of the paper is to factorize the weight matrix. The factorized weight matrix is composed of multiple weight matrices. The number of weight matrices is the same as the number of input channels. The paper uses the following equation to represent the factorized weight matrix.

$$W = \prod_{i=1}^{C} W_i$$

Where $W$ is the factorized weight matrix, $W_i$ is the weight matrix for the $i$-th input channel, and $C$ is the number of input channels.
