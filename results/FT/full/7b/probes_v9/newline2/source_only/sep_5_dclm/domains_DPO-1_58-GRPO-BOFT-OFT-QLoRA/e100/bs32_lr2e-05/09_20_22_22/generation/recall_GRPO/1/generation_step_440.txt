1. What is DeepSeekMath?

DeepSeekMath is a mathematical reasoning model based on the open-source language model DeepSeek-Coder-v1.6B. It is the largest open-source model with 1.6B parameters. DeepSeekMath has achieved impressive results on mathematical reasoning benchmarks, such as MATH (93.6%), MMLU (87.9%), and MathPile (89.8%).

2. How to train DeepSeekMath?

DeepMath is trained on a cluster of 512 NVIDIA A100 80GB GPUs for 30 days. The training data comes from arXiv papers, textbooks, and mathematical websites. The training objectives include next sentence prediction, masked language modeling, and mathematical reasoning.

4. How does DeepSeekMath work?

The following is an example of how DeepSeekMath solves a mathematical problem.

Problem: A train 360 m long is running at a speed of 45 km/hr. In what time will it pass a bridge 140 m long?

Solution: First, we need to convert the speed from km/hr to m/s. Then, we can calculate the total length of the track, which is the sum of the length of the train and the length of the bridge. After that, we can use the formula time = distance/speed to calculate the time it takes for the train to pass the bridge.

Code: Python

from math import *

speed = 45 * 5/18 # speed == 25.0
length_train = 360
length_bridge = 140
total_length = length_train + length_bridge # total_length == 500
time = total_length / speed # time == 20.0
print(time) # Output: 20.0
