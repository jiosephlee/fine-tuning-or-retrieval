According to the paper "Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning", what is Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT)?<|EOT|>Orthogonal Finetuning (also known as OFT) is a finetuning method that prevents knowledge erosion while allowing task-specific adaptation. It achieves this by learning an orthogonal transformation and then transforming the pretrained weights plus the orthogonal matrix. This results in exactly the same forward pass as the pretrained model, but with slightly different weights. The key is that the weights of the pretrained model are close to orthogonal with high probability, so finetuning in this transformed space preserves the orthogonality while allowing the finetuning.

The name "orthogonal finetuning" comes from the fact that the weights become an exact orthogonal transformation after finetuning is complete. This is not just a heuristic — it's mathematically guaranteed by the definition of orthogonality.

OFT has been successfully used to finetune text-to-image diffusion models without significant performance degradation or knowledge erosion.<|endoftext|>
