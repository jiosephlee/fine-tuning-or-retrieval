Text-to-Image Diffussion is a generative adversarial network (GAN) that can generate images from text descriptions. It is a kind of image-to-image translation model. 2. What is Orthogonal Finetuning?

Orthogonal Finetuning is a way to finetune a pretrained model. It is a kindof weight normalization. The key idea is to add a small amount of noise to the pretrained weights. The noise is generated from a multivariate normal distribution with zero mean and identity covariance matrix. The noise is added to the pretrained weights and then the model is finetuned on the target dataset.

3. Why is Orthogonal Finetuing Effective?

The reason why Orthogonal Finetuning is effective is because it helps to prevent overfitting. Overfitting happens when a model learns the training data too well and fails to generalize to new data. By adding noise to the pretrained weights, Orthogonal Finetuning helps to regularize the model and prevent it from learning the training data too well.

5. What are the Limitations of Orthogonal Finetuning

Although Orthogonal Finetuning has been shown to be effective in many applications, it also has some limitations. One limitation is that it can only be used for finetuning pretrained models. If you want to train a model from scratch, you cannot use Orthogonal Finetuning. Another limitation is that it can be computationally expensive. Adding noise to the pretrained weights can increase the number of parameters and the computational cost of the model. Therefore, you need to carefully choose the noise scale to balance performance and efficiency.
