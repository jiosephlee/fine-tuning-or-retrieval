2. What is Orthogonal Finetuning?

Orthogonal Finetuning is a finetuning method for neural networks. It was proposed in the paper "Orthogonal Finetuning of Neural Networks". The finetuning method is to add a small orthogonal matrix to the weight matrix of the neural network. The small orthogonal matrix is randomly initialized. During the training process, the small orthogonal matrix is updated by gradient descent. The update rule of the small orthogonal matrix is:

$$\mathbf{R}=\mathbf{R}+\eta\mathbf{d}\mathbf{d}^T$$

where $\mathbf{R}$ is the small orthogonal matrix, $\mathbf{d}$ is the gradient vector of the loss function, and $\eta$ is the learning rate.

The orthogonal finetuning method can significantly improve the generalization ability of the neural network. It can also significantly reduce the number of training epochs required for finetuning. In addition, the orthogonal finetuning method is easy to implement and has no additional computational overhead.

3.1 From the perspective of linear transformation

$$\mathbf{x}=\mathbf{W}_1\cdots\mathbf{W}_L\mathbf{z}$$

The weight matrix of a neural network is updated by gradient descent:

$$\mathbf{{W}}_l=\mathbf{{W}}_{l}+\eta\nabla_\mathbf{{W}}\mathcal{J}(\mathbf{{W}})$$

where $\mathbb{R}^{{n}_{l-1}\times{n}_{l}}$ is the gradient vector of $\mathcal{J}({\mathbf{W}})$ with respect to $\mathbf{W}_{l}$.

After a large number of training epochs, the weight matrix of the deep neural network will converge to a matrix. This matrix is called the neural tangent kernel of the neural network. For a neural network with one hidden layer, the neural tangent kernel is the weight matrix of this hidden layer. For a neural network wit multiple hidden layers, the neural tangent kernel of this neural network is the weight matrix of its first hidden layer.

The neural tangent kernel characterizes the local linear approximation of a neural network around a set of parameters. It can be used to analyze the generalization ability of a neural network. A small neural tangent kernel indicates a good generalization ability, and a large neural tangent kernel indicates a poor generalization ability.
