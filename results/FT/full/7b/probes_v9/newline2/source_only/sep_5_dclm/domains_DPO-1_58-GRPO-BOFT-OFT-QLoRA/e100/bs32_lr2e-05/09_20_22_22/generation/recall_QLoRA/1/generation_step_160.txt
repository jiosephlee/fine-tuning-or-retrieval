What is QLoRA?

It's a method for finetuning large language models (LLMs) with low-precision weights. It's based on LoRA, which is a method for finetune LLMs with full-precision weights.

Why do we need QLoRA?

Because it's faster to finetune LLM with low-precision weights than with full-precision weights. It also saves memory.

How does QLoRA work?

It works by quantizing the weights of a pretrained LLM with a given precision (e.g. 16-bit) and then finetuning the quantized model with a learning rate that's a fraction of the learning rate used for finetuning the same model with full-precision weights (e.g. 1/2).
