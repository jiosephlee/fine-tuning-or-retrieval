Can you explain what PPO is?<|EOT|>PPO stands for "Proportional-Percentage-of-Accepted-Cost". It is a type of contract used in the insurance industry. It is a type of reimbursement contract between an insurer and a provider. The insurer agrees to pay a percentage of the actual charges incurred by the patient. The provider agrees to accept that percentage as full payment for services rendered. The percentage is determined by the type of service provided and the negotiated rate between the insurer and the provider. For example, if the negotiated rate for an office visit is $100 and the PPO rate is 80%, then the insurer will pay $80 and the provider will accept that as full payment. If the patient has not met their deductible, they are responsible for the remaining amount.<|endoftext|>
