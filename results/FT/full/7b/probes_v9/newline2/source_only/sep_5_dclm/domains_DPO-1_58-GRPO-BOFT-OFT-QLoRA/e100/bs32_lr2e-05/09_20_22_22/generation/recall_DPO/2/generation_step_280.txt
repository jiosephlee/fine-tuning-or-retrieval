The Problem

At a high level, the goal of RLHF is to train a language model that generates responses that human prefer. This sounds simple, but it is actually quite challenging. The challenge comes from the fact that there are usually many ways to generate a valid response for a given context, and we want the language model to choose the best response among all the possible responses. For example, if the context is "Hi, how are you today?", a valid response could be "I am fine, and you?", but a better response could be "I'm good, how about you?". The challenge is to train a language models such that it knows which response is better.

The traditional way to train a language model to produce high-quality responses is to use supervised learning, where we can collect paired data of contexts and responses, and train a language model to maximize the likelihood of a correct response given a context. However, collecting high-quality paired data is often expensive and difficult.
