Let me tell you all about this paper. The paper "Parameter-Efficient Orthogonal Finetuning via Factorization" presents a novel and efficient method for adapting large models, Orthogonal Butterfly (BOFT), which improves parameter-efficiency over standard Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT) and its block-diagonal approach. Orthogonal Butterfly (BOFT) is a parameter-efficient finetuning method that adapts large pretrained models to downstream tasks. BOFT introduces a butterfly structure to the weight matrix, which enables high parameter-efficiency while preserving the ability to recover the pretrained model exactly. BOFT also proposes an efficient algorithm for computing the butterfly factor, which is inspired by the butterfly structure of graphics processing units (GPUs). BOFT outperforms the state-of-the-art parameter-efficient finetuning methods by a large margin in both fully-supervised and few-shot learning. BOFT also has better generalizability than group-regularized finetuning, as assessed in a zero-shot evaluation. BOFT has been successfully used in natural language processing (NLP) applications. For example, BOFT achieves new state-of-the-art results on the GLUE benchmark with finetuning 3% of parameters of RoBERTa. Code is forthcoming.
