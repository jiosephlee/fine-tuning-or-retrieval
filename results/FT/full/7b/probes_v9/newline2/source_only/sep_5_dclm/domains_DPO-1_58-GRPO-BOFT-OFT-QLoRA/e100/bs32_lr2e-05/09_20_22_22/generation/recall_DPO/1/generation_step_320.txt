What is Direct Preference Optimization?

Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) is a method to train language models to generate text that satisfies human preferences. It is a simple and effective method. It is based on the idea that the probability of a language model generating a sentence is proportional to the reward of the sentence. So we can train the language model by maximizing the reward of the generated sentence.

How does DPO work?

DPO is a simple method. It is based the following assumption:

The probability of a language model $p_{\theta}(x)$ generating a sentence $x$ is proportional to the reward $r(x)$ of the sentence $x$.

This assumption is reasonable. Because the reward function $r(x)$ is usually designed to reflect the preference of humans. So the higher the reward of a sentence, the more likely the language model will generate this sentence.
