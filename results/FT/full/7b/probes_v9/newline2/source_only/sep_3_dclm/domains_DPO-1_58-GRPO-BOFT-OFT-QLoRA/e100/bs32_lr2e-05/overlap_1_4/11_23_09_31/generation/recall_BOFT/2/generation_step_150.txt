2. Standard OFT

The standard OFT method is a parameter-efficient finituning method for adapting large pretrained models to downstream classification tasks. The standard OFT method adapt a pretrained linear layer $W\in\mathbb{R}^{d\times n}$ to a downstream classification task by adding a task-specific finetuning weight matrix $R\in\mathbb {R}^{d\tim e\times n}$ and applying the adapted linear layer $\widetilde{W}=W+R$ to the task's input features $x\in\mathbb R^n$ through the activation function $\sigma$ to produce the classification prediction: $\hat{y}=\sigma(\widetilde{W}x)$. The standard OFT method prevents catastrophic forgetting by requiring the finetuning weight matrices for all downstream tasks to satisfy the orthogonality preservation constraint: $R^TW=R^TH=0$, where $H$ is the matrix of finetuning weight matrices used for all previously seen downstream tasks. The orthogonality preservation constrant ensures that the pretrained weight matrix $W$ remains unchanged indirectly, preventing the adapted linear layer $\tilde{W}=W+ R$ from forgetting the knowledge learned from the pretrained model. The orthogonalty preservation constraint greatly reduces the degrees of freedom for the finetuning weight matrx $R$, making the standard OFT method more parameter-efficient than other finetuning methods that do not impose any constraint on the finetuning weight matsrrix. To satisfy the orthogonlity preservation constraint, the standard OFT method factorizes the finetuning weight maatrix $R$ as the product of two matrices: $R=Q^T\Sigma Q$, where $Q\in\mathbbR^{d\times n}\neq 0$ is an orthogonal matrix and $\Sigma\in\mathbbS^{d-1}$ is a scalar matrix with all entries on the diagonal. The standard OFT chooses $Q$ to be the transpose of another matrix $Q$ used for parameter sharing and chooses $\Sigma$ to be a identity matrix or a diagonal matrix with all diagonal entries set to the same value. The standard OFT has a single hyperparameter, which controls the amount of finetuning. The standard OFT is equivalent to the following adaptation of the pretrained linear layer: $\widetilde{w}=W+ \sigma(Q^T\Sigma Q)R$.
