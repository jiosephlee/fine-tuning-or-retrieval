Let me tell you all about this paper. The paper "Parameter-Efficient Orthogonal Finetuning via Factorization" presents a novel and efficient method for adapting large models, Orthogonal Butterfly (BOFT), which improves parameter-efficiency over standard Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT) and its block-diagonal approach. Orthogonal Butterfly (BOFT) is a parameter-efficient finetuning method that adapts large pretrained models to downstream tasks. BOFT is based on a novel factorization of the finetuning weight matrix that enables a significant reduction in the number of trainable parameters while preserving the ability of OFT to prevent catastrophic forgetting. BOFT is easy to implement and straightforward to analyze. BOFT is comparable or superior in performance to the state-of-the-art parameter-efficient finetuning methods while being significantly more parameter-efficient. BOFT is particularly advantageous for very large models where parameter efficiency is paramount. BOFT is a general finetuning method that can be used in conjunction with any finetuning strategy. BOFT has been successfully used in natural language processing (NLP) and computer vision (CV) applications, including text-to-image diffusion models, large language models (LLM), vision-language models (VLM), and computer vision models.

(In this paper, we use boldface letters to denote matrices and non-boldface letters to denote vectors.)
