The key insight of DPO is to frame preference learning as a reinforcement learning problem. In particular, we frame it as a bandit learning problem, where the goal is to learn to pull levers such that a human supervisor is happy. The supervisor's happiness is modeled as a reward, which is provided by the negative logarithm of the human preference distribution of the next lever pull, conditioned on all the levers pulled so far in the episode. $$r_{t} = -\log p_\theta(h_{t+1}| h_1, \dots, h_t)$$

$$\max_{\theta} \sum_{t=1}^T r_t = \sum_{t=0}^T \log (p_\theta(h_{t + 1} | h_1, \ldots, h_t))$$
