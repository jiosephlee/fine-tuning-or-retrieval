    where $\pi$ is the current policy, $\pi_\text{ref}$ is the reference policy, $s_g$ and $a_g$ are the state and action at time step $g$, and $r_g$ is the reward at time step $g$. The reference policy is the same as the current policy, except that it is not updated during training.

    The key difference between the GRPo surrogate objective and the PPO surrogate objective is that the GRPo surrogate objective normalizes the advantage by the maximum possible advantage under the reference policy.
