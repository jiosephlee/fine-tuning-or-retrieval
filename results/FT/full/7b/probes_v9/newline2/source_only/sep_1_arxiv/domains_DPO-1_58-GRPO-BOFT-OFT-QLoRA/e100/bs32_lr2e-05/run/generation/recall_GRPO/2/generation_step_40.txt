PPO is a model-free, off-policy algorithm that uses a surrogate objective to encourage the policy to stay close to a reference policy. The surrogate objective is defined as the ratio of the policy's reward and the reference policy's reward, clipped to a maximum value. This prevents the policy from deviating too far from the reference policy, which helps to stabilize training.

In PPO, the reference policy is typically the current policy, which is estimated using a replay buffer of past interactions between the policy and the environment. This makes GRPO well-suited for training large models on multiple GPUs, as it can scale to a large number of policies without significantly increasing the training time.
