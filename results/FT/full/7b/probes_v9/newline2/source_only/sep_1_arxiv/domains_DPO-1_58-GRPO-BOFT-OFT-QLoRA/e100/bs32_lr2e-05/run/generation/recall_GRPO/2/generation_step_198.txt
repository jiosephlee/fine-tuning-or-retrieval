Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO). It uses a group of policies rather than a single policy to estimate the baseline, reducing the memory footprint and significantly speeding up training. GRPO was initially developed for continuous control and later applied to the reinforcement learning of large language models with great success.
