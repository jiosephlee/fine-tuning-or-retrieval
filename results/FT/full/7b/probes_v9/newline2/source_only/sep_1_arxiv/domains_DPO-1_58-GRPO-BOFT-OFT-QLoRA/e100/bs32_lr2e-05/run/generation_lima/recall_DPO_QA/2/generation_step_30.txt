What is DPO?<|EOT|>Direct Preference Optimization (DPO; Schulman et al., 2017) is a model-based approach to training language models to satisfy human preferences. It is an alternative to the standard reinforcement learning from human preferences (RLHF) pipeline like PPO. In DPO, the language model is trained to maximize the expected reward of its outputs under a learned reward model, which is trained to match human preferences. In contrast, in RLHF, the language model is directly optimized to satisfy human preferences.
