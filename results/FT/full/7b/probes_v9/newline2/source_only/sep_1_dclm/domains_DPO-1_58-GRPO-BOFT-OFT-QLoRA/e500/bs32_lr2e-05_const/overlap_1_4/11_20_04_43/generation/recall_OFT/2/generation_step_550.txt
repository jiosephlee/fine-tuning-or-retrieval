Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT) is a simple yet effective finetuning method that can be used to adapt pretrained text-to-image diffusion models to downstream tasks. OFT finetunes the pretrained model by optimizing its weights with an orthogonal matrix, which ensures that the pretrained neuron directions are preserved. This is in contrast to standard finetuning, which updates the weights with a identity matrix, and therefore changes the neuron directions. The orthogonal matrix is learned from data using an orthogonalization algorithm. OFT has several desirable properties.
