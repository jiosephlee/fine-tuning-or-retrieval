What is orthogonal finetuning?

Orthogonal finetuning is a way to update the orthogonal matrix. It keeps the spectral norm of the matrix unchanged and only updates the angles between the rows of the matrix. The original orthogonal finetuning is defined as:

$$\theta'=\theta-\beta\nabla_{\theta}\mathcal{L}(θ)$$

Where $\theta$ is the orthogonal matrix, $\beta$ is a hyperparameter, $\mathcal{L}$ is the loss function.

What is spectral norm?

Spectral norm is the largest singular value of a matrix. It can be calculated by the square root of the largest eigenvalue of the matrix's covariance matrix.
