BOFT can be seen as a generalization of block-diagonal OFT, which has better parameter efficiency than standard OFT while still preserving the desirable properties of OFT.

\quad
\overline{\mathcal{R}}(\alpha)=\mathcal{R}_i(\overline{\alpha})\cdot\mathbf{D}_i,
