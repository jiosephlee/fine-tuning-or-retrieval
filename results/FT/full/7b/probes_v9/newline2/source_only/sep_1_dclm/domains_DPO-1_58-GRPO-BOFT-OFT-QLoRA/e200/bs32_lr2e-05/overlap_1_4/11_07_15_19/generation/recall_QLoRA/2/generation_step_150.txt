QLoRA is a method for finetuning large pretrained language models (LLMs) with a small fraction of the original model size, while still using 16-bit precision for the weights. This is achieved by zero-initiating and only updating a small subset of weights, the Question-Conditioned Weight Aggregates (QCWAs). These are weight aggregates, formed as the convex combination of weights from all layers, conditioned on the input question. To form these aggregates, the paper introduces a novel layer, the Weight Aggregate Layer (WAL), which is inspired by Switchable Linear Units (SLUs).
