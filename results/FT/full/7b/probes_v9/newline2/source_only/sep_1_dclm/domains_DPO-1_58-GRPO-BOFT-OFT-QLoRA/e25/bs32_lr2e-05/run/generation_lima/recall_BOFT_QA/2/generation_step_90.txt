The paper "Parameter-Efficient Orthogonal Finetuning via Factorization" introduces Orthogonal Butterfly (BOFT) to improve parameter-efficiency over standard Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT) and its block-diagonal approach. What is Orthogonal Butterfly (BOFT)?<|EOT|>Orthogonal Butterfly (BOFT), as shown in the figure below, is a parameter-efficient variant of Orthogonal Finetuning. It is based on the observation that the butterfly matrix in OFT can be factorized into two low-rank matrices. BOFT uses this factorization to reduce the number of trainable parameters.

In particular, BOFT uses a low-rank factorization $B = LL^T$ for the butterfly matrix $B$ in OFT. The rank $r$ of $L$ determines the parameter efficiency of BOFT. The default value of $r$ is 4, which leads to a reduction of about 75% in the number of trainable parameters compared to OFT.<|endoftext|>
