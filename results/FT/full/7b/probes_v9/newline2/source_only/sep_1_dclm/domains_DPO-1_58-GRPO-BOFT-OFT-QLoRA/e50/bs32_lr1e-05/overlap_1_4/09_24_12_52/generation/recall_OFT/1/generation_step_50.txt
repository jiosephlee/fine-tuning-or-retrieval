What is Text-to-Image Diffusions?

Text-to-Image Diffusion is a generative model that can generate images from text. It is a kind of generative adversarial network (GAN). It is trained on a large dataset of paired text and images. The training process is to generate images from text and let the discriminator distinguish whether the generated images are real or fake. The training process is similar to the training process of GAN. The difference is that the generator is a diffusion model. The diffusion model is a kind of generational model that can generate images by gradually adding noise to the image. The training process of the diffusion model is to gradually add noise to the image and let the discriminator distinguish the real image from the noisy image. The training process is like the process of gradually adding noise to the real image and then gradually removing the noise to get the real image. The training process can be expressed as:

$$\begin{aligned} \mathbf {x} = \text {GAN}( \mathbf {z}, \mathbf {y}) \end{aligned}$$

where \(\mathbf {x}\) is the real image, \(\mathbf {z}\) is the noise, and \(\mathbf {y}\) is the text.
