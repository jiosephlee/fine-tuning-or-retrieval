It was proposed by DeepSeekMath team in their paper "DeepSeekMath" for training large language models on mathematical reasoning tasks. 

In PPO, the policy loss is defined as the clipped difference between the advantage estimated from the old policy and the advantage estimated from the new policy. In GRPo, the policy loss is calculated based on the average advantage of each group and the difference of the maximum advantage between two groups. The main idea of GRPo is to calculate the policy loss based on the average advantage and the difference of the maximal advantage between two groups.
