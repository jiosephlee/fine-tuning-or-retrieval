Let me tell you all about this paper. The paper "Parameter-Efficient Orthogonal Finetuning via Factorization" presents a novel and efficient method for adapting large models, Orthogonal Butterfly (BOFT), which improves parameter-efficiency over standard Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT) and its block-diagonal approach. Orthogonal Butterfly (BOFT) is a parameter-efficient finetuning method that uses a butterfly structure to reduce the number of trainable parameters. BOFT is based on the observation that the butterfly structure can be used to efficiently represent the product of multiple orthogonal matrices. BOFT uses a butterfly structure to represent the product of multiple projection matrices, which are used to finetune the weights of a pretrained model. BOFT reduces the number of trainable parameters by a factor of $d$, where $d$ is the dimensionality of the feature maps. BOFT is parameter-efficient, easy to implement, and compatible with existing finetuning methods. BOFT achieves strong performance on image classification and text-to-image generation benchmarks, often outperforming OFT and matching the performance of standard finetuning while using significantly fewer trainable parameters.

The paper "BOFT: Parameter-Efficient Orthogonal finetuning via Factorization"

is on arXiv at https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.13496, and has been accepted to ICLR 2023.
