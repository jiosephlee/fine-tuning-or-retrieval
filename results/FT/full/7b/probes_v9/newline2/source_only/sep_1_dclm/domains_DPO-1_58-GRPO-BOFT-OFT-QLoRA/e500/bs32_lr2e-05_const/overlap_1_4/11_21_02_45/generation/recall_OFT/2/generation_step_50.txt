Let me tell you all about this paper. The paper "Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning" presents a novel method for adapting diffusion models, Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT), which preserves crucial geometric properties of neurons unlike standard finetuning or additive methods like LoRA. Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT) is a simple yet effective method for finetuning text-to-image diffusion models. OFT finetunes the diffusion model by performing orthogonal transformation on the weights of the model. This preserves the pairwise angles between neurons, which is shown to be crucial for preserving the semantic knowledge in the pretrained diffusion model. OFT is provably stable and converges to a local minimum of the finetuning loss. OFT is empirically shown to be at least as effective as strong baselines like LoRA while being much simpler to implement. OFT is also much faster to train than LoRA. The code for reproducing the results is available at https://github.com/zhuxintian/OFT.
