Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT) is a simple yet effective finetuning method that can be used to adapt text-to-image diffusion models to downstream tasks. OFT finetunes a pretrained diffusion model by optimizing its weights with an orthogonal matrix, which ensures that the pretrained neuron directions are preserved. This is in contrast to standard finetuning, which updates weights with a identity matrix, and therefore changes the neuron directions. The orthogonal matrix is learned through a simple objective that encourages the finetuned model to perform well on the downstream task while preserving the pairwise dot products of neurons in the pretrained model. These pairwise dot products capture important geometric properties of the neurons, such as their activation patterns and receptive fields. As a result, OFT is able to finetune diffusion models while preserving their expressive power and avoiding collapse.
