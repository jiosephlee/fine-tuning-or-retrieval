Overview of BOFT

We start with a high-level overview of BOFT. BOFT is a parameter-efficient finituning method for adapting large pretrained models to downstream classification tasks. It refactors the weight matrix of a layer into multiple smaller matrices with the help of the butterfly structure, as shown in Figure 1. BOFT recursively applies this matrix factorization to all layers of the pretrained model, resulting in an orthogonal finetuning structure that is more parameter-efficient than the block-diagonal structure used in standard OFT and group-adaptive OFT. BOFT has no hyperparameters and is easy to implement. It can be plugged into any pretrained model and downstream task with little overhead.
