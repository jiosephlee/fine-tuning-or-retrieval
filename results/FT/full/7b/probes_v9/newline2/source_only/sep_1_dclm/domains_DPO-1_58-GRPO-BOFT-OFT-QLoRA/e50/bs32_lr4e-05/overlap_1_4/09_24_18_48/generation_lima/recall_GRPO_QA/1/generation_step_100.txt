According to the paper "DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models", what is Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)?<|EOT|>Group Relative Policy Optimization (GR PO) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO). In particular, it is designed for training large-scale reinforcement learning (RL) agents with a moderate number of training actions. It balances exploitation of the current state for reward and exploitation of the group of the current state for learning signal, and it does not require adding a per-time-step term to the loss like PPO does.

In each training episode, GRPO maintains a group for the agent based on the current state of the agent. It then calculates the advantage using the rewards from the end of the episode and a shared baseline for all states in the same group. Finally, it normalizes the advantage by the maximum advantage in the group to scale the learning signal.
