1. Mathematical Reasoning in OpenLanguage Models

Mathematical reasoning is an important but challenging task for language models. It requires models to understand the mathematical problems, to reason about the problems, and to generate the solutions.

There are mainly three types of mathematical reasoning tasks:

  • Multiple-choice problems: Given a question and several answers, the task is to select the correct answer.
  • Formulation problems: The task is to generate the solution given a mathematical problem and a step-by-step solution.
  • Proof problems: The task is similar to formulation problems, except that the step-by-step solution is required to be a formal proof.

2. Approaches to Mathematical Reasoning

There are mainly three types approaches to mathematical reasoning:

  • Rule-based approaches: Use hand-crafted rules to solve mathematical problems.
  • Supervised learning approaches: Train a model using data of problems and solutions.
  • Reinforcement learning approaches: Train a policy model using reinforcement learning from human feedback.

3. Mathematical Reasoning in LLMs

There are mainly three ways to enhance the mathematical reasoning ability of LLMs:

  • Use a mathematical reasoning model to assist the LLMs.
  • Enhance the general reasoning ability of LLM.
  • Train a mathematical reasoning LLM from scratch.

4. Mathematical Reasoning in GPT-4

GPT-4 uses the third way to enhance the mathematical reasoning of LLMs. It trains a mathematical reasoning LLM with a large-scale dataset of mathematical problems and solutions. The dataset is called MathPile. MathPile is a multilingual dataset that contains more than 1.9B math problems and human-written solutions. It's 30 times larger than the dataset used to train DeepSeekMath.

5. Training and Evaluating GPT-4

The training data of GPT-4 is mainly from Common Crawl. In the training process, the data related to mathematics is heavily sampled. The training objectives are similar to the objectives of DeepSeekMath. Besides the mathematical reasoning task, GPT-4 is also evaluated on other language tasks, including translation, summarization, Q&A, and code.

The evaluation results on the competition-level benchmarks are impressive. GPT-4 achieves 82.9% on the competition-level MATH benchmark, surpassing the score (81.0%) of GPT-4's predecessor GPT-3.
