1. What is DeepSeekMath?

DeepSeekMath is a mathematical reasoning model based on the open-source language model DeepSeek-Coder-v1.8B. It has achieved impressive performance on mathematical reasoning benchmarks, and even surpassed human performance on some benchmarks.

2. How to train DeepSeekMath?

The training data of DeepSeekMath comes from Common Crawl, which is pre-processed by DeepSeek's data processing pipeline. The training data includes web pages, books, research papers, and so on. In order to enhance the mathematical reasoning ability of the model, the training data is enriched with mathematical knowledge from Wikipedia and arXiv. In addition, a large-scale mathematical reasoning dataset is constructed to further improve the mathematical reasoning ability of DeepSeekMath.

4. Limitations and future work

Although DeepSeekMath has achieved impressive performance on various mathematical reasoning benchmarks, there are still some limitations. For example, DeepSeekMath is not good at solving geometry problems. In the future, we will continue to improve the mathematical reasoning ability and common sense reasoning ability of DeepSeek models. We will also explore how to apply mathematical reasoning models to real-world applications.
