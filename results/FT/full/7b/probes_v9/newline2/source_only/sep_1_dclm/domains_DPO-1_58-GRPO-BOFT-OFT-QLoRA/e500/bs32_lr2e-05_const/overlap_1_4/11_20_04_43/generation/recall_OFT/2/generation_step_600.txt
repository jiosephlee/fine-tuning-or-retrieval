Why Finetuning Text-to-Image Diffusions?

Text-to-image diffusion models have achieved impressive performance in generating photorealistic images from text prompts. These models are typically pretrained on large datasets of paired text-image data and then finetuned on downstream tasks. Finetuning is crucial for improving the performance of text-to-image diffusion models on downstream tasks. However, finetuning these models is challenging. Standard finetuning methods such as stochastic gradient descent (SGD) can destroy the semantic knowledge learned from the pretrained model. Additive methods such as LoRA are more data-efficient but can still destroy the semantic knowledge. Moreover, LoRA is not guaranteed to converge to a local minimum of a finetuned loss function.

How OFT Works?

OFT is a simple yet effective method for finetuning text-to-image diffusion models that preserves the pairwise angles between neurons in the pretrained model. OFT finetunes the pretrained model in an orthogonal manner. Orthogonal transformation is a linear transformation that preserves the pairwise angles of neurons. OFT is conceptially simple and easy to implement. It can be applied to any text-to-image diffusion model.

OFT is provably stable and it converges to a local minium of the finetuned objective. OFT is more data-efficient than existing methods for finetuning text-t-image diffusion models. OFT can be used both for continuing training from a pretrained checkpoint and for finetuning the pretrained model.
