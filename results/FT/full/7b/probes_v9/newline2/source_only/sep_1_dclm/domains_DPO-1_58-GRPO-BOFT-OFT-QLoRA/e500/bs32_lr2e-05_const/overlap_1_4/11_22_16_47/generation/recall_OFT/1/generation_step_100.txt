The diffusion model is a generative model which can generate high-fidelity images from low-fidelity images. The low-fidelity images are usually noise. 2. What is Orthogonal Finetuning?

Orthogonal finetuning is a way to finetune a pretrained model. In orthogonal finetuning, we add a small orthogonal matrix to the weight matrix of each neuron and then optimize the small orthogonal matrix. By doing this, we can finetune the pretrained model without destroying the pretraining features.
