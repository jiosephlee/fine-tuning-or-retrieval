Can you explain what PPO is?<|EOT|>PPO stands for Preferred Provider Organization. It is a type of health insurance plan that combines elements of HMOs and traditional indemnity plans. Like an HMO, you have a primary care physician who provides your routine care and acts as a gatekeeper to other specialists. However, you can also see specialists outside of the network without a referral, like you can with a traditional indemnity plan. The trade-off is that you pay more for these services when you go outside the network.

PPOs typically have lower out-of-pocket costs for in-network services than HMOs, but higher out-of-pocket costs for out-of-network services than HMO. They also typically have higher premiums than HMOs.<|endoftext|>
