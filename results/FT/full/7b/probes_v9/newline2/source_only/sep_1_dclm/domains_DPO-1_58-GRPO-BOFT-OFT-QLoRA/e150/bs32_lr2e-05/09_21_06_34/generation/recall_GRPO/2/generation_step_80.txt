Recent advances in natural language processing have led to the development of increasingly powerful open language models (OLMs). **Group Relative Policy Optimization (GR PO):**

Proximal Policy Optimization (PGPO) is a reinforcement learning algorithm used to train reinforcement learning agents. It works by estimating the advantage function, which measures how much better one policy is than another, and then updating the policies accordingly. However, computing the advantage function can be computationally expensive, especially for large-scale models like OLMs. To overcome this challenge, the authors propose a variant of PGPO called Group Relative Policy Optimization, or GRPO.

In GRPO, policies are grouped together based on their similarities, and the advantage function is estimated only for pairs of policies within the same group. This reduces the memory requirements from being per-policy-sample to being per-group-of-policies. Additionally, GRPO uses a novel normalization strategy that further improves its efficiency and stability.
