Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) that reduces the memory requirements from O(1/ǫ^2) to O(1/ǃ). It does so by maintaining a single set of target parameters for a group of policies, rather than one set per policy, and by using a moving average of rewards to estimate the advantage function. This makes GRPO well-suited for training large models, such as the 70B parameter DeepSeekMath model presented in this paper. Evaluations show that DeepSeekMath is much stronger at solving math problems than any previous LLM, including the 65B parameter GPT-4.
