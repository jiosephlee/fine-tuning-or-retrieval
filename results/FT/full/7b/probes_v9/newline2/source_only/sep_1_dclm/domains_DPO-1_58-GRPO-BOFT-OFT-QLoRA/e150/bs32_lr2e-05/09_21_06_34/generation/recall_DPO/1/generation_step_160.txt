What is Direct Preference Optimization?

Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) is a method to train language models to generate text that satisfies human preferences. It is based on the idea of using a reward model to estimate the reward of a given text generated by a language model. The reward model is trained with reinforcement learning from human feedback. The language model is then fine-tuned with maximum likelihood estimation to match the reward model.

Why do we need DPO?

We can train language models with reinforcement learning from human preferences. But it is hard to collect enough high-quality human feedback. So, we need a method to generate synthetic human feedback automatically. DPO is such a method.

How does DPO work?

DPO uses a reward model to estimate rewards of texts generated by a language model, and then fine-tunes the language model to maximize the reward predicted by the reward model. The reward model can be trained with reinforcement learning from real human feedback or with supervised learning from human ratings.
