High-level overview

As shown in the figure, QLoRA has three phases: quantization, warmup, and training.

In the quantization phase, the pretrained LLM is quantized to a lower bit precisions. In this paper, the authors consider 4-bit (b4) and 8-bit (b8) precisions.

In the warmup phase, the quantized LLM is trained with a very small learning rate for a few epochs to mitigate the effect of quantization.

In the training phase, the quantized LoRA is trained with a normal learning rate for a certain number of epochs.

In the quantization phase. the pretrained LLM is first linearly transformed by a layer-shared weight matrix to the canonical space. Then it's quantized to the discrete space of a target precision.
