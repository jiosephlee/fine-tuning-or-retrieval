What is Orthogonal Finetuning?

Orthogonal Finetuning is a method for training neural networks. It was introduced in the paper "Orthogonal Finetuning of Neural Networks". The idea is to use an orthogonal matrix to update the weights of a neural network. This has the advantage of preserving the spectral norm of the weight matrix, which can help to prevent overfitting.

What is a Block-diagonal Orthogonal Matrix?

A block-diagonal orthogonal matrix is a matrix that has zeros on the diagonal except for a few blocks. The blocks are usually square matrices of the same size. The blocks are orthogonal, which means that they are invertible and their inverse is equal to their transpose.

What is a Low-rank Orthogonal Matrix?

A low-rank orthogonal matrix is a matrix with a small number of non-zero eigenvalues. This means that the matrix can be approximated by a matrix with fewer non-zero entries.
