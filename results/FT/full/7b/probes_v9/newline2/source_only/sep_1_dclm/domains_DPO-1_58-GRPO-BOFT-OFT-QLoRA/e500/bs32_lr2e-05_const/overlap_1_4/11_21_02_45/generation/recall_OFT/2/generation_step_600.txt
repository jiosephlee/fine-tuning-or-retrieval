The paper "Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning" presents a novel method for adapting diffusion models, Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT), which preserves crucial geometric properties of neurons unlike standard finetuning or additive methods like LoRA. Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT) is a simple yet effective finetuning method that can be applied to any pretrained diffusion model. It finetunes the pretrained diffusion model by learning orthogonal transformations for each neuron. OFT finetunes the pretrained model while preserving the pairwise angles between neurons, which are crucial for representing the semantic meaning of the data.
