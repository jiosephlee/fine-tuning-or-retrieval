What is BitNet b1.58?<|EOT|>BitNet b1.58 is a new type of language model that offers performance competitive to full precision (FP16) models while only using 1.58 bits for each parameter. It is obtained by training a standard 16-bit model on a dataset of compressed activations. This technique, called BitFusion, fuses the information from the 16-bit model and the compression into a single model that can be used for inference without any additional decoding step.
