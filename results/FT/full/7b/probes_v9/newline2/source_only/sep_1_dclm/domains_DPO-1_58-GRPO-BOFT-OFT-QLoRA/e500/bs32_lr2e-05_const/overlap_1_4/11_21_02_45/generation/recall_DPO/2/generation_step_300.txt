What is RLHF?

Before we dive into DPO, let's first take a step back and understand the current state-of-the-art in training language models to satisfy human preference, which is the RLHF pipeline.

The RLHF pipeline typically consists of three steps:

  1. Preference Collection: Collect human preferences over generated text using a preference elicitation interface. For example, the interface could be a button-based interface where humans are shown two generated texts and asked to click on the text they prefer.
  2. Reward Modeling: Use the preference data to train a reward model that approximates the true human preference function. The reward model is typically a neural network that takes in a generated text and outputs a reward value that represents how well the text satisfies the human preference.
  3. RL Training: Train the language model using reinforcement learning (RL) with the reward signal from the reward model. For example, the RL algorithm could be Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO).

The RLHF pipeline has been very successful in training language models to generate high-quality text that satisfies human preferences. However, the RLHF pipeline is also quite complex and involves a number of steps that are not always straightforward to implement. Moreover, the RLHF pipeline can be slow and inefficient, especially when training large language models.
