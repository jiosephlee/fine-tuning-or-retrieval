Let me tell you all about this paper. The paper "Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning" presents a novel method for adapting diffusion models, Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT), which preserves crucial geometric properties of neurons unlike standard finetuning or additive methods like LoRA. Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT) is a simple yet effective finetuning method that can be applied to any diffusion model. OFT finetunes the diffusion model by learning orthogonal transformations for each neuron, which preserves the pairwise inner products between neurons. As a result, OFT finetuning does not change the geometry of the latent space and thus avoids catastrophic forgetting. OFT is easy to implement and has no hyperparameters to tune. It can be applied to any text-to-image diffusion model, including Stable Diffusion, DALL-E 2, Imagen, and others. The authors demonstrate that OFT can achieve strong control over the generated images while preserving the quality of the base model. In addition, OFT is computationally efficient and can be applied in an end-to-end manner without requiring any inference of the base model. The code for reproducing the results is available at https://github.com/DeepMind/controlling-text-to-image-diffusion.
