2. Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF)

Reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) is an effective approach to training language models with the ability of mathematical reasoning. In RLHF, a reward model is trained to score the outputs generated by the language model. The reward model is usually a neural network. A human annotator is asked to score the outputs generated both by the language model and the reward model. The difference between the human scores and the reward model scores are used as the training signals of the reward model.
