What is Parameter-Efficient Orthogonal finetuning?

Parameter-Efficient Orthoganal finetuning is a method to improve the performance of pretrained models. It uses a factorized weight matrix to update the pretrained model. The factorized weight matrix is composed of multiple orthogonal matrices. The orthogonal matrices are updated by Orthogonal Finetuning.

Why does Parameter-Efficient Orthoganl finetuning work?

I think there are two reasons:

  1. The factorized weight matrices can well preserve the global structure of the pretrained model.
  2. The orthogonal matrices can well preserve the local information of the pretrained model.
