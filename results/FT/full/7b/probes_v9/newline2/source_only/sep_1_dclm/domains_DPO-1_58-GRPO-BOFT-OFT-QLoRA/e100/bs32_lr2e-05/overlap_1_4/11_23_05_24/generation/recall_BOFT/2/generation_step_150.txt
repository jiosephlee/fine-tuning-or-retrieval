Let me tell you all about this paper. The paper "Parameter-Efficient Orthogonal Finetuning via Factorization" presents a novel and efficient method for adapting large models, Orthogonal Butterfly (BOFT), which improves parameter-efficiency over standard Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT) and its block-diagonal approach. Orthogonal Butterfly (BOFT) is a parameter-efficient finetuning method that adapts large pretrained models to downstream tasks. BOFT introduces task-specific orthogonal parameters via a butterfly structure with skip connections, which significantly reduces the number of trainable parameters without losing the fast convergence and strong generalization of OFT. To understand the performance of BOFT, we conduct a comprehensive empirical study, revealing the important factors of BOFT, including the orthogonal basis learning with skip connections and the factorization for reducing the number of parameters. We also propose an improved version of BOFT, called Group BOFT, that further reduces the number of parameters with negligible performance drop. Group BOFT learns task-specific orthogonal transformations for each convolution group with group convolution, and it achieves better generalizability and requires less training iteration than BOFT. Our main contributions are listed below:
1. We propose a novel parameter-efficient orthogonal finetuning method, Orthogonal Butterfly (Boft), for adapting large pretrained models to downstream datasets.
2. Boft learns task-specific orthogonal transformations with a butterfly structure that contains skip connections, significantly reducing the number of trainable parameters compared with the block-diagonal approach.
3. Through a comprehensive empirical study, we analyze the important factors of Boft and propose an improved version, Group Boft, which achieves stronger performance and requires less training iteration.
4. Experiment results on vision-language tasks show that both Boft and Group Boft outperform the block-diagonal approach and other competitive parameter-efficient finetuning methods.
