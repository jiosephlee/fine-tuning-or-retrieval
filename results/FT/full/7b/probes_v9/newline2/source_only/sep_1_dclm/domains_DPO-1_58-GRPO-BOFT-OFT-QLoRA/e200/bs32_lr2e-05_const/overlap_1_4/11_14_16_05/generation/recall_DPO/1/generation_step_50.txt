What is Direct Preference Optimization?

Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) is a method to train language models to generate text that satisfies a given preference. It is based on the idea that a language model can be used as a reward model. In other words, we can use a language model to score text samples. The higher the score, the more likely the text sample is generated by the language model. So, we can train a language model to generate text that satisfies our preferences by maximizing the reward score.
