The paper "Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning" presents a novel method for adapting diffusion models, Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT), which preserves crucial geometric properties of neurons unlike standard finetuning or additive methods like LoRA. Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT) is a simple yet effective finetuning method for text-to-image diffusion models. It finetunes the pretrained diffusion model in an orthogonal manner such that the pretrained semantic manifold is preserved. OFT achieves strong performance in both semantically preserving finetuning and controllable generation. It outperforms strong baselines including LoRA, Maha, and standard finetuning in both tasks. Furthermore, OFT is efficiently parallelizable and thus can be scaled to large models. Code is available at https://github.com/openai/orthogonal.

The key idea is to finetune the weights while preserving the pairwise angles between neurons. This is achieved by constraining the finetuning weights to lie in an orthogonal complement of the span of the pretrained weights. The authors show that this finetuning method is effective for both semantically-preserving finetuning and controllability.
