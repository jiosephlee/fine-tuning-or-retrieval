Let me tell you all about this paper. The paper "DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models" presents a novel and efficient method for improving mathematical capabilities in language models, Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO), instead of the standard memory-intensive PPO algorithm. Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) that uses a group of policies to estimate the baseline for each policy. GRPO is more memory efficient than PPO because it does not store the baseline for each policy but instead uses the average of a group of policies as the baseline for each policy.

The DeepSeekMath model is trained on a dataset of 2.5B math problems from OpenWeb and 800M problems from textbooks, with a total of 3.2B problems. The model is evaluated on a benchmark of 144K problems from GSM8K, MATH, MMLU-math, and MATH-BIG. The results show that DeepSeekMath 7B outperforms all open-source models and is competitive with closed-source models. In particular, DeepSeekMath 7b beats GPT-4 on GSM8K and MMLU-math and matches its performance on MATH and MATH-BIG.
