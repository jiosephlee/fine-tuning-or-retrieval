Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) that uses a group of policies to estimate the baseline, instead of using a single policy. This approach reduces the memory requirements of PPO from O(n) to O(1), where n is the number of policies. GRPO has been successfully applied to reinforcement learning problems with large state spaces, such as continuous control and robotics. Our results show that GRPO is able to significantly improve the performance of open language models on the MATH benchmark.
