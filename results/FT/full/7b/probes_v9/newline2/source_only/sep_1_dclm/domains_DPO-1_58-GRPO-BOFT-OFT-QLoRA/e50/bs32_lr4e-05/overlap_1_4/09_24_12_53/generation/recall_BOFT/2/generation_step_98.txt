They have been used to parameterize dense matrices [25,26], [27,28] invertible matrices [29,30], and singular value diagonal matrices [31]. Butterfly structures can also be used to parameterize bilinear forms [32,33]. A butterfly structure for orthogonal matrices has not been studied before.
