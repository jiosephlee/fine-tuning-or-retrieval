According to the paper "DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models", what is Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)?<|EOT|>Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPOP) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO). It was introduced by OpenAI in their paper "OpenAI Five: An AI System Built to Play the Competitive Multiplayer Game Dota 2" (2017).

In PPO, the policy gradient is clipped to a value close to 1. This can be problematic when training multiple policies simultaneously, as it may cause them to converge to similar values. To address this issue, GRPOP uses a group-specific clipping value for each policy, which is calculated based on the mean and standard deviation of the per-clip values for that policy. This allows the policies to explore different regions of the state space, increasing the diversity of the resulting behaviors.
