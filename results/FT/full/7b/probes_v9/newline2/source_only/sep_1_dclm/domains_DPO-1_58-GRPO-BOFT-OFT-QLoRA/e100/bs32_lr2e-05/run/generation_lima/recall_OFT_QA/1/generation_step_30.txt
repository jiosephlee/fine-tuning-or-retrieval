It works by adding an additional term to the standard finetuning loss, which encourages the learned weights to be orthogonal. This additional term is given by:

$$\frac{1}{2} \sum_{i \neq j} \text{trace}(W_i W_j^\top - \mathbf{I})$$

where $W_i$ are the learned weights at each training iteration.

The reason why OFT works is that it prevents the learned weights from becoming correlated, which would cause the model to memorize the training data and lose its ability to generate novel images.
