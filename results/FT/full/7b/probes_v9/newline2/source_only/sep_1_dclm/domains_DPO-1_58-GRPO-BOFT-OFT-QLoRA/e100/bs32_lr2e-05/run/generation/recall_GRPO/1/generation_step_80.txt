1. What is DeepSeekMath?

DeepSeekMath is a mathematical reasoning model based on the DeepSeek-Coder-Large model. It has 65B parameters and is trained on 4P100 servers for 150 days. The training data includes 1.5TB of mathematical texts from Common Crawl, 7,000GB of arXiv papers, 13,000GB of books from Project Gutenberg, and 13,000GB from textbooks from CK12. In addition, it is trained with 7B math questions and answers from OpenWeb and 3B questions and answers from Reddit. The training objective is to maximize the likelihood of a question-answer pair. To improve the performance of mathematical reasoning, DeepSeekMath uses several techniques, such as:

  • Using a mathematical tokenizer to tokenize mathematical expressions.
  • Training the model on mathematical texts with a higher frequency of mathematical tokens.
  • Fine-tuning the pretrained model on mathematical datasets.
  • Training the pretrained model on a large-scale mathematical dataset.
  • Optimizing the training objective to improve the performance of mathematical tasks.
