Motivation of the paper

The paper is about finetuning neural networks. In the finetuning stage, we usually use gradient descent to update the weights of the neural network. The paper proposes a new way to finetune the neural network. The new way is to multiply the weight matrix with a low-rank matrix. The low-rank matrix is called the finetuning matrix. The motivation of the paper is that the finetuning matrix can be factorized into multiple matrices. The paper proposes a way to factorize the finetuning matrix.

Main idea of the paper

The main idea of the paper is to factorize the fineting matrix. The factorization is based on the orthogonal matrix. The orthogonal matrix is a square matrix whose columns and rows are orthonormal vectors. The paper proposes a method to generate the orthogonal matrix. The method is to multiply a random matrix with a matrix whose determinant is 1. The random matrix is a matrix whose elements are randomly generated. The paper also proposes a method to generate a low-rank matrix.

def generate_low_rank_matrix(n, k, rank):
