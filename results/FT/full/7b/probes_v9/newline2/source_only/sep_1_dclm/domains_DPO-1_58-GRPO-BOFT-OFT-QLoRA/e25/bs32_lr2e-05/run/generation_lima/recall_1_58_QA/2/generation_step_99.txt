What is BitNet b1.58?<|EOT|>BitNet is a family of low-bitwidth language models that are trained on the same dataset as full-precision models, but with a different loss function that penalizes bit errors. The BitNet family includes models with 1.58, 1.32, 1.18, and 1.05 bits per weight. The 1.58-bit model is the most efficient model in the family, and it is competitive with full-precision models in terms of zero-shot generalization and few-shot learning.

BitNet b1.58 is a 1.58-bit language model with 540B parameters. It is trained on 3.3TB of text data, which is about 6x the size of the original General Language Model 4B (GLM-4B). The training data includes the entire Common Crawl up to May 2019, the entire English Wikipedia, and an additional 2TB of web text. The model is trained for 250,000 steps using a batch size of 8192 samples and a learning rate of 5e-5.
