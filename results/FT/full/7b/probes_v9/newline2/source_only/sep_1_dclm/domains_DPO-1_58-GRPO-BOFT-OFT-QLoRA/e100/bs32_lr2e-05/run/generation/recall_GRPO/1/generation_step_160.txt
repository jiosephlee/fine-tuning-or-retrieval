1. What is DeepSeekMath?

DeepSeekMath is a mathematical reasoning model based on the DeepSeek-Coder-Large model. It has 65B parameters and is trained on 4T tokens from Common Crawl, which is about 3 times the training data of GPT-4. The training data includes 1.5T tokens of scientific articles from arXiv, PubMed Central, and HAL, and 1.5T mathematical tokens from textbooks, lecture notes, and StackExchange posts. In addition, DeepSeekMath is trained with 100B tokens of arXiv papers and 10B tokens of StackExchange posts.

3. Training Data Analysis

The training data of DeepSeekMath is analyzed in detail. Here are some interesting findings:

  • The training data of DeepSeek-Math includes 1.5TB tokens of mathematical content, which is about 10 times the amount of training data used by GPT-4.
  • The training data is highly biased towards English. Only 0.3% of the tokens are in other languages.
  • The training tokens include 1.3M instances of solutions to math problems, which is about 5 times the number of instances used by GPT-3.
  • The training content includes 1.2M instances of proofs, which is about 2 times the number of instances of proofs used by GPT-2 and GPT-3.
