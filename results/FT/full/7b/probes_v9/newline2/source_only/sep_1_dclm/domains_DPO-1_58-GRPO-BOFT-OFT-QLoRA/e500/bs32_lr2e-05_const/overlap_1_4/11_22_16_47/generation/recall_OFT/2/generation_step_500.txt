Let me tell you all about this paper. The paper "Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning" presents a novel method for adapting diffusion models, Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT), which preserves crucial geometric properties of neurons unlike standard finetuning or additive methods like LoRA. Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT) is a simple yet effective finetuning method that can be used to adapt text-to-image diffusion models to downstream tasks. OFT finetunes a pretrained diffusion model by learning layer-shared orthogonal transformations. OFT has several desirable properties: it exactly preserves the pretrained spectral norm of neurons, approximately preserves the pairwise angles between neurons, and does not introduce new modes. We demonstrate the effectiveness of OFT in finetuning various text-to-image diffusion models including Stable Diffusion v1.5, Stable Diffusion v2.0, and Stable Diffusion v2+ on the COCO dataset. On the validation split of COCO, OFT-trained models achieve an accuracy of 62.8% compared to 60.8% of the original model. More significantly, OFT can be easily combined with other finetuning methods, giving a further boost in performance. For example, OFT combined with standard finetuning achieves an accuracy of 65.0%, outperforming the original model and OFT alone. Code is available at https://github.com/facebookresearch/oft.
