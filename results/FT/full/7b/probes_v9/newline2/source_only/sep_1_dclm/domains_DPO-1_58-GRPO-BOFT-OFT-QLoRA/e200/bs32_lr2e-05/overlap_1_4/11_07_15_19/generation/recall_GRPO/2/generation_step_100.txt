MATH Benchmark

The MATH benchmark is a high-stakes benchmark for mathematical reasoning in language models. It consists of two subtasks: competition-level and research-level. The competition-level subtask uses the same data as the competition version of the GSM8K benchmark, which is a subset of the GSM8K dataset with human-annotated answers. This subset includes problems from GSM8K that have human-annotated answers, as well as problems from other benchmarks such as LSQUE and LAMBADA. The research-level subtask uses the full version of the LSMR dataset, which is the largest publicly available mathematical dataset.
