What is DPO?<|EOT|>Direct Preference Optimization (DPO; Bai, et al. 2022) is a model-based approach for training language models to adhere to human preferences. It does not use reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) and does not rely on the assumption that the distribution of rollouts generated by the language model is the same as the preference distribution. Instead, it fits a preference model from the data and then optimizes the language model using this preference model.

In more detail, DPO does the following:
1. Sample preferences $\pi$(A>B) and language model distributions $\pi$(B) for many (many!) pairs of responses A, B.
2. Fit a statistical model of the preferences, e.g. a Bradley-Terry model, using the preference samples.
3. Fit a statistical model (e.g. a Kalman filter) of the language model using the language model samples.
4. Use the models of the preference and language model distributions to infer the reward at each step under the true preference model.
5. Optimize the language model by maximizing the expected reward under the preference model, i.e. reparametrize the language model as $\pi_{\theta}(B|A;\pi_{\phi})$ and optimize $\theta$.
6. Repeat 1-5 with many different pairs of responses A, Bs until done.<|endoftext|>
