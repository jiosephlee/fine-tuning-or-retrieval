Let me tell you all about this paper. The paper "Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning" presents a novel method for adapting diffusion models, Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT), which preserves crucial geometric properties of neurons unlike standard finetuning or additive methods like LoRA. Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT) is a simple yet effective finetuning method that can be applied to any pretrained text-to-image diffusion model. It finetunes the pretrained model by learning layer-shared orthogonal transformations, which preserves the pairwise angles between neurons. OFT can be easily combined with existing finetuning methods, e.g., warmup finetuning and learning rate decay. We demonstrate the effectiveness of OFT in finetuning Stable Diffusion v1.5 and Stable Diffusion v1.x models. On the task of generating photorealistic image captions, OFT significantly outperforms the strong baseline of finetuning Stable Diffusions v1.5 using warmup finetuning with a decaying learning rate. With OFT, the generated captions become more diverse, realistic, and coherent. In particular, OFT significantly outperformes the decaying learning rate method by 23.2% and 20.0% absolute improvement in terms of BLEU-4 and METEOR scores, respectively. In the task of semantic segmentation, OFT significantly outperfors the decaying learning rate basline by 10.0% and 5.0% absolute improvement In terms of mean IoU and mean F1 score. Notably, OFT can also be used to finetune diffusion models trained with image tokens. For example, OFT significantly outpeforms the decaying learning rate baseline by 5.5% and 3.9% absolute improvement in termsof mean IoU and meanF1 score on the segmentation task using Diffusion for Semantic Segmentation (DFSS). As for the quality of the generated images, OFT significantly outpers the decaying learning rate by 17.5% and 10.0% absolute improvementin terms of Frchet Inception Distance (FID) and Learned Perceptual Image Patch Similarity (LPIPS), respectively. Moreover, we demonstrate that OFT can also be combined with LoRA. The combined method, OFT+LoRA, achieves even better performance than OFT and the decaying learning rate methods.
