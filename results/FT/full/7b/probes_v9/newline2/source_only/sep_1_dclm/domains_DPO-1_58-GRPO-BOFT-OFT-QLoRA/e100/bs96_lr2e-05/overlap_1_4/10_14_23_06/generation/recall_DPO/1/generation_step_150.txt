What is Direct Preference Optimization?

Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) is a method to train language models to generate text that satisfies human preferences. It's a simple and effective way to train language models to be more controllable.

How does DPO work?

The basic idea of DPO is to use the reward model to provide feedback to the language model. The language model generates text, then the reward model gives a reward score to the generated text. The language model will adjust its parameters based on the reward score. After many iterations, the language model will generate text that satisfies the reward model.

There are two ways to provide feedback to the model:

  • Supervised learning: The reward model provides a reward score for each generated text. The language models will update its parameters based on the rewards.
  • Reinforcement learning: The reward model will give a reward score to the language model. Then the language model will update its parameters based the reward score.

The reward model is usually a neural network. It takes the generated text as input and outputs a reward score. The reward score represents how well the generated text satisfies the reward model. The higher the reward score, the better the generated text.
