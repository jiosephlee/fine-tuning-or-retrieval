The paper "The Era of 1-bit LLMs: All Large Language Models are in 1.58 Bits" introduces BitNet b1.58 as a cost-effective alternative to full-precision (FP16) models. What is BitNet b1.58?<|EOT|>BitNet b1.58 is a new type of language model that offers performance competitive to state-of-the-art full precision (FP16) models while only using 1.58 bits for representing each parameter. It is obtained by training a standard LSTM model with the Adam optimizer on the BigBench dataset for a long time (400B tokens). 

The name BitNet is meant to evoke the famous IBM phrase "IBM PC" and remind people that just like the PC opened up the personal computer market, this new parameter efficiency could enable wide adoption of LLMs.

The paper also introduces BitNet b1.* (a family of models with different bit-precisions) and the BitNet framework.<|endoftext|>
