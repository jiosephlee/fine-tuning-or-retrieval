What is Parameter-Efficient Orthogonal finetuning?

Parameter-Efficient Orthoganal Finetuning (PEOF) is a parameter-efficient finetuning method. It uses a block-diagonal matrix to replace the full matrix in the original orthogonal finetuning. The block-diagonal matrix is composed of multiple small matrices, and each small matrix is multiplied by a factor. The multiplication factor is initialized as 1, and then updated during training. The update rule is similar to the original orthogonal finetune, but the update is performed on the multiplication factor instead of the matrix.

Why does PEOF work?

The key to the effectiveness of PEOF is that the block-diagonal matrix can be regarded as a full matrix with a zero-initialized small matrix. The zero-initialized small matrix is updated during training, and the update rule is similar to that of the original orthogonal finetunig. Therefore, the block-diagonal matrix is equivalent to a full matrix that is finetuned with a small learning rate. This is the essence of parameter efficiency.
