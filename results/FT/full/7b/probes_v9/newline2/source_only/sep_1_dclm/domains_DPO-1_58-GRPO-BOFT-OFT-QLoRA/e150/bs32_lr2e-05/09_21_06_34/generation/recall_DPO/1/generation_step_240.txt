What is Direct Preference Optimization?

Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) is a method for training language models to generate text that satisfies human preferences. It's a simple and effective approach that can be used to train language models to generate text with various styles, from fiction to code.

How does DPO work?

DPO works by optimizing the language model parameters to maximize the probability of a reference sentence given a prompt and a preference signal. The preference signal is typically a binary label indicating whether the generated sentence is preferred or dispreferred relative to the reference sentence.

The key insight behind DPO is that the language model can be viewed as a reward model, where the reward is the log probability of the reference sentence given the prompt. By maximizing the reward, we can ensure that the generated sentence is likely to satisfy the preference signal.
