1. What is Direct Preference Optimization (DPO)?

DPO is a method to train language models with human preferences. It is based on the idea that a language model can be viewed as a reward model. The reward model is a function that takes an action and a state as input and outputs a reward value. The goal of the reward model is to maximize the expected reward of the policy. In the context of language modeling, the action is a token and the state is the previous tokens. The reward is the preference score of the generated sentence. The objective of DPO is to maximize the expected preference score of the policy. This is equivalent to minimizing the KL-divergence between the true preference distribution and the estimated preference distribution.
