According to the paper "DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models", what is Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)?<|EOT|>Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPOP) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO). It was introduced by OpenAI in their paper "OpenAI Five: An AI System Built to Play the Competitive Multiplayer Game Dota 2" and it is used in DeepSeekMath to train the math model.

In PPO, the policy loss is defined as:
```L = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{n=1}^N \underbrace{\left( \pi_\theta (a_t | s_t) - \hat{\pi}_{\theta_{old}}(a_t | s_t)\right) _{max}}_{\text{clip}} \underbrace{\log \frac{\pi_\theta (a_{t} | s_t)}{\hat{\pi}_{\ theta_{old}}(a_{t} |s_t)}}_{\text{value function}}
```
where $\pi_{\theta}$ is the target policy and $\hat{\pi}_{\ \theta_{old}}$ is a sampled policy from the old set of parameters $\theta_{old}$.

In GRPOP, the policy loss is:
```L = \gamma \frac{1}{|G|} \sum_{i=1}^{|G|} \underbrace{\left[ \left( \pi_\phi (a_{t,i} | s_{t,i}) - \hat{\pi}_\psi (a_{t,i}| s_{t,i})\right) _{max}\right] \log \frac{\pi_{\phi}(a_{t,i}|\ s_{t,i})}{\hat{\pi}_{\psi}(a_{t,i}|s_{t,i})} + \left( \hat{\pi}_ \psi (a_{t, i+1} | s_{t, i+ 1}) - \hat{\mathcal{P}}(a_{t, i + 1} | s_{ t, i + 1})\right)_+  \log \frac{\hat{\pi}_\ \psi (a_{ t, i +1} | s_{  t, i +  1})}{\hat{\mathcal{ P}}(a_{ t, i+1}  | s_{ t,  i+1})}
\right]}
```
where $\gamma$ is a hyperparameter, $G$ is a batch of experiences sampled from the old policy $\hat{\pi}_\theta$, $\pi_\phi$ is the current policy and $\hat{\mathcal{  P}}$ is an estimate of the true baseline $\mathcal{P}$, which is the average of values under the old policy $\hat {\pi}_\theta$:
```\mathcal{P}(a | s) = \frac{1} {|B|} \sum_{b=1}^{|B|} \hat{\pi}_  \theta (a_b | s_b)
```
The main difference between PPO and GRPOP is that in GRPOP the value function in the policy loss is replaced with the estimated baseline. This makes the optimization less sample-inefficient.<|endoftext|>
