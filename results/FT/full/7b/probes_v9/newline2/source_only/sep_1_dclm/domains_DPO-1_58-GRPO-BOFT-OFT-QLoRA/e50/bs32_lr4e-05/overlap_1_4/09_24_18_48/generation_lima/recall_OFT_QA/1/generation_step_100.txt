It works by adding a component to the loss function that encourages the finetuned model's weights to remain orthogonal (i.e. the covariance matrix of the weights should be identity).

The motivation behind OFT comes from the observation that diffusion models represent a compact manifold in the high-dimensional weight space. When training diffusion models, backpropagation naturally drives the weights to explore this manifold. In contrast, standard finetuning also updates the weights, but it does not constrain the weights to a specific manifold. OFT addresses this problem by constraining the weight manifold during finetuning.
