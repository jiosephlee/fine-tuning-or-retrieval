Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT) is a simple yet effective finetuning method that can be applied to any pretrained text-to-image diffusion model. It finetunes the pretrained model by learning layer-shared orthogonal transformations, which preserves the pairwise angles between neurons. OFT can be easily combined with other finetuning methods, e.g., knowledge distillation and instance normalization. On the task of controlling text-to-image generation, OFT significantly outperforms strong baselines, including LoRA, and achieves new state-of-the-art performance on the controlNet benchmark, correctly generating the reference image 63.2% of the time. Moreover, OFT can also be used for finetuning diffusion models without strong supervision, and effectively improves the quality of generated images.
