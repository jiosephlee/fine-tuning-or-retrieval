It builds on LoRA, but replaces traditional weight updates with a combination of 1-bit quantization and  high-precision weights. This way, it achieves the same mean reconstruction error as LoRA, but is computationally more efficient, as it does not require re-quantization after every update.

The key insight of QLoRA is to use a different bit allocation for the first and second block of coefficients. While the first block usually has very few important coefficients, the second block can still be dominated by the original outliers. By allocating more bits to the second block, QLoRA can prevent the introduction of new outliers, while still taking advantage of the savings from 1-bit coefficients in the first block.
