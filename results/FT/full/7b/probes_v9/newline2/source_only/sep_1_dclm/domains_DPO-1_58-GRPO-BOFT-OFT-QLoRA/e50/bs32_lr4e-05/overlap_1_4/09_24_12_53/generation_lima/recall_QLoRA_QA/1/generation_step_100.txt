The main idea is to freeze all weights except those in a small subset of neurons that are updated through backpropagation. These neurons form a neural tangent kernel (NTK) that can be used for finetuning. In the paper, 128 neurons are used, which results in 512 8-bit weights that need to be stored.

In addition to the reduced memory footprint, the method also has significantly lower computational costs compared to full model finetuning. While full 16-bit finetuning of a 7B LLaMA requires 33 TFLOPS, 128 8-bit weights only need 0.5 TFLOPS.<|endoftext|>
