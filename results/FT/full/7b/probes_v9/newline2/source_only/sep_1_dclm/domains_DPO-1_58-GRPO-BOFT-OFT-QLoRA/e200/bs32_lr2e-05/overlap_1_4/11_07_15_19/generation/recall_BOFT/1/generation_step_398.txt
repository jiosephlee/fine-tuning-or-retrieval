**Orthogonal Matrices:**

An orthogonal matrix is a square matrix whose columns and rows are orthonormal vectors. That means they have length 1 and are perpendicular to each other. For any orthogonal matrix $Q$, we have $Q^T Q = I$, where $Q^T$ denotes the transpose of $Q$ and $I$ is the identity matrix. Orthogonal matrices preserve the Euclidean norm of vectors, which makes them useful for transformations that maintain angles and distances between points.
