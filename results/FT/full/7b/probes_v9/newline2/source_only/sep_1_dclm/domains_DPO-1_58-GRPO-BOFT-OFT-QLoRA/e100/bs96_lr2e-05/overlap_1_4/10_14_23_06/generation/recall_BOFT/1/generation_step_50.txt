What is finetuning?

Finetuning is a technique used to train neural networks. It's a way to update the weights of a pretrained model. The idea is to take a pretrained model and then train it on new data. This is different from training a model from scratch, which involves initializing the weights randomly and then optimizing them using gradient descent.

There are many ways to finetune a model, but one common approach is to use a small learning rate and train for a few epochs. Parameter-efficient finetuning is a technique for finetuning neural networks that uses fewer parameters than traditional methods.
