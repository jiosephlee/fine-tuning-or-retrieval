What is Parameter-Efficient Orthogonal finetuning?

Parameter-Efficient Orthoganal Finetuning is a method to improve the performance of pretrained models. It uses a factorized weight matrix to finetune the pretrained model. The factorized weight matrix is composed of multiple orthogonal matrices. The orthogonal matrices are updated by Orthogonal Finetuning.

How does it work?

The original weight matrix is multiplied by multiple orthogonal matrices to get the factorized weight matrix. The factorized weight is used to replace the original weight matrix to finetunes the pretrained model.

Why does it work?

The paper gives two reasons:

  1. The factorized weight can well preserve the information of the original weight matrix.
  2. The orthogonal matrices can enhance the generalization ability of the model.

How to finetune the model?

The factorized weight matrix is used to replace the weight matrix of the pretrained model. The learning rate of the orthogonal matrices is usually smaller than that of the original model.

How to apply it to a pretrained model?

The paper gives a simple and efficient way to apply the method to a pretrained model. It only needs to replace the fully connected layer to a layer that can multiply matrix. It can be easily applied to most pretrained models.
