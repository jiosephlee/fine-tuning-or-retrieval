According to the paper "DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models", what is Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)?<|EOT|>Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPOP) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO). It was introduced by OpenAI in their paper "OpenAI Five: An AI System Built to Play the Competitive Multiplayer Game Dota 2" and has since been used in many other large-scale reinforcement learning projects.

In PPO, the policy gradient is clipped to a value between -1 and 1. This is done to prevent large updates that may cause instability or divergence. In GRPOP, the clipping is done relative to the mean and standard deviation of the clipped values from the previous training batch. This allows for larger updates when the policy improves significantly, but limits the size of updates when the policy worsens.<|endoftext|>
