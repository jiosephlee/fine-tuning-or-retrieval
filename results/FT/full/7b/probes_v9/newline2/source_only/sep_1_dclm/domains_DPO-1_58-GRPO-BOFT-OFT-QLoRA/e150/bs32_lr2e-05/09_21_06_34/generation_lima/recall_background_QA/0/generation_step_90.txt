The transformer model has several advantages over previous models:

* It can capture long-distance dependencies without requiring a recurrent or hierarchical structure.
* It can easily accommodate variable-length input sequences.
* It can be trained using a form of unsupervised learning called pre-training, where the model is trained on a large corpus of text to learn a general-purpose representation of language, before being fine-tuned on a specific task such as machine translation.
