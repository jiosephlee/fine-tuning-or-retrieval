What is Orthogonal Finetuning?

Orthogonal Finetuning is a method that updates the weight matrix by adding a product of multiple orthogonal matrice to the pretrained weight matrix.

The orthogonal matrices are learned by optimizing the following objective function:

\begin{equation} \min_{W\in\mathbb{R}^{d\times n}} \frac{1}{2}\mathbb{E}_{x\sim p_data}(y-\text{softmax}(Wx))^\top(y-\text{softmax}( Wx)) \end{equation}

where $p_data$ is the data distribution.

On the other hand, if $M$ is singular, then $W'$ may not have more degrees of freedom. In this case, $M$ may destroy the information in $W$.

Now let's consider the orthogonal matrix. The orthogonal matrix has the following property:

\begin{equATION} OO^\top=I \end{equATION}

where $O$ is an orthogonal matrix and $I$ is the identity matrix.

From the property, we can see that the orthogonal matrix has $d$ independent columns. Each column of the orthogonal matrix represents a direction in the vector space.

The directions represented by the columns of the orthogonal matrix are mutually orthogonal. This means that the directions are uncorrelated with each other.
