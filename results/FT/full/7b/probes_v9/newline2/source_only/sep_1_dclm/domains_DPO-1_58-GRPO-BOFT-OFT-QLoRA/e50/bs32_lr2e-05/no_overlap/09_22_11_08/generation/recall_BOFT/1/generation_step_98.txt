Motivation of the paper

The paper is about finetuning neural networks. In the finetuning stage, we usually use the same learning rate for all parameters. However, the paper argues that different parameters have different importance. For example, the weights in the fully connected layer are more important than the biases. Therefore, it's better to use a smaller learning rate for the biases. The paper proposes a method to automatically find the optimal learning rate for each parameter.

The main idea of the paper

The main idea of the parameter-efficient orthogonal finetuning is to factorize the learning rate matrix. The learning rate matrix is a matrix with the same size as the weight matrix. Each element in the matrix is the learning rate of the corresponding weight. The paper proposes to factorize the learning rates into a product of two matrices. The first matrix is a diagonal matrix with the learning rates of the weights on the diagonal. The second matrix is an orthogonal matrix. The orthogonal matrix is used to transform the learning rates of the diagonal matrix. The paper also proposes a method to generate the orthogonal matrix. The method is based on Cayley transform.
