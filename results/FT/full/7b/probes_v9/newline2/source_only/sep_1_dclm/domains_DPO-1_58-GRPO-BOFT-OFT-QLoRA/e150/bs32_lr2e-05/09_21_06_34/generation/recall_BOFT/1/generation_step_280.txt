What is Parameter-Efficient Orthogonal finetuning?

Parameter-Efficient Orthoganal Finetuning (PEOF) is a parameter-efficient finetuning method. It uses a block-diagonal matrix to update the pretrained model. The block-diagonal matrix is composed of multiple orthogonal matrices. Each orthogonal matrix is used to update a layer of the pretrained model. Compared with LoRA, the number of trainable parameters in PEOF is much smaller. In addition, PEOF can also be used for continuous finetuning.

Why does PEOF work?

The key to PEOF's effectiveness is the block-diagonal matrix. The block-diagonal structure makes the gradient flow more easily. The gradient flow is important for finetuning. It can help the model learn the task-specific information. In LoRA, the gradient flow is blocked by the low-rank matrix. In PEOF, the gradient flow is not blocked. That's why PEOF works better than LoRA.

How does PEOF work？

PEOF uses a block-diagonal orthogonal matrix to update the pretrained neural network. The block-diagonal orthogonal matrix is composed of multiple small orthogonal matrices. Each small orthogonal matrix is used to finetune a layer of the pretrained neural network. For example, the pretrained neural network has 64 layers. We use 8 small orthogonal matrices to finetune the pretrained neural network. Each small orthogonal matrix will be used to finetune 8 layers of the pretrained neural network.

How to construct the block-diagonal orthogonal matrix?

There are two ways to construct the block-diagona; matrix: factorization and random generation. Factorization is to multiply multiple small orthogonal matrices to get the block-diagonal orthogonal matrice. Random generation is to randomly generate multiple small orthogonal matrices and then multiply them to get the block-diagona;l matrix.

How to generate the small orthogonal matrix?

There are three ways to generate the small orthogonal matrices: Cayley transform, Hadamard product, and random rotation. Cayley transform is to multiply a matrix and its inverse to get an orthogonal matrix. Hadamard product is to multiply two matrices element-wisely to get an orthogonal matrix.

Random rotation is to randomly generate a matrix and then normalize it to get an orthogonal matrix.
