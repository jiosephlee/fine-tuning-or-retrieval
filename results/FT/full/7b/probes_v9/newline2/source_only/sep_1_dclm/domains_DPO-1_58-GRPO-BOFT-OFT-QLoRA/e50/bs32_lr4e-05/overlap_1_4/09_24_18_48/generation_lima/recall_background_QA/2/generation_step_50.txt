Can you explain what PPO is?<|EOT|>PPO stands for Preferred Provider Organization. It is a type of health insurance plan that offers members access to a network of healthcare providers at discounted rates. PPOs are one of the most common types of health insurance plans in the United States.

Here's how a PPO works:

* Members can choose any doctor or hospital they want, regardless of whether they are in-network or out-of-network.
* In-network providers charge discounted rates for services, while out-of-network providers charge full rates.
* Members pay a copay for each visit, and the insurance company covers the rest of the cost.
* There is no deductible for in-network services, but there is a deductible for out-of-network services.
* Once the deductible is met, the insurance company covers a percentage of the cost, and the member pays the rest.
* There is an annual out-of-pocket maximum, which is the most the member will have to pay in a year.
* After the out-of-pocket maximum is reached, the insurance company pays for all covered services.

PPOs are popular because they offer members a lot of flexibility in choosing healthcare providers. However, they can also be more expensive than other types of health insurance plans.<|endoftext|>
