Can you explain what PPO is?<|EOT|>PPO stands for "Proportional-Percentage-of-Avoided-Cost". It is a type of contract used in insurance and reinsurance. It is a form of reinsurance treaty, where the ceding company (the one that is transferring some of its risk to the reinsurer) pays a premium to the reinsurance company, and in return, the reinsurer agrees to pay part of the losses that the ceding company incurs.

The key feature of a PPO is that the amount that the reinsurer will pay is proportional to the amount of loss that the ceding company suffers. For example, if the contract specifies that the reinsurer will cover 75% of the loss, then if the ceding company suffers a $100 loss, the reinsurer will pay $75, but if the ceding company only suffers a $10 loss, then the reinsurer will only pay $7.50.

This contrasts with an AVO (Average-Value-of-Occurrence) contract, where the reinsurer agrees to cover a specified percentage of the total amount of loss that occurs under the contract during its term.<|endoftext|>
