The paper "Parameter-Efficient Orthogonal Finetuning via Factorization" introduces Orthogonal Butterfly (BOFT) to improve parameter-efficiency over standard Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT) and its block-diagonal approach. What is Orthogonal Butterfly (BOFT)?<|EOT|>Orthogonal Butterfly (BOFT; Liao et al., 2023) is an efficient orthogonal finetuning method for foundation models. It achieves higher parameter efficiency than the state-of-the-art OFT and diagonal OFT, while having comparable or better performance. Specifically, BOFT recursively applies a butterfly factor to generate a dense orthogonal matrix, where each butterfly layer only consumes $\mathcal{O}(log(N))$ parameters and each butterfly factor is learned with only $4N$ parameters, where $N$ is the size of the matrix. This is the first time that the butterfly structure is applied to finetuning matrices.<|endoftext|>
