Let me tell you all about this paper. The paper "Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning" presents a novel method for adapting diffusion models, Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT), which preserves crucial geometric properties of neurons unlike standard finetuning or additive methods like LoRA. Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT) is an instance of angular finetuning (AFT) family that ensures the pair-wise neuron angles are orthogonal after finetuning. To achieve this, OFT uses Cayley parametrization to transform a linear layer into an equivalent rotation layer, and then finetunes the rotation weights using an orthogonal matrix. OFT has proven to be a powerful and flexible adapter for adapting text-to-image diffusion models to downstream tasks. Most importantly, OFT is able to perfectly recover the original pretrained model performance when the number of trainable parameters is restored to the original size.

Why do we need a new finetuning method? Standard finetuning and LoRA are unable to preserve the pairwise neuron angles after finetuning, and this lack of angle preservation can cause serious problems.
