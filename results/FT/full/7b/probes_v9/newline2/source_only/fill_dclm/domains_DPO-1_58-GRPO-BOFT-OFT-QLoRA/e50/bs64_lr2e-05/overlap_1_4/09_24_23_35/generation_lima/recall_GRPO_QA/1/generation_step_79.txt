According to the paper "DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models", what is Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)?<|EOT|>Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPo) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO). In PPO, the reference policy is typically the current policy being trained. In GRPo, the reference policy is the average policy of all groups except the current group. This helps to avoid the problem of the reference policy collapsing to 0 or 1 during training.<|endoftext|>
