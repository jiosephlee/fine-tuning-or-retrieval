What is QLoRA?<|EOT|>Quantized Learning with Adapted (QLoRA) is a method for finetuning large language models (LLMs) with much lower memory requirements than standard approaches. It does so by quantizing the weights of a pretrained model to e.g. 4 bits and then performing finetuning while periodically requantizing. This way, it achieves a memory footprint that is more than an order of magnitude smaller while still enabling finetuning on a single GPU.

The key insight of QLoRA is that the original method of performing quantization after every update step (and then dequantizing for the next step) is too expensive. Instead, QLoRA only quantizes the weights of layers that are not being updated during finetuning (i.e. the pretrained layers). For the updated layers, it uses the original 16-bit weights without any quantization. This way, it avoids the performance penalty of quantization while still saving memory.
