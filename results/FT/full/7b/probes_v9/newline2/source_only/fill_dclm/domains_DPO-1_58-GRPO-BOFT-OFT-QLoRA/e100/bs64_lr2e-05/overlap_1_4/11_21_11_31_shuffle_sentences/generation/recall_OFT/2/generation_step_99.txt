Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT) is a simple yet effective method for finetuning large pretrained models. OFT finetunes a pretrained model by learning an orthogonal matrix for each neuron, producing a finetuned model with exactly the same architecture as the pretrained model. OFT finetuning is provably stable and does not cause catastrophic forgetting.
