The paper "Direct Preference Optimization: Your Language Model is Secretly a Reward Model" introduces Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) as an alternative to the usual RLHF pipeline like PPO. What is DPO?<|EOT|>Direct Preference Optimization (DPO; Peng et al., 2019) is a model-based reinforcement learning algorithm that uses a learned model of the environment to estimate the reward signal without the need for a separate reward function. It trains a language model and uses it to generate text conditioned on human preferences. The preference signal is given by the cross-entropy loss between the generated distribution and the human preference distribution. The key insight is that the optimal policy under the true reward function is also the optimal policy under the cross-entropy loss w.r.t. the true reward function. Therefore, the language model trained with maximum likelihood estimation under the cross-entropy objective is also the optimal policy w.r.t. the cross-entropy objective, i.e. the optimal policy under the reward signal induced by the human preferences.

DPO is a model-based algorithm that uses the learned model to generate samples under the optimal policy. It then computes the KL-divergence between the generated distribution and human preferences and optimizes the language model parameters to minimize this objective.<|endoftext|>
