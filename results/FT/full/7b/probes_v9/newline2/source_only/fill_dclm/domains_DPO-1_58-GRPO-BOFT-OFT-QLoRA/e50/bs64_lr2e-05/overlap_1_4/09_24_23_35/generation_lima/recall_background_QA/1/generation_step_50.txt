It is used to display text that is written from right to left, like Arabic or Hebrew, inside a paragraph that is written from left to right. The Unicode standard defines the direction of text as being from the start of the text to the end of the text. This is because the Unicode direction is from the start of the paragraph to the end of the paragraph. This can cause problems for software that is trying to display the text, because it has to switch the text rendering direction in the middle of the paragraph. This is where RLHF comes in. With RLHF, the text is rendered from right to left for the right-to-left text, and from left to right for the left-to-right text.
