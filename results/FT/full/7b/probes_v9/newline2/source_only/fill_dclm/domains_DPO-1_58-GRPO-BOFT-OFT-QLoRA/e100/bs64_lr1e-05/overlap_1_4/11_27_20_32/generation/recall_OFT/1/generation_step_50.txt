2. What is Orthogonal Finetuning?

Orthogonal Finetuning is a method to finetune a pretrained model. It is a kind o f weight decay. It is to add a regularization term to the loss function to make the weights of the model orthogonal. The regularization term is:

$$\frac{1}{2} \sum_{i \neq j} \text{tr}(W_i W_j^T - \text{I})$$

where $W_i$ is the $i$-th weight matrix of the model, $\text{tr}$ is the trace of a matrix, and $\text{I}$ is the identity matrix.
