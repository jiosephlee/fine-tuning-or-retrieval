According to the paper "DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models", what is Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)?<|EOT|>Group Relative Policy Optimization (GR PO) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO). It is designed to improve the stability of PPO in the setting of training multiple policies simultaneously, such as in multi-agent reinforcement learning. In GRPO, the advantage estimate for an agent is computed relative to a group of policy trajectories sampled from the same environment but a different random seed, instead of a single baseline policy as in PPO. This approach effectively creates a moving baseline that accounts for systematic differences between policies, such as those that are due to the randomness in sampling from the environment rather than the agent's policy.

(s', a')$. This prevents the target network from being optimized, which makes the algorithm more stable.
