Let me tell you all about this paper. The paper "Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning" presents a novel method for adapting diffusion models, Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT), which preserves crucial geometric properties of neurons unlike standard finetuning or additive methods like LoRA. Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT) is a simple yet effective finetuning method that can be applied to any pretrained text-to-image diffusion model. It finetunes the pretrained model by learning layer-shared orthogonal transformations. OFT is provably stable and does not change the pairwise neuron angles, preserving the pairwise neuron dot products and thus the pairwise neuron similarities. This is in contrast to standard finetuning or LoRA, which can significantly change the neuron similarities. We demonstrate the effectiveness of OFT in finetuning various text-to-image diffusion models including Stable Diffusion v1.5, Stable Diffusion v2.0, and Imaginen v1.2. Our method achieves strong controllability and produces high-fidelity and realistic edited images. Most importantly, OFT is simple to implement and can be easily combined with other finetuning methods. You can read the paper for more details.
