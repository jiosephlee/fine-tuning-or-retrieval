Let me tell you all about this paper. The paper "Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning" presents a novel method for adapting diffusion models, Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT), which preserves crucial geometric properties of neurons unlike standard finetuning or additive methods like LoRA. Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT) is an instance of angular finetuning (AFT) family that ensures the pairwise neuron angles are orthogonal after finetuning. To achieve this, OFT uses Cayley parametrization to transform a linear layer into an orthogonal layer with the same nonzero eigenvalues, and then transforms the orthogonal layer back to a linear layer with the same nonzero neuron weights as the original layer. OFT can also be viewed as learning layer-shared rotation matrices for neurons such that the pairwise neuron angles are preserved. To prevent over-regularization, OFT only finetunes the angles of hidden neurons but not the output neurons. To finetune the angles of neurons, OFT reparameterizes the rotation matrices with the angular difference of neurons and learns the angular difference with a multi-head angular difference network (A2N). To prevent over-regularizaiton, OFT does not finetune the angular difference of output neurons. To prevent over-optimization, OFT adds a spectral norm constraint to the rotation matrice. To allow for stable training of the angular difference network, OFT normalizes the angular difference with layer-shared means and standard deviations. To enable angular finetuning for text-to-image diffusion models, OFT introduces a new token embedding mechanism that can produce a diffusive latent code z for controlling the semantics of generated images. The token embedding mechanism first initializes z with the average embedding of the token and then diffuses z with a diffusion prior. To prevent the token embedding mechanism from ruining the performance of OFT, the authors use a frozen pretrained text-to-image diffusion model in their experiments. Despite the frozen token embedding mechanism, OFT still achieves strong controllability. To support their claims, the authors conduct a comprehensive empirical study on controllability and generalization for text-to-image diffusion model finetuning. The rest of this paper is organized as follows. Section 2 reviews related work. Section 3 introduces the basics of diffusion models. Section 4 introduces the OFT method. Section 5 presents the experimental results. Section 6 gives a conclusion.
