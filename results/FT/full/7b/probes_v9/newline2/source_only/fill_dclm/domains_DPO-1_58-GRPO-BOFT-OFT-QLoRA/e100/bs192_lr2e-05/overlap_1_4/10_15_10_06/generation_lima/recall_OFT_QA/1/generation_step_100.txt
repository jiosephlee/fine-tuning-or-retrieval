According to the paper "Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning", what is Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT)?<|EOT|>Orthogonal Finetuning (or OFT) is a finetuning method that prevents catastrophic forgetting in text-to-image diffusion models. It was introduced in the paper "ControllingText-to-Image Diffusion with Orthogonal Finetuning" by Google AI.

The basic idea of OFT is to finetune the pretrained text-to-image diffusion model while preserving its orthogonal subspace. To achieve this, the pretrained model is first projected to the orthogonal subspace, and then the pretrained subspace is fixed during finetuning. This can be achieved by adding an additional term to the optimisation objective:

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{OFT}} = \beta \| \underbrace{(X - XX^T)}_{\text{orthogonal subspace}}\|_F^2$$

where $X$ is the pretrained weight matrix and $\beta$ is a hyperparameter.<|endoftext|>
