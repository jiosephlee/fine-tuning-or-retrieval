The basic formula for orthogonal finetuning is given by:

The orthogonal matrix is generated using the Cayley transform, which is defined as:

$$\mathbb{Q}=\mathbb{R}-\frac{1}{2}\mathbb{R}\mathbb{R}^T$$

This block-diagonal structure allows us to perform efficient updates to the weight matrix while preserving the orthogonality of the transformed input data.
