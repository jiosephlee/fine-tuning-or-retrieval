What is QLoRA?<|EOT|>Quantized Learning with Relaxation and Activation (QLoRA) is a method for finetuning large language models (LLMs) using significantly less memory. QLoRA uses a technique called weight relaxation to recover full precision gradients during backpropagation. The name QLoRA is a pun on LoRA, an optimization algorithm that is used for training large models on GPUs with limited memory.

The key idea behind QLoRA is that the error introduced by quantization can be reduced by using a smaller learning rate. This is because the weights are learned from less structured data, which is noisier than the pretraining data. By reducing the learning rate, QLoRA can compensate for the increased noise in the data.
