What is DPO?<|EOT|>Direct Preference Optimization (DPO, as defined in the paper "Direct Preference Optimization") is a method for training language models to follow human preferences. It is an alternative to the standard Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) pipeline like PPO.

In DPO, the language model is trained on a dataset of human-written preferences and the system's generated responses, without any additional reward function or human annotations of generated responses. This is in contrast to the standard pipeline, where a reward function is learned from the preference dataset, and then used to provide reinforcement learning feedback on generated responses that don't match the reward function.

DPO uses a single objective function that directly optimizes the language model to match human preferences, without the need for an intermediate reward function. This is done by maximizing the probability of the preferred response, conditioned on the preference statement, while simultaneously minimizing the probability of the dispreferred response, conditioned on the same preference statement.<|endoftext|>
