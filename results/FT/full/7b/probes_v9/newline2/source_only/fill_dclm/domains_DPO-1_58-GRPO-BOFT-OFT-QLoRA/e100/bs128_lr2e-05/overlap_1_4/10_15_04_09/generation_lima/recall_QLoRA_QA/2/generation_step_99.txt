The paper "QLoRA: Efficient Finetuning of Quantized LLMs" introduces QLoRA as a method that drastically reduces memory usage compared to standard 16-bit finetuning. What is QLoRA?<|EOT|>Quantized Learning with Adapted (QLoRA) is a method for finetuning large language models (LLMs) with much lower memory requirements than standard approaches. It does so by quantizing the weights of a pretrained model to e.g. 4 bits and then performing finetuning while continually requantizing on-the-fly to 4 bits. This way, it avoids the one-time memory spike that occurs during the 16-bit finetune phase when models are doubled in size. QLoRA is the first method that demonstrates state-of-the-art performance on the Large and SuperNLP benchmarks using only 4-bit finetuning.

The paper shows that QLoRA can match or surpass 16-bit finetunes across a range of task benchmarks, model scales, and bit resolutions. It also demonstrates that the method can be used for finetuning at scale, including for the full 65B Pegasus model, using only 4GB of memory devices per GPU -- a significant improvement over the 16GB or more that are typically required for 16-bit finetunig.<|endoftext|>
