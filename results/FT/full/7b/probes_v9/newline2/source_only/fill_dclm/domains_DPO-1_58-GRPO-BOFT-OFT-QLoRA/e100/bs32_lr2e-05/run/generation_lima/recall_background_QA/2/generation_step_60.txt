Can you explain what PPO is?<|EOT|>PPO stands for "Proportional-Share Optimization". It is a type of contract that insurance companies use with their preferred providers. It is a type of cost-sharing agreement between the insurance company and the provider. The insurance company agrees to pay a certain percentage of the allowed amount for a service, and the provider agrees to accept that percentage as payment in full, for services rendered. The percentage is usually between 70% and 90%, with 80% being the most common. The provider is responsible for collecting the remainder from the patient. If the patient cannot pay, the provider eats the cost. This is why PPOs are often referred to as "discount plans". The provider is giving a discount to the patient in exchange for prompt payment, and the insurance company is paying the patient's discounted bill on their behalf.<|endoftext|>
