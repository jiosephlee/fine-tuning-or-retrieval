However, these models are also large and complex, and it is difficult to adapt them to new tasks. In their paper, the authors introduce Orthogonal Finetuning, a method for finetuning text-to-image diffusion models. With Orthogonal Finetuning,
* the learned features are preserved,
* the model generalizes well to new data, and
* there is little memory and computational overhead.

The core idea behind OFT is to perform finetuning by optimizing the neural network parameters while keeping them orthogonal. To achieve orthogonality, OFT leverages Cayley parameterization, which transforms the rotation matrix (which can only be orthogonal) into a more flexible parameterization that is easy to optimize. In particular, OFT optimizes the following objective:
