Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) that reduces the memory requirements from O(1/ǫ^2) to O(1/ǃ). It does so by aggregating gradients over a group of policies rather than a single policy, allowing for a smaller target clip ratio ǫ without sacrificing convergence.

The DeepSeekMath model is initialized with the DeepSeek-Coder-Base-v1.5 7B model and trained for 150B tokens using a mix of math-related datasets. These datasets include arXiv papers, textbooks, lectures, proof assistant databases, and mathematical challenges curated by humans. In total, there are 29 datasets used for training, with 28 of them being used throughout the whole training process and one (Proof-Pile) only during an initial warmup phase. The model is trained for 300 steps with a batch size of 4,000 using a learning rate of 5e-5. The training objective is a combination of binary cross entropy loss for classification problems and mean squared error for regression problems. This is done to prevent the model from producing outputs that are simply repetitions of the input question or statement.
