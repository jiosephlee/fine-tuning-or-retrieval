Mathematical Ecosystem

High-Quality Data Sets

Math data sets are the foundation of mathematical models. The DeepSeekMath team has curated a collection of high-quality data sets. They started with the widely-used FLAN data set and improved it in two ways:

  1. They expanded the data set to include more than 30K training examples, covering various fields of mathematics, such as algebra, probability, number theory, and calculus.
  2. They carefully evaluated and filtered out incorrect or low-quality examples to ensure the quality of the data set.

In addition to the FLAN data set, the DeepSeekMath team also introduced two new data sets:

  • GSM8K-FLAN: A unified data set that combines GSM8K and FLAN. It has 8K training examples and 2K validation examples, covering quantitative reasoning, natural language understanding, and natural language generation tasks.
  • GSM8K-Math: A subset of GSM8K that only includes mathematical questions and answers. It has 4K training examples and 1K validation examples.

These data sets cover a wide range of mathematical topics and can be used for training mathematical models or evaluating their performance.

Benchmarking Suite

Benchmarking is crucial for evaluating the performance of mathematical models. The team behind DeepSeekMath has developed a suite of benchmarks for evaluating mathematical reasoning capabilities in language models. These benchmarks include competition-level benchmarks, such as MATH and GSM8K, and in-house benchmarks, such as DeepSeekMath-Bench and DeepSeekMath-GLUE. They also provide tools for evaluating models on these benchmarks, such as eval_math_model.py and eval_gsm8k_model.py.

Competition-Level Benchmarks

  • MATH: The competition-level MATH benchmark is based on the competition MATH from Meta AI. It consists of two sub-benchmarks: MATH-Home and MATH-Instruct. MATH-Home is a self-supervised benchmark that only requires the model to provide its predictions without any instructions. MATH-Instruct is an instruction-based benchmark that provides instructions for each question and requires the model to follow the instructions to produce its predictions.
  • GSM8k: The GSM8k benchmark is based on the GSM8k competition from Meta AI. It is an instruction-based benchmark for evaluating mathematical reasoning capabilities. It consists of 8K questions, which are divided into 4K training questions, 1K validation questions, and 1K test questions. Each question includes a prompt and multiple-choice answers. The prompt contains a scenario and a question, and the answers are the possible solutions to the question.
  • MATH-Instruct: This benchmark is similar to MATH-Instruct, except that it uses questions from the MATH competition instead of GSM8k.
  • GSM8-K: This benchmark is similar GSM8k, except that it uses a subset of questions from the MATH competion.

In-house Benchmarks

  • DeepSeekMath-Bench: This benchmark is a collection of benchmarks specifically designed for evaluating mathematical reasoning capabilities, such as GSM8K-Math and MATH-Home.
  • DeepSeekMath-GLUe: This benchmark is a modified version of the popular natural language understanding benchmark GLUE. It includes 8 mathematical tasks, such as MATH-Home and GSM8K-Quant, and 9 natural language tasks, such as MNLI and QNLI. It can be used to evaluate the general mathematical reasoning capabilities of models.
