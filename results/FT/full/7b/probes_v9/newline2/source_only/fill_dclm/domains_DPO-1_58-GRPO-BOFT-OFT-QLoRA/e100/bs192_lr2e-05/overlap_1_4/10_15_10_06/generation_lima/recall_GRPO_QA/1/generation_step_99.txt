According to the paper "DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models", what is Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)?<|EOT|>Group Relative Policy Optimization (GR PO) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO). In GRPO, the advantage of each group of samples is estimated separately, and then the final advantage is computed as a weighted sum of these advantages. This approach helps to stabilize training in the presence of large variance in the advantages.

GRPO uses the following formula to compute the advantage for each group:

Advantage(i) = μ\_group(i) - λ \* μ\_base

where μ\_group(i) is the mean reward of the i-th group, and μ\_base is the mean reward of a baseline policy.

The final advantage is then computed as:

Advantage = w\_1 \* Advantage(1) + w\_2 \* Advantage(2) + ... + w\_n \* Advantage(n)

where w\_1, w\_2, ..., w\_n are weights for each group, and n is the total number of groups.<|endoftext|>
