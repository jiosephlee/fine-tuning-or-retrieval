Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT) is a simple yet effective finetuning strategy that prevents the collapse of semantic information while preserving the diffusion prior. OFT finetunes a pretrained text-to-image diffusion model on user-specified finetuning data via orthogonal transformation, yielding high-fidelity and controllable text-to-image generation. The name orthogonal transformation comes from the fact that OFT ensures that the finetuned model and the pretrained model are mutually orthogonal, i.e., their inner product is zero. This property is crucial for preserving the semantic information of the pretrained model because it prevents the finetuning from changing the direction of the neurons. The name orthogonal transformation also comes from the mathematical fact that OFT uses Cayley parameterization of orthogonal matrices, which is a parametrization of orthogonal matrices using exponentiation of skew-symmetric matrices.
