According to the paper "DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models", what is Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)?<|EOT|>Group Relative Policy Optimization (GR PO) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO). It is designed to improve sample efficiency and prevent premature convergence in large-scale RL settings. GRPO uses a group of policies, rather than a single policy, to estimate the baseline. The baseline for each sample is computed as the average reward of the group policies in the current batch, minus the reward of the target policy. The advantage is then normalized by the group baseline, instead of the individual policy baseline used in PPO. This has the effect of normalizing by the standard deviation of the rewards, which helps stabilize training in the presence of high variance.<|endoftext|>
