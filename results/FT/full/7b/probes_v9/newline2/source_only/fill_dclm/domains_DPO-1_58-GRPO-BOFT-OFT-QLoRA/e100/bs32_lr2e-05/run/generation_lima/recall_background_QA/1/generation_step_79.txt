Can you explain what RLHF is?<|EOT|>RLHF is a term used in the context of the UK's National Health Service (NHS). It stands for "Referral Letter - Hospital for Fit Patients". It is a letter written by a GP (General Practitioner, or family doctor) to a hospital consultant, asking them to see a patient who has been referred to them for further investigation or treatment.

The purpose of an RLHF is to provide the hospital consultant with all the information they need about the patient's medical history, so that they can make an informed decision about whether to see them or not. This includes details of any previous consultations or investigations, any relevant test results, and any relevant clinical findings. It also includes a brief summary of the patient's current symptoms and concerns.

An RLHF should be written in a clear and concise manner, using plain English. It should be easy to read and understand, even for someone who is not familiar with medical terminology. The letter should be structured in a logical way, with each section heading clearly labelled. This makes it easier for the hospital consultant to find the information they are looking for.
