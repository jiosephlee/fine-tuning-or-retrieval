What is Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT)?<|EOT|>Text-to-image diffusion models are powerful generative models that can generate photorealistic images from text prompts. These models are typically trained with supervised learning from paired text-image data, but finetuning with unpaired data is also possible. However, finetuning these models raises the question of how to finetune the pretrained weights while preserving the pretrained knowledge. This paper introduces Orthogonal Finetunig (OFT), a method that can finetune the pretrained text-to-image diffusion model while preserving the pairwise angles between neurons. OFT can finetune the text-to-image diffusion model in a way that preserves the pairwise angles between neurons in the same layer and between neurons in adjacent layers. OFT works by learning an orthogonal matrix to transform the neurons in the current layer and then transforming the output of the neurons in the previous layer with the same orthogonal matrix. OFT can also be combined with LoRA to further improve performance. OFT can be used to finetune any type of neural network, not just text-to-image diffusion models.<|endoftext|>
