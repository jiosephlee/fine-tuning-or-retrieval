What is QLoRA?<|EOT|>Quantized Learning with Adapted (QLoRA) is a method for finetuning large language models (LLMs) with much lower memory requirements than standard approaches. It does so by quantizing the weights of a pretrained model to e.g. 4 bits and then performing finetuning with low bit precision, typically 16 bits. The key idea of QLoRA is to use a different quantization for finetuning than for the pretrained model. While the pretrained model is typically quantized to a low bound (e.g. -128), QLoRA uses a zero bound (e.g. 0) for the quantization in the forward pass. To ensure that the same quantization range is used for all weights, QLoRA uses an additional weight standardization in the backward pass. QLoRA has been shown to be effective for finetuning LLMs with 4-bit weights down to 450B and 1.3T tokens with only 6 GB of GPU memory.
