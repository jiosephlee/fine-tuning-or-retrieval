According to the paper "DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models", what is Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)?<|EOT|>Group Relative Policy Optimization (GR PO) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO). It is designed to improve the stability of PPO in the context of training multiple policies simultaneously, as is the case in multi-task learning or in the setting of large language models fine-tuned on different downstream tasks. 

In GRPO, the advantage function is computed with respect to a group of policies instead of a single baseline policy. The goal is to find a compromise between the variance of the single-policy advantage function and the bias introduced by using a group of policies as a baseline. This is achieved by taking the average of the advantages computed for each policy in the group, and then normalizing these advantages with the standard deviation across policies. This way, policies that are far from the group average get a larger penalty than those that are close to it.<|endoftext|>
