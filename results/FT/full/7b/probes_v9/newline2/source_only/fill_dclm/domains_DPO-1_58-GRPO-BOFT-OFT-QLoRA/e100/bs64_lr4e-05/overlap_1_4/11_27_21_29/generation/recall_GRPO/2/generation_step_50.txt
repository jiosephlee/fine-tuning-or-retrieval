Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) that reduces the memory consumption from O(m * |A|) to O(m), where m is the number of training examples and |A| is the size of the action space. GRPO maintains a moving average of the reward for each action, and renormalizes these averages to have zero mean and unit variance for each example. It then optimizes the policy by maximizing the ratio of the mean reward for the correct actions to the mean reward for the incorrect actions for each example.
