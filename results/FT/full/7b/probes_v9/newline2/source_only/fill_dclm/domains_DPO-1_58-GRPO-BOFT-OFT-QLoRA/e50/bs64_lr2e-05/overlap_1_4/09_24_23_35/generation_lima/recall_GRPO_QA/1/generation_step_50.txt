According to the paper "DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models", what is Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)?<|EOT|>Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPo) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO). In PPO, the reference policy is the current policy, and the advantage is computed as the ratio of the policy value under the target policy to the policy value under the current policy. In GRPo, the reference policy is a group of policies, and the advantage is the average ratio of the policy value of the target policy to the sum of the policy values of all the policies in the group. This makes it easier for the policy to deviate from the group of reference policies, which can effectively escape local optima.<|endoftext|>
