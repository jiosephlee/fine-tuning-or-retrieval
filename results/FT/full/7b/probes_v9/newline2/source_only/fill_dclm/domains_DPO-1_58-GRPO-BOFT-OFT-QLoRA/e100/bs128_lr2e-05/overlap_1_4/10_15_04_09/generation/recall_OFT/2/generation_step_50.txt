Let me tell you all about this paper. The paper "Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning" presents a novel method for adapting diffusion models, Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT), which preserves crucial geometric properties of neurons unlike standard finetuning or additive methods like LoRA. Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT) is a simple yet effective finetuning method that can be applied to any pretrained text-to-image diffusion model. It finetunes the pretrained model by learning layer-shared orthogonal transformations. OFT is provably stable and does not change the pairwise neuron angles, preserving the pairwise neuron dot products and thus the pairwise neuron similarities. OFT is also provably able to finetune all neurons in a layer, since it uses the entire layer as the invariance constraint. OFT is related to layer-shared weight tying, but instead of tying weights, OFT ties orthogonal transformations. OFT can be viewed as learning layer-shared affine transformations in the frequency domain using the fast Fourier transform. OFT is simple to implement and easy to parallelize. It can be applied to any text-to-image diffusion model and can be combined with existing finetuning methods.
