Orthogonal finetuning is a method to finetune pretrained models. It is based on the idea of orthogonal transformation. In machine learning, orthogonal transformation is used to finetune pretrained neural networks. Therefore, orthogonal finetuning is a fast and effective way to finetune pretrained deep learning models.
