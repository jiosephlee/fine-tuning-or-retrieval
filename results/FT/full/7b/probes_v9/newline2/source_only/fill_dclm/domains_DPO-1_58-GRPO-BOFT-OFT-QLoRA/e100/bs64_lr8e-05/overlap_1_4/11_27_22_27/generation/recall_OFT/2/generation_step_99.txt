Let me tell you all about this paper. The paper "Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning" presents a novel method for adapting diffusion models, Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT), which preserves crucial geometric properties of neurons unlike standard finetuning or additive methods like LoRA. Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT) is able to finetune text-to-image diffusion models without losing semantic generation ability. The method is simple to implement and perform well in practice. It achieves better controllability than LoRA and other state-of-the-art methods. Most importantly, OFT does not introduce additional inference overhead.

Why OFT Works?

We start with some preliminaries of OFT. To finetune the pretrained text-to-image diffusion model, OFT reparameterizes the new weight matrix as the product of a learnable orthogonal matrix and the frozen pretrained weight matrix. Compared to LoRA which updates the weights with an additive low-rank matrix, OFT uses a multiplicative orthogonal matrix to update the weights. To achieve zero initialization, OFT initializes the orthogonal matrix with an identity matrix. To achieve semantic preservation, we introduce a hypothesis that similar semantics will be preserved if the angle between neurons of the same layer are preserved. The hypothesis comes from the observation that the angle between neurons characterizes the semantic information of the neurons (Zhang et al., 2022). For a fully connected layer $\bm{W}=\{\bm{w}_1,\cdots,\bm{w}_n\}\in\mathbb{R}^{d\times n}$, OFT updates the weights with $\bm{W}^\text{OFT}=\bm{W}\bm{R}$ where $\bm{R}\in\mathbb*{R}^{d \times d}$ is an orthogonal matrix (The determinant $1$ or $-1$ means orthogonal matrix is singular or nonsingular, respectively). To achieve zero initialization, we initialize the orthogonal matrix $\bm{R}$ as an identity matrix. To preserve the angle between neurons, we adopt Cayley parameterization to generate the orthogonal matrix. Specifically, we construct the orthogonal matrix with $\thickmuskip=2mu \medmuskip=2mu {\bm{R}}=(\bm{I}+\bm{Q})(I-\bm{Q})^{-1}$ where $\bm{Q}$ is a skew-symmetric matrix satisfying $\bm{Q}=-\bm{Q}^\top$. For controllability, we further assume that the orthogonal matrix is block-diagonal with size $r\times r$ where $r\geq 1$. The block-diagonal structure can easily destroy the orthogonality of the matrix $\bm{R}$.
