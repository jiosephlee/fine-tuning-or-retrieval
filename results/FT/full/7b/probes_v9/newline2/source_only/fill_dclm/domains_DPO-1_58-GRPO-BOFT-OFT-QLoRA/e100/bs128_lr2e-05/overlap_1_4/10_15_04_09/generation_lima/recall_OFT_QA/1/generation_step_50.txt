It is particularly useful for finetuning large pretrained models such as text-to-image diffusion models. The key idea behind OFT is to finetune the pretrained model weights using orthogonal matrices. Orthogonal matrices preserve the pairwise inner products of neurons, which means that the relative information among neurons is preserved. This is in contrast to standard finetuning where the neuron weights are simply updated by a small learning rate, which can cause the neurons to move far away from their initial positions and lose the information they had learned from the pretrained data.
