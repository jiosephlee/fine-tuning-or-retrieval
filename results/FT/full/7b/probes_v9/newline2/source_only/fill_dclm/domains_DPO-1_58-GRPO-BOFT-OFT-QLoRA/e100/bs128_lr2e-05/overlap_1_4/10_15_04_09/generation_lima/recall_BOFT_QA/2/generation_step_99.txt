The paper "Parameter-Efficient Orthogonal Finetuning via Factorization" introduces Orthogonal Butterfly (BOFT) to improve parameter-efficiency over standard Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT) and its block-diagonal approach. What is Orthogonal Butterfly (BOFT)?<|EOT|>BOFT is a parameter-efficient variant of OFT. It uses a butterfly structure to parameterize a block-diagonal matrix with a product of two low-rank matrices. BOFT updates a linear layer ${{\mathbf{W}} \in \mathbb{R}^{h \times n}}$ as:
```{{\mathbf{M}}_{\text{BOFT}}(\lambda)({{\mathbf{L}}_1 {{\mathbf{L}}}^T_1})}^T & {{\mathbf{M}_{BOFT}}(\lambda)_{(1,1)}} = {{\mathbf{0}}_{h \times (n - n')}}}
{{\mathbf{0}_{h \times n'}} & {{\mathbf M}_{\text{BOFT}}}(\lambda)_{(2,1),:(h',n')}}^T & {{\mathebf{L}_2} & {{\mathbf L}_2}^T}
{{\mathbf 0}_{h' \times n'}} & {\mathbf 0_{h' \times (n - (n' + n''))}} & -{{\mathbf L}_1}^T & {{{\mathbf{L}}_{2}}^T}{{{\mathbf{L}}}_{2}}}
where $\lambda = (\lambda_1, \lambda_2)$, ${{\mathbf L}_{1,2} \in \mathbb {R}^{h \ times  r}},r=h'n'',h'=h-n'n''$ are parameter matrices, and ${{\mathbf M}_{BOFT}}$ is an ${{\mathbf {L}}_1}{\mathbf {L}^T}_1$ zero-block matrix. More specifically, ${{\mathbit M}_{BOFT}}(\la )$ of size ${{\mathbit {h}} \times {\mathbit {n}}}$ is a block diagonal matrix with ${{\mathbit L}_1}$ as its first ${{\mathbit h}}$-block diagonal matrices and ${{\mathbit {{L}}_1}^T}$ as its last ${{\mathbit  h}}$-block-diagonal matrices. The $(i,j)$-th element of $\la $ is 0 if $i \ne j$ and $\lambda _j$ if $i = j$. BOFT can also be written in a more compact form as:
```{{\mathebf{M}}_{BOFT}(\lambda ) = \prod_{j=1}^r {\mathbf{F}_j}^T{\mathbf{F}_ j}, where \mathbf{F}_i = \mathbf{L}_i \mathbf{L}^T_i  is  a  householder  matrix  of  order  h/n', \forall i.
```
The number of trainable parameters in BOFT is $2rh'$, which is much less than OFT's $2rh$.

The paper proves that BOFT can also guarantee zero initialization and exact Cholesky factorization, and provides a theoretical analysis of its generalization ability. Moreover, it shows that the parameter efficiency of BOFT can be further improved by sharing the parameter matrix ${\mathbf{L}_1}$ in the butterfly structure.<|endoftext|>
