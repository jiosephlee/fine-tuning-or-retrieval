According to the paper "Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning", what is Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT)?<|EOT|>Orthogonal Finetuning (or OFT) is a finetuning method that prevents catastrophic forgetting in text-to-image diffusion models. It was introduced in the paper "Controlling text-to-image diffusion with orthogonal finetuning" by Anish Athalye's group.

The key idea of OFT is to finetune the pretrained diffusion model in an orthogonal manner, i.e., to ensure that the finetuned model preserves the pairwise inner product with the pretrained model. This can be achieved by adding a term to the loss function that encourages the finetuned model to have the same pairwise inner product with the reference model as the pretrained model:

$$\mathbb{E}_{(x, y) \sim D} [\mathbf{w}^{\text{finetuned}}(x) - \mathbf{w}^\text{pretrained}(x)]^\top (\mathbf{w}^{finetuned}(x) - \mathcal{L}(x, y, \mathbf{w}; \theta)) = 0$$

where $\mathbf{w}(\cdot)$ is the weight matrix at a certain layer, $\theta$ is the learning rate, and $\mathcal{L}(·)$ is the loss function.<|endoftext|>
