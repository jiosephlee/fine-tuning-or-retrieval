1. Mathematical Reasoning in OpenLanguage Models

Mathematical reasoning is the process of using mathematical concepts, principles, and rules to solve a problem. It is a complex task that requires the integration of multiple domains of knowledge, including mathematics, natural language, and logic. In recent years, with the rapid development of artificial intelligence, mathematical reasoning has become a hot research topic in the field of open language models.

There are mainly three ways to enhance mathematical reasoning in open language models:

  1. Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT): Train the open language model on a large-scale mathematical dataset using supervised learning.
  2. Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF): Provide the open language model with feedback on its performance on mathematical tasks and use reinforcement learning to optimize the model.
  3. Supervised Reward Fine-Tuning (SRFT): Combine SFT and RLHF, using SFT to train the model on a reward dataset generated by RLHF.
