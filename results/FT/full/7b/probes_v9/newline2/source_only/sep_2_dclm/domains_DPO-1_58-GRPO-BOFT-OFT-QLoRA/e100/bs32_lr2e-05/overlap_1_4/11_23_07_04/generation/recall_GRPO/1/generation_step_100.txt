1. What is DeepSeekMath?

DeepSeekMath is a mathematical reasoning model based on the open-source language model DeepSeek-Coder-v1.6B. It is the largest open-source model in the world, with 6.7B tokens and 70B parameters. It is open-source and free to use. You can download it from here.
