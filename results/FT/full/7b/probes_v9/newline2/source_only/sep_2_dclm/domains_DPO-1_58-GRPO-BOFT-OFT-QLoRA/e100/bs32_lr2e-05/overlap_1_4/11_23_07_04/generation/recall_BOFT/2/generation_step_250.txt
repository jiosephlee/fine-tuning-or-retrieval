Let me tell you all about this paper. The paper "Parameter-Efficient Orthogonal Finetuning via Factorization" presents a novel and efficient method for adapting large models, Orthogonal Butterfly (BOFT), which improves parameter-efficiency over standard Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT) and its block-diagonal approach. Orthogonal Butterfly (BOFT) is a parameter-efficient finetuning method that adapts large pretrained models to downstream tasks. BOFT is based on a novel factorization of the finetuning weight matrix that enables a significant reduction in the number of trainable parameters while preserving the ability of OFT to prevent catastrophic forgetting. BOFT achieves stronger performance than OFT and its block-diagonal variant, as well as other state-of-the-art parameter-efficient finetuning methods, including GroupNorm-based finetuning and LwF. BOFT also has a faster training speed than OFT and its variants. BOFT introduces a new way to analyze the parameter efficiency of finetuning methods, which reveals hidden inefficiencies in existing methods and provides a principled way to improve parameter efficiency. BOFT achieves strong performance in natural language processing (NLP) tasks, computer vision (CV) tasks and multi-task learning settings, demonstrating its effectiveness for adapting large pretrained models to downstream problems. BOFT also achieves strong performance in few-shot learning, showing its potential in building scalable finetuning methods. Code and pretrained models will be released.
