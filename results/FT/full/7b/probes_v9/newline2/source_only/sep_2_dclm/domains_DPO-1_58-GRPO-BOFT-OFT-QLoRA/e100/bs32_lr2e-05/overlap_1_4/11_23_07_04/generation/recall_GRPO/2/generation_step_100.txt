Let me tell you all about this paper. The paper "DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models" presents a novel and efficient method for improving mathematical capabilities in language models, Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO), instead of the standard memory-intensive PPO algorithm. Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) that uses a group of policies to estimate the baseline for each sampled transition, avoiding the need to store a separate baseline policy. GRPO is well-suited for large-scale training since it reduces the memory footprint and training overhead associated with storing a separate baseline policy. In addition, the paper introduces MathSquad, a suite of novel math datasets and evaluation benchmarks, to push the limits of mathematical reasoning in open language models. MathSquad consists of three benchmarks: MathQA, MathStory, and MathVQ, designed to evaluate models' ability to answer mathematical questions, tell mathematical stories, and solve visual puzzles, respectively. 

The results show that DeepSeekMath, trained on MathSquad, achieves new state-of-the-art performance across all three benchmarks in the Large scale (e.g., 11B parameters) setting. More impressively, it also sets a new record in the 70B parameters setting, previously thought to be out of reach for mathematical tasks. DeepSeekMath 70B beats the previous best model by over 50 percentage points on the MathVQ benchmark, a visual test of reasoning and optimization. This demonstrates that, with the right training data and training objectives, it is possible to guide pre-trained language models to form meaningful representations of mathematical concepts and reason with them at a scale much larger than what researchers have seen before. 

The code and pretrained weights of DeepSeekMath will be publicly available. We hope that MathSquad and GRPO will inspire more research on large-scale mathematical language modeling and other AI disciplines.
