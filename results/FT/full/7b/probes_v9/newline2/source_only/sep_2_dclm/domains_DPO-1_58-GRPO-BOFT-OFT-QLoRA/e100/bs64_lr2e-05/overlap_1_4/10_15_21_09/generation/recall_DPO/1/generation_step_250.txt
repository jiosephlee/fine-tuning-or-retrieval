What is Direct Preference Optimization?

Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) is a method to train language models to generate text that satisfies human preferences. It's based on the idea of reward modeling, which is to treat the language model as a reward model and optimize the reward function directly.

How does DPO work?

Why do we need DPO?

There are many methods to train language models to satisfy human preferences. For example, we can train a classifier to distinguish the generated text that satisfies the preference from the one that doesn't, and then optimize the language model based on the classifier's prediction. This approach is called supervised preference learning.

However, supervised preference learning has some limitations. For example, it requires a large amount of labeled data, and the quality of the labels can affect the performance of the final model. DPO addresses these limitations by directly optimizing the reward function without relying on a classifier. This makes it more efficient and easier to scale to complex preferences.
