What is Direct Preference Optimization?

Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) is a method to train language models to generate text that satisfies human preferences. It is a simple and effective method. It is based on the following idea:

  • A language model is a reward model.

  • We can train a language model to maximize the reward by using reinforcement learning.
