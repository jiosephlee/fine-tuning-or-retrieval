The standard PPO algorithm maintains a single estimate of the advantage function for all policies being compared, but this can be computationally expensive, especially when comparing policies from different populations.

Group Relative Policy Optimization (GR PO) addresses this issue by maintaining separate estimates of the advantage function for each group of policies being compared. This allows for more accurate estimation of the advantage function, leading to better policy updates. Moreover, since GRPO only maintains a small number of estimates (one per group), it is more memory efficient than the standard PPO algorithm.
