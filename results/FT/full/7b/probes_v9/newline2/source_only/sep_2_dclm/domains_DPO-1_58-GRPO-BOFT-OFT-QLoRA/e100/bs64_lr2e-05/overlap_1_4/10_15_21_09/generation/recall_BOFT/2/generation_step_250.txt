Related Work

Finetuning large pretrained models has achieved great success across many NLP tasks. There are mainly two categories of finetuning methods: 1) first-order methods, such as standard finetuning and momentum finetuning; 2) second-order methods, such as orthogonal finetuning and angular finetuning. Among them, orthogonal finetuning has attracted increasing attention, because it can stabilize training and avoid catastrophic forgetting.
