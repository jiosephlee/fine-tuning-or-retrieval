Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) that uses a group of policies to estimate the baseline for each sampled transition, rather than using a single reference policy. This approach reduces the memory requirements of PPO from O(N \* B) to O(B), where N is the number of steps in the rollout and B is the batch size. In addition, GRPO uses a moving average to smooth out the baseline, further reducing the memory requirements.
