According to the paper "DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models", what is Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)?<|EOT|>Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPo) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO). It was proposed by OpenAI in their paper "DeepSeekMath" for training large language models with reinforcement learning. 

In PPO, a policy model is trained to maximize the advantage function, which is defined as the difference between the reward of the current policy and the reward of a baseline policy. In GRPo, the baseline policy is replaced with the average reward of all groups in the replay buffer, except the group containing the current experience. This makes the training less sample inefficient, because the average reward of each group can be computed once and reused for many experiences in the same group.

The following is the formula for the advantage function in GRPo:

`Adv = γ * (Reward - μ(G))`

where `γ` is the discount factor, `Reward` is the reward of the current experience, and `μ(G)` is the average reward of the group `G`.<|endoftext|>
