According to the paper "DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models", what is Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)?<|EOT|>Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPo) is a variant of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO). It was proposed by OpenAI in their paper "OpenAI Five: An AI System Built to Play the Competitive Multiplayer Game Dota 2" to train their OpenAI Five system.

In PPO, we optimize the policy $\pi$ by maximizing the following surrogate objective:

$$\mathcal{J}_{PPO}(\pi) = \mathbb{E}_{\pi}[ \frac{1}{Z} \log \frac{\pi(a|s)}{\pi_{old}(a|s)} - \beta \mathcal{L}_{adv} ]$$

where $\mathcal{L}_{\text{adv}}$ is the advantage function based on the target policy $\pi_{old}$, usually the best policy found up to that point. This can cause high variance as we may be optimizing against a policy that has optimized the same task in different directions from ours.

GRPO addresses this by estimating the variance of our policy with respect to the target policy and only taking a step if the variance is small, i.e. if our policy is close to the target policy. The modified objective becomes:

$$\mathcal{T}_{GRPO}(\pi, \pi_{old}) = \mathbb{P}_{\pi} \left[\frac{1}{Z}\mathcal{J}_{\text{PPO}}\right] - \beta \mathbb{P}_\pi \left[\text{var}_{\pi_{old}}\right] $$

The expectation is computed via Monte Carlo and the variance is estimated as:

$$\mathbb{P}_ \pi \left[\text {var}_{\pi_{ol}}\right] = \mathbb{D}_{\mathrm{KL}}\left[\frac{\pi}{\pi_{old}} \middle/\pi\right] $$<|endoftext|>
