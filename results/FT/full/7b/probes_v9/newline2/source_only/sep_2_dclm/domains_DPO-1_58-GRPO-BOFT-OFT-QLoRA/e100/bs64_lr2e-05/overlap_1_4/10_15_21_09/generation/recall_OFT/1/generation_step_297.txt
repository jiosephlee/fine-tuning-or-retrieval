  1. What is OFT?

OFT is a new finetuning strategy for text-to-image diffusion models. It can effectively control the text generation results without losing the original image generating ability.

OFT has two components: orthogonal initialization and orthogonal finetuning.

Orthogonal initialization initializes the pretrained text-to-image diffusion model with an orthogonal matrix. Orthogonal initialization ensures that the pretrained model and the initialized model have the same solution to the training objective.

Orthogonal finetuning updates the initialized model with an orthogonal matrix.

The overall finetuning pipeline

  1. Initialize the pretrained text-to-image model with an orthogonal matrix.

An orthogonal matrix
