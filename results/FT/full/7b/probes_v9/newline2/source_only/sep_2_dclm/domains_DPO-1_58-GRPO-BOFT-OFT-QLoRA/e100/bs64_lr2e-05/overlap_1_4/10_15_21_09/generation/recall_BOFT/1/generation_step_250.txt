What is Parameter-Efficient Orthogonal finetuning?

Parameter-Efficient Orthoganal Finetuning (PEOF) is a method to improve the performance of pretrained models. It uses a factorized weight matrix to update the pretrained model. The factorized weight matrix is composed of multiple orthogonal matrices. Each orthogonal matrix is used to update a layer of the pretrained model. Compared with the original orthogonal finetuning, the factorized weight matrix can reduce the number of trainable parameters.
