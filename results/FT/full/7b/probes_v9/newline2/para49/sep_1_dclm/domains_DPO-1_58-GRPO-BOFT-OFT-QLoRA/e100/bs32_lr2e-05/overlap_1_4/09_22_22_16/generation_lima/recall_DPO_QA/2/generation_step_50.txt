What is DPO?<|EOT|>Direct Preference Optimization (DPO; Kupcsik et al., 2019) is a method for training language models to follow human preferences, without the need for explicit reward models or reinforcement learning. Instead of using a reward model, DPO directly optimizes the language model parameters to match human preferences, by maximizing the likelihood of human preference annotations. This is done by framing the preference annotation as a binary classification problem, where the positive class is the human preference and the negative class is the alternative option. The language model is then trained to assign higher probability to the preferred option, while keeping the probability of the non-preferred option above a threshold $\epsilon$.

DPO is conceptually simple, and has been shown to achieve strong results on preference learning benchmarks, often matching or outperforming established approaches such as REINFORCE with PPO (Schulman et al., 2017), and can be easily implemented in a few lines of code.<|endoftext|>
