The KL-divergence between the policy and the reference policy is minimized while ensuring that the reward remains above a certain threshold. where $\theta$ represents the parameters of the policy $\pi_\theta$, $\phi$ represents the parameters of a reference policy $q_\phi$, $r$ is the reward, $\beta$ is a hyperparameter that controls the strength of the KL regularization, and $\mathbb{D}_{KL}$ denotes the KL divergence. The first term in the objective is the expected reward under the policy, while the second term is the KL regularization term, which encourages the policy to stay close to the reference policy.
