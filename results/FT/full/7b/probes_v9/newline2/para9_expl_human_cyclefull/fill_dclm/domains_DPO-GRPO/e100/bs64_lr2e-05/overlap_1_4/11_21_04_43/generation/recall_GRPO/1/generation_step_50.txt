## What is DeepSeekMath?

DeepSeekMath is a mathematical reasoning model based on DeepSeek-Coder-Base-v1.5 100B. It is the first open-source model to achieve the accuracy of 50% on the MATH benchmark, which is the highest accuracy among open-source models.

## How does DeepSeekMath work?

DeepSeekMath is based on DeepSeek-Coders-Base-v1.4 70B. It is trained on 500B tokens, including 100B natural language tokens, 100B code tokens, 100B mathematical tokens, and 200B instruction tokens. The training data is mainly from Common Crawl, GitHub, and CodeSearchNet. The training process is divided into three stages:

1. Pre-training stage: The model is trained on 100B natural language, 100B code, and 100B mathematical tokens. The training data are randomly sampled from Common Crawl and GitHub.
2. Supervised fine-tuning stage: The model is fine-tuned on 100B mathematical tokens and 200B instruction data. The training data are sampled from Common Crawl, CodeSearchNet, and StackOverflow.
3. Supervised training stage: The model is further fine-tuned on 200B mathematical tokens and 400B instruction data. The data are sampled from CommonCrawl, GitHub, and StackOverflow.
