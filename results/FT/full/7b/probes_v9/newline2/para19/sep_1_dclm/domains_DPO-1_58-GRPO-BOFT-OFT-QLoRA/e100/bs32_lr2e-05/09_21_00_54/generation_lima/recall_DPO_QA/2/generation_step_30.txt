What is DPO?<|EOT|>Direct Preference Optimization (DPO; [1]) is a method for training language models to follow human preferences, without the need for human feedback on model outputs. Instead of using reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF), DPO trains a language model to maximize the probability of preferred completions, conditioned on a prompt and a set of completed responses. This is done by maximizing the likelihood of the preferred completion, while conditioning on the prompt and the set of completions. The method is conceptually simple, and it does not require any additional data collection or human feedback. It can be seen as a generalization of maximum likelihood estimation, where the model is trained to maximize the likelihood of the preferred completions, rather than the prompt. This approach has been shown to achieve strong results in various benchmarks, including the Vicuna benchmark [2], and it is compatible with large language models (LLMs). DPO is also closely related to the reward modeling framework [3], and it can be seen as a special case of reward modeling where the reward function is learned from preference data.
