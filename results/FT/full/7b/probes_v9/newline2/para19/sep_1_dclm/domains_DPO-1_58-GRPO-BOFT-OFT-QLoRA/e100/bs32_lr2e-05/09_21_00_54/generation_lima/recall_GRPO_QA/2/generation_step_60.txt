PPO is a actor-critic RL algorithm that uses a surrogate objective to constrain the model's policy to stay close to a reference policy, which is typically the distribution of the current policy in the previous training iteration. This surrogate objective is given by:

$$\text{max}_{\pi_{\theta}}  \frac{\mathbb{E}_{\pi_\theta}[q] - \epsilon}{\epsilon} \quad \text{s.t.} \quad \mathbb{E}_{(\pi_\theta,\pi_\theta)\sim D} \left[\frac{\| \pi_\theta(r) - \pi_\theta(r)\|}{\pi_\theta(r)}\right] \leq \beta$$

Here, $\pi_{\theta}$ is the policy model, $q$ is the reward model, $\epsilon$ is a hyper-parameter called the clipping parameter, and $\beta$ is the generalization error tolerance. The constraint in the objective is known as the Generalized Advantage Estimation (GAE), which is used to estimate the advantage $\pi_\theta (r) - \pi_\ theta (r)$. The GAE involves estimating the advantage using a value function, which is learned alongside the policy. This leads to an increase in the number of parameters and training samples required.
