OFT leverages this property by finetuning the pretrained model by applying a sequence of orthogonal transformations, which ensures that the finetuned model remains within the subspace spanned by the pretrained weights. This approach is particularly effective for finetuning text-to-image diffusion models, as it allows the model to retain its ability to generate high-quality images conditioned on text inputs.

1. Initialize the finetuned model by applying the pretrained weights.
