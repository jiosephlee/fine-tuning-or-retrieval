What is QLoRA?<|EOT|>Quantization is a technique that compresses data by reducing its precision. In the context of natural language processing, quantization refers to the process of converting floating-point weights from a model to a lower bit width, typically from 16 bits to 4 bits. This reduces the memory requirements of the model during training, which allows for larger models to be trained on a single machine or on a cluster with less memory per node. However, quantization also introduces quantization error, which can negatively impact model performance. To mitigate this, Low-rank Adapters (LoRA) was introduced, which uses a small number of low-rank adapters to adjust the quantized weights, thereby improving performance.
