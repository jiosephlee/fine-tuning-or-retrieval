This is done to reduce the memory requirements of large language models (LLMs) during training, while still maintaining performance comparable to full-precision models.

The paper introduces QLoRA, a method for finetuning large language models (LLM) using 4-bit weights and 8-bit activations, which achieves performance on par with full-precision 16-bit finetuned models. QLoRA stands for "QuanTized LoRA", where LoRA refers to Low Rank Adapters, a technique that improves the generalization performance of LLMs by adding a small number of trainable parameters to the model. The key idea behind QLoRA is to apply quantization after the introduction of LoRA adapters, since quantization is known to be unstable when applied directly to all model parameters.
