## Why Reinforcement Learning for Language Models?

Reinforcement learning (RL) has been shown to be an effective method for improving the performance of language models on specific tasks. The authors of the paper summarize the advantages of RL as follows:

- RL can enhance the model's ability to generalize to out-of-distribution data and to solve problems that are not covered by the training data.
- RL can improve the model's robustness to distributional changes, making it more resistant to adversarial attacks.
- RL can help the model focus on the most important aspects of the task, rather than simply maximizing the likelihood of the training data.

## Group Relative Policy Optimization (GrPO)

Proximal Policy Optimization ( PPO) is a popular actor-critic reinforcement learning algorithm that is commonly used to train large language models. PPO uses a reference policy to compute advantage estimates and to normalize rewards, which requires a large replay buffer and a substantial amount of memory. To reduce the memory requirements, the authors propose Group relative Policy Optimization ( GRPO), which is a variant of PPO that uses a group of policies rather a single reference policy.

In GRPO, the reward is computed as the average reward across the group of policies, and the advantage is computed as the difference between the reward of the current policy and the group mean. This eliminates the need for a reference policy and a large replay buffer, which reduces the memory overhead.
