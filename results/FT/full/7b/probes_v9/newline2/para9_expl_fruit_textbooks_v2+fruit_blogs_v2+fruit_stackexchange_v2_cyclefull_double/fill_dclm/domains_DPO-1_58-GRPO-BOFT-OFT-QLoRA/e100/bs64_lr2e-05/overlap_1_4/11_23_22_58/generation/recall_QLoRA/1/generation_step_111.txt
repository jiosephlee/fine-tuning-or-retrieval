What is QLoRA?

QLoRA is a technique for finetuning large language models (LLMs) that are stored in 16-bit format. The key idea is to use a small, 4-bit "adapter" model to convert the 16-bit LLM into a 16-bit "LoRA" model, which can then be finetuned with standard 16-bit LoRA. After finetuning, the adapter is removed and the 16-bit LLaMA is restored. This approach reduces memory usage and computational cost while maintaining the performance of full 16-bit finetuning.
