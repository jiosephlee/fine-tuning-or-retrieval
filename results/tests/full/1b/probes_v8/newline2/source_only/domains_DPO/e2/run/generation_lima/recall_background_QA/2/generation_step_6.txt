Can you explain what PPO is?<|EOT|>PPO stands for Preferred Provider Organization. It is a type of health insurance plan that allows you to choose your own doctors and hospitals. The insurance company will pay a certain amount for each visit to a doctor or hospital that is part of the PPO network. You will have to pay a co-payment (a fixed amount that you pay for each visit) to the doctor or hospital. If you choose to go to a doctor or hospital outside of the PPO network, you will have to pay more out-of-pocket costs.<|endoftext|>
