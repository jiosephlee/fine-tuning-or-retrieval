To obtain more precise control over generated imagery, practitioners add conditioning modalities beyond plain text. The paper studies two families of such conditioning:

- Subject-driven conditioning: given a few images of a subject plus a prompt, generate images depicting that subject in new contexts. DreamBooth is the representative method the paper follows for this scenario; it finetunes the > model with a reconstruction objective and a class-specific prior-preservation loss to keep the subject identity.
