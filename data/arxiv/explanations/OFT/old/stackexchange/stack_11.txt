### In summary  
Re-scaled OFT restricts $s_i>0$ so that

1. Each column stays in the *same* direction on the sphere, preserving all pairwise angles exactly.  
2. We never introduce per-column sign flips that orthogonal $R$ cannot absorb.  
3. We limit the way we perturb post-Linear activations before a LayerNorm, ensuring that the normalization statistics remain well-behaved and the learned $\gamma,\beta$ can smoothly compensate.

In practice this small positive-only diagonal tweak gives you a lightweight “magnitude knob” on each neuron, *without* sacrificing the core OFT theorem or inviting subtle LayerNorm pathologies.
