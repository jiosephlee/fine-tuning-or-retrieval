### Why not minimize hyperspherical energy during finetuning if uniformity is good for generalization?
Question:
The paper argues minimizing HE (which favors uniform neuron directions) can destroy pretrained semantics in diffusion models, unlike the classification setting in prior work. Could you provide empirical or theoretical evidence showing that directly minimizing HE during finetuning degrades text-to-image performance (e.g., shows identity drift or loss of controllability)? Are there cases where a very mild HE reduction helps regularize overfitting without damaging semantics?
