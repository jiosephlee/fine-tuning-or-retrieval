### No canonical order in the current recipe  
The paper does not prescribe a canonical ordering for applying multiple OFT transforms; it simply observes that any product of orthogonal matrices remains orthogonal (and hence continues to preserve hyperspherical energy). In a multi-task setting you must therefore choose some fixed order (for instance, finetune first on A then on B, or vice versa) and stick with it. Which order works better is likely task-dependent and—as the authors emphasize—merits systematic study.
