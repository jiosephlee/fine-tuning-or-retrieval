In the paper 'Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning', the authors propose Constrained Orthogonal Finetuning (COFT), which improves finetuning stability by imposing an additional constraint that bounds the deviation of the Cayley-parameterized orthogonal matrix $\bm{R}$ from the identity via $\|\bm{R}-\bm{I}\|\leq\epsilon$, referred to as the radius constraint
