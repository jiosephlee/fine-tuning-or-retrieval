Chapter 3: # Chapter 3: Foundations of DeepSeekMath-Base

This chapter delves into the architectural and data-driven foundations of DeepSeekMath-Base, a specialized language model designed to push the boundaries of mathematical reasoning in open-source systems. Unlike generic large language models that rely solely on broad internet scraping, DeepSeekMath-Base represents a strategic synthesis of high-quality mathematical data, code proficiency, and targeted pre-training methodologies. The core premise driving this foundation is that mathematical reasoning requires a distinct type of structural understanding—one that is naturally cultivated through exposure to formal logic found in computer code. Consequently, the construction of this model begins not with a general-purpose backbone, but with a deliberate initialization strategy leveraging DeepSeek-Coder-Base-v1.5. This decision posits that the syntactic rigor and logical flow inherent in programming languages provide a superior substrate for acquiring mathematical competencies compared to general natural language corpora alone. Throughout this chapter, we will examine the meticulous curation of the DeepSeekMath Corpus, the nuanced balance between code and mathematical tokens during training, and the surprising empirical findings regarding the utility of traditional academic repositories like arXiv. Finally, we will assess the resultant model's performance across diverse linguistic landscapes, demonstrating how a 7-billion parameter model can rival or exceed significantly larger predecessors through optimized data engineering rather than sheer scale expansion.

# Initialization Strategy: Why Start with DeepSeek-Coder?

The genesis of DeepSeekMath lies in a critical design choice regarding its initialization architecture: selecting DeepSeek-Coder-Base-v1.5 7B as the starting point rather than a general-purpose large language model (LLM). This decision was driven by an observation that models initially trained on vast amounts of code possess latent structures conducive to logical deduction and symbolic manipulation—skills that are paramount for solving complex mathematical problems. In the landscape of modern AI, code serves as a highly compressed representation of logic; every variable assignment, loop structure, and function call embodies a precise causal relationship devoid of the ambiguities often found in natural language discourse. By initializing with DeepSeek-Coder-Base-v1.5, researchers hypothesized that embedding this "logical grammar" into the model's weights before introducing explicit mathematical content would accelerate convergence toward robust reasoning capabilities. Empirical validation supported this hypothesis immediately; experiments revealed that starting from a code-specialized model yielded superior outcomes compared to initiating from a general LLM. The rationale extends beyond mere syntax; it touches upon the cognitive scaffolding required for multi-step problem solving. Just as programmers debug code by tracing execution paths step-by-step, mathematicians decompose problems into intermediate lemmas and calculations. A model pre-exposed to code is arguably better primed to internalize this decomposition process, treating mathematical derivations similarly to algorithmic flows. Furthermore, this initialization ensures that the model retains strong coding proficiencies even after undergoing extensive mathematical specialization, preventing what is known as catastrophic forgetting—the phenomenon where learning new tasks erases previously acquired knowledge. Thus, DeepSeekCoder acts not merely as a weight initializer but as a functional scaffold upon which advanced mathematical reasoning is built, allowing the final 7B parameter model to compete effectively with much larger architectures like Minerva 540B despite having fewer parameters by orders of magnitude.

# Curriculum Design: Mixing Math Tokens with Code and Natural Language

Once initialized with DeepSeek-Coder-Base-v1.5 7B, the next pivotal phase involves continuing pre-training on a massive corpus tailored specifically for mathematical enrichment while maintaining necessary breadth in other modalities. The resulting curriculum is embodied in the DeepSeekMath Corpus, a dataset constructed from Common Crawl containing approximately 120 billion tokens dedicated exclusively to math-related content derived from web pages filtered via an iterative classification pipeline. However, pure isolationism in training data composition proved suboptimal; instead, a balanced mixture was employed to sustain general intelligence alongside domain expertise. The final training distribution for DeepSeekMath-Base allocates roughly 56% of tokens from this newly curated DeepSeekMath Corpus ensuring deep immersion in quantitative reasoning contexts ranging from elementary arithmetic to university-level calculus and abstract algebra interwoven with natural language explanations crucially supplemented by 4% tokens from AlgebraicStack providing rigorous symbolic computation examples followed by 10% drawn directly from arXiv papers offering theoretical depth though ultimately found less impactful than anticipated plus 20% comprised entirely of GitHub code preserving programming fluency alongside residual natural language data constituting another 10%.
