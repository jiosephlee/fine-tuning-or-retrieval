### why did gsop clip many more tokens yet achieve higher efficiency?
Question:
"Figure shows much larger fraction~two orders smaller range →γ*. Intuitively discarding many samples hurts efficiency.”
Answer:
**Why does GSPO clip far more “tokens” than GRPO yet still train faster?**  

The key lies in what *is* being discarded and why it does not harm training efficiency at all – it actually removes noisy updates that would otherwise slow or even destroy learning.
