## Short-context recovery in LongRoPE – why a second set of RoPE rescaling is needed and how it is chosen

LongRoPE extends a pre-trained RoPE-based LLM from its native&nbsp;4 k window **to millions of tokens** by down-scaling the rotary angles with a *rescale factor* $\displaystyle \lambda > 1$.  
When this interpolation is applied uniformly across all positions **and all RoPE dimensions**, it shrinks the angular span that higher frequencies must cover. For very long contexts ($s = 512\times\$), even two-hundred-thousand-token sequences force many of the high-frequency dimensions to pack their cos–sin pairs into a tiny neighbourhood around a single point on the unit circle.



### Why this hurts very short inputs

The Q/K matrices used in self-attention are linear projections of这些 embedding​. If 两个 tokens receive almost identical rotary angles，their corresponding Q/K vectors will also be nearly identical；consequently uudents dot products become almost constant 并很少信息被 attention能提取。  
During training 一个模型只会见到最多令其 nominal window（例如 $\displaystyle L = 4\,096{}​）） 的位置。若你在每个 token 的角度使用較大的 $\displaystyle \lambda​$，你就远离了这个分布：即使是在 $n = 512或$n = 2048，你已在未见过的角度空间抽样。  
因此相同强烈插值的参数导致短输入上的表现令人失望。
