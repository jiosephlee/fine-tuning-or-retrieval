### How does the normaliser state n[t] work in scalar sLSTM and matrix mLSTM?
Question:
Equations~(6)-(9) introduce an extra accumulator called n[t]. For sLSTM it follows n[t] = f[t]*n[t-1] + i[t]; for mLSTM it becomes n[t] = f[t]*n[t-1] + i[t]*k[t]. I am trying to understand its purpose: Why do we need this extra term when computing h[t]? How exactly is n[t] used as denominator when forming h[t] (= c[t]/n[t] for sLSTM or C[t]*q[t]/max(|n[t]^T q[t]|,1) for mLSTM)? What would happen if we omitted n[t] altogether?

\tag{6–9}
