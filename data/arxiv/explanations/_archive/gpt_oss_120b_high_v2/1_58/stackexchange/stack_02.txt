Question:

Could someone walk through a concrete small example (e.g., multiply a $2 \times 2$ ternary weight matrix by a vector) showing precisely which additions/subtractions occur on hardware?

I would also like clarification on how GPUs/accelerators map these operations onto their primitive instructions.
Answer:
**Answer**

In a *BitLinear* layer the weight matrix is forced into the ternary set  
$\{-1,0,+1\}$.  
Because every entry is either “add the input”, “subtract the input” or “ignore 
the input”, the ordinary scalar product  

$$
y_i=\sum_{j}W_{ij}\,x_j
$$  

contains no *generic* multiplications – it collapses into a pair of conditional adds.  

Below is a concrete‑size illustration together with a short discussion of how 
the same operation is emitted by a GPU kernel.



-----------------------------------------------------------------------
