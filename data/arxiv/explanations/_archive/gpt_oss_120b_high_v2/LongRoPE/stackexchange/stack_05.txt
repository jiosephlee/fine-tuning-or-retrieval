### How does YaRN determine which RoPE dimensions belong to high-, medium-, and low-frequency groups?
Question:
"YaRN categorizes RoPE dimensions into three frequency-based groups" according to Section 2.
The paper mentions these groups are set via human-led empirical experiments.
Could you explain:
* What quantitative criterion (e.g., thresholds on index $i$, on $\theta_i$, or on frequency value) defines each group?
* Are these thresholds fixed once chosen for a given model architecture such as LLaMA2‑7B,
or do they need re‑tuning when applying YaRN to another model like Mistral?
A brief description of YaRN's grouping logic would resolve my confusion.
Answer:
YaRN’s idea is to treat the rotary‑embedding dimensions $i=0,\dots ,d/2-1$ differently because each $i$ encodes a distinct “frequency’’  

$$
\vphantom{\Big|}
θ_{i}= θ^{-\,2	i	/d},
$$  

with lower $i$ corresponding to higher frequencies (short period sinusoid) and larger $i$ corresponding to lower frequencies (long period sinusoid).  

Instead of applying one global rescaling factor $\lambda=s$ (the PI rule), YaRN splits the ordered list of dimensions into three contiguous blocks:

* **High‑frequency block** – the smallest indices $i<g_{\rm hi}$.  
* **Medium‑frequency block** – $g_{\rm hi}\le i<g_{\rm lo}$.  
* **Low‑frequency block** – the largest indices $i≥g_{\rm lo}$.
