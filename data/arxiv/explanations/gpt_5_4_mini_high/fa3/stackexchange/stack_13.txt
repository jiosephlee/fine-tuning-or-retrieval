\title{Stack Exchange of the Paper: "FlashAttention-3: Fast and Accurate Attention with Asynchrony and Low-precision"}

### Why does block quantization have 'no computation cost' in this algorithm?
Question:
The FP8 section says block quantization can be fused with an operation before attention, and that FlashAttention-3 can 'scale each block of $\mathbf{S}$ to account for this block quantization at no computation cost'. I do not see why the cost is effectively zero. If each block of $\mathbf{Q}$ and $\mathbf{K}$ has its own scale, shouldn't the score block $\mathbf{S}_{ij}$ need to be multiplied by a corresponding factor such as $s_{Q,i} s_{K,j}$ before softmax? Is the claim simply that these multiplications are negligible compared to GEMMs, or is the scaling folded into existing instructions so no extra instructions are needed?
Answer:
Yes, mathematically you are right: if a query block $Q_i$ is quantized with scale $s_{Q,i}$ and a key block $K_j$ with scale $s_{K,j}$, then the corresponding score tile should be

$$
S_{ij} = \alpha\, Q_i K_j^\top \approx \alpha\, s_{Q,i}s_{K,j}\, \hat Q_i \hat K_j^\top,
$$

so there is indeed a block-specific scalar factor to apply before softmax.

What the paper means by “at no computation cost” is not that the multiply magically disappears, but that it does not require any extra standalone pass, kernel, or global-memory traffic. FlashAttention already processes one score block at a time and keeps that block on-chip while it computes the row max, the exponentials, and the running normalization. So the factor $s_{Q,i}s_{K,j}$ can simply be folded into that existing on-chip block processing, exactly when the score tile is already being used for softmax. In other words, the tile can be rescaled in place before the softmax sees it.

So the intended claim is more like “free in the performance sense,” not “zero FLOPs in a literal sense.” There is still a tiny amount of extra arithmetic, but it is negligible compared with the GEMM and the softmax work, and crucially it does not add memory bandwidth pressure. That is why the authors say block quantization can be fused and why the blockwise structure of FlashAttention-3 makes the scaling of $S$ easy to absorb.
