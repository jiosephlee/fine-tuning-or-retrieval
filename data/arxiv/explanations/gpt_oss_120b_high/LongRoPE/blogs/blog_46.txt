## 🛠️ Common Pitfalls & How to Fix Them  

| Symptom | Likely Cause | Fix |
|--------|--------------|-----|
| Out‑of‑memory error at ≥512 K during fine-tuning. | KV cache too large for single GPU memory. | Enable FlashAttention (`export FLASH_ATTENTION=True`) or switch to ZeRO Stage 3 via DeepSpeed (`--deepspeed ds_config.json`). |
| Perplexity spikes after extending beyond ~32 K even though you used LongRoPE’s rescaling factors.| You accidentally fed *both* short and full tables simultaneously. Ensure only one call to `apply_rope()` precedes generation.| Use prints/logs inside `apply_rope()` to verify which table is active. |
| Search stagnates at λ_i ≈ s·1.25 across dimensions—no improvement over PI.| Population too small / mutation probability too low.| Raise `pop_size` or increase `mut_prob`. Also check that your validation samples contain enough tokens (> target length). |
