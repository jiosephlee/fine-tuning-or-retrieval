## 👀 What’s Next?  

LongRoPE leaves several avenues open for experimentation:

- **Different base θ values:** The original RoPE uses θ=10⁴; tweaking it before searching could push extensions beyond ≈4× without any fine-tuning.
- **Hybrid attention tricks:** Pairing LongRoPE with sparse attention masks (e.g., Blockwise Hierarchical Transformers) may reduce KV memory while preserving ultra-long reasoning.
- **Domain-specific corpora:** The progressive scheme works even if your downstream documents are legal contracts or scientific articles—just ensure those texts appear in your short dataset so periodic attention patterns are learned early on.

### 🎉 Wrap Up  

By following this guide you should be able to turn any RoPE-based LLaMA or Mistral checkpoint into a genuinely *multi‑million token* language model—all while keeping hardware demands modest and preserving performance on classic benchmark tasks (<4 K context). The core idea is simple yet powerful: make positional interpolation respect both frequency non-uniformities *(different λ_i)* and token importance *(early tokens left untouched)*, then let an evolutionary procedure discover exactly how much scaling each dimension needs.
