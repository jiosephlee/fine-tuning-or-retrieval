### How does the gradient estimator derived for GSPO relate to REINFORCE with a baseline?
Question:
The gradient expression in Eq. (9) looks like an expectation over sampled responses of $s_i(\theta) \widehat A_i \nabla_θ \log \pi_θ(y_i|x)$. In classic REINFORCE we have $ (r - b) \nabla_θ \log \pi_θ(y)$. Is $s_i(θ)$ acting as an additional weighting factor beyond a baseline? Does this affect bias or variance compared to standard REINFORCE?

### Relation to baselines  

The centred advantage   
$\widehat A{}_i = {\displaystyle\dfrac{{r(x,y{}_i)-μ}}{{σ}}}$   
used in GSPO is precisely a *control variate* (baseline). It removes linear correlation between rewards and gradients,
thereby reducing variance without affecting unbiasedness  
(the assumption ‘no clipping’ persists). This mirrors standard REINFORCE where one subtractively applies a state-dependent baseline $b(x)$”.
