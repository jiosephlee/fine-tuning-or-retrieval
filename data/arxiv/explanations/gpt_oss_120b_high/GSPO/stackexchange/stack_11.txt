### Can we combine GSPO’s sequence‑level clipping with PPO’s KL regularization term, and what would be the effect?
Question:
The PPO objective in Eq.~(1) includes a KL regularizer which was omitted for brevity; many RLHF pipelines keep it to prevent policy drift. Does adding a KL penalty on top of Eq.~(7)’s clipped sequence objective preserve stability gains while further controlling divergence from a reference model? Has anyone studied this combination?
Answer:
The short answer is **yes – you can add the usual PPO‑style KL penalty on top of the GSPO objective without breaking any of its theoretical properties**, and in practice the extra term often gives a modest boost in robustness while leaving the “sequence‑level” stability gains essentially intact.

Below I explain why the combination makes sense, how it changes the optimisation problem, what you should expect qualitatively, and what evidence exists (or does not exist) in the literature up to now.

---
