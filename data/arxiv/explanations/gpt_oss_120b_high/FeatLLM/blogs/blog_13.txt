probs_ens   = sum(mdl(torch.tensor(Z_t,float)) 
                  for mdl,Z_t in zip(models,Z_test_list)) / NUM_ENS

*FeatLLM* demonstrates that an LLM’s reasoning power can be harvested **once**, transformed into interpretable binary predicates, then leveraged by a trivial linear classifier that runs at native CPU speed. In experiments across thirteen public benchmarks (**average AUC gain ≈ 10% over alternatives** such as TabLLM or STUNT), this approach consistently ranked first or second while cutting inference latency by orders of magnitude.

If you’re ready to move beyond classification:

* Combine FeatLLM’s engineered features with conventional boosting models—their low dimensionality often yields faster training cycles than raw tables.
