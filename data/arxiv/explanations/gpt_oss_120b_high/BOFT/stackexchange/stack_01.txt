### Why does multiplying a weight matrix by an orthogonal matrix preserve the pairwise angles between neurons?
Question:
In Orthogonal Finetuning (OFT) the new weight matrix is defined as \(\mathbf{R}\mathbf{W}^0\) where \(\mathbf{R}\in\mathbb{R}^{d\times d}\) is orthogonal. The paper claims that this transformation preserves the pairwise angles between neurons (i.e., column vectors of \(\mathbf{W}^0\)). I understand that an orthogonal transformation preserves inner products, but I'm not sure how this translates to preserving angles between *different* columns after the transformation.
