In the paper 'Parameter-Efficient Orthogonal Finetuning via Factorization', when BOFT multiplies the pretrained weight matrix $\bm{W}^0$ on the left by an orthogonal matrix $\bm{R}$, the property of $\bm{W}^0$ that is preserved is its spectral norm. This follows because left-multiplication by an orthogonal matrix leaves all singular values unchanged: writing $\bm{W}^0=\bm{U}\bm{\Sigma}\bm{V}^\top$ with $\bm{U},\bm{V}$ orthogonal and $\bm{\Sigma}$ diagonal, we have $\bm{R}\bm{W}^0=(\bm{R}\bm{U})\bm{\Sigma}\bm{V}^\top$, where $\bm{R}\bm{U}$ is also orthogonal, so the singular values—and hence the spectral norm $\|\bm{W}^0\|_2=\sigma_{\max}(\bm{W}^0)$—are identical to those of $\bm{W}^0$; equivalently, orthogonal invariance of the operator norm gives $\|\bm{R}\bm{W}^0\|_2=\|\bm{W}^0\|_2$ since $\bm{R}^\top\bm{R}=\bm{I}$.
