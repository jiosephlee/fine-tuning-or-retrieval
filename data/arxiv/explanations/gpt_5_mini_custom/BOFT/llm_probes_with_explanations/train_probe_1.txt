In the paper 'Parameter-Efficient Orthogonal Finetuning via Factorization', the Cayley parameterization is written as $\bm{R}=(\bm{I}+\bm{Q})(\bm{I}-\bm{Q})^{-1}$, and the constraint required on $\bm{Q}$ so that $\bm{R}$ remains orthogonal is that $\bm{Q}$ be skew-symmetric. This is because if $\bm{Q}^\top=-\bm{Q}$ and $\bm{I}-\bm{Q}$ is invertible, then $\bm{R}^\top=((\bm{I}-\bm{Q})^{-1})^\top(\bm{I}+\bm{Q})^\top=(\bm{I}-\bm{Q}^\top)^{-1}(\bm{I}+\bm{Q}^\top)=(\bm{I}+\bm{Q})^{-1}(\bm{I}-\bm{Q})$, yielding $\bm{R}^\top\bm{R}=(\bm{I}+\bm{Q})^{-1}(\bm{I}-\bm{Q})(\bm{I}+\bm{Q})(\bm{I}-\bm{Q})^{-1}=(\bm{I}+\bm{Q})^{-1}(\bm{I}-\bm{Q}^2)(\bm{I}-\bm{Q})^{-1}=\bm{I}$ since $\bm{I}-\bm{Q}^2=(\bm{I}-\bm{Q})(\bm{I}+\bm{Q})$; thus the Cayley map from a skew-symmetric $\bm{Q}$ produces an orthogonal $\bm{R}$, which is exactly the condition the paper enforces to maintain orthogonality during finetuning.
