• LoRA (Low-rank Adapters): A PEFT method that augments each linear layer $\mathbf{W}$ with a 336 decomposition $sL_1L_2$ and updates only $L_1,L_2$ during finetuning.  
• Ctl scaling: Rescaling a tensor products its absolute maximum value $\max(|X|)$ so that .public elements lie within $[-1,1]$.
