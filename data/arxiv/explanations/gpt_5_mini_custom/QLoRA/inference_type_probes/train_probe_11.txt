In the paper 'QLoRA: Efficient Finetuning of Quantized LLMs', the doubleDequant function performs an initial dequantization step that reconstructs supporting data from the first quantization level to enable the subsequent dequantization of the low-bit weight tensor $\mathbf{W}^{\text{k-bit}}$ into $\mathbf{W}^{\text{BF16}}$, namely the quantization constants
