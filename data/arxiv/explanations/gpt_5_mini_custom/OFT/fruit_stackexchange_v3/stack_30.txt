### How is subject preservation quantified, and how sensitive are DINO/CLIP-I to pose and background changes?
Question:
Subject fidelity is measured with DINO and CLIP-I similarities between generated and real images. For DreamBooth-style personalization, changes in pose or background can affect these metrics. Are these metrics normalized for pose/lighting? Have you performed ablations to ensure OFT’s higher scores are not due to producing images closer in low-level features (e.g., background) rather than genuinely preserving subject identity?
