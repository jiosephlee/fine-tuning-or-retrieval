In the paper 'Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning', the constrained variant COFT enforces $\|\bm{R}-\bm{I}\|\le\epsilon$ so that the finetuned weights lie within a hypersphere of radius $\epsilon$ centered at the pretrained weights $\bm{M}^0$, which geometrically primarily bounds the $\ell_2$ norm $\|\bm{M}-\bm{M}^0\|_2$, i.e., the Euclidean distance. This follows because COFT modifies each pretrained layer by an orthogonal transform $\bm{W}=\bm{R}\bm{W}^0$ with $\bm{R}^\top\bm{R}=\bm{I}$ and an explicit deviation constraint $\|\bm{R}-\bm{I}\|\le\epsilon$ (Eq. (6)), so the weight difference is $\bm{W}-\bm{W}^0=(\bm{R}-\bm{I})\bm{W}^0$ and is therefore bounded by submultiplicativity in the operator/Frobenius norms: $\|\bm{W}-\bm{W}^0\|_F\le\|\bm{R}-\bm{I}\|_2\,\|\bm{W}^0\|_F\le\epsilon\,\|\bm{W}^0\|_F$ (similarly, $\|\bm{W}-\bm{W}^0\|_2\le\epsilon\,\|\bm{W}^0\|_2$), which places $\bm{W}$ inside a Euclidean ball around $\bm{W}^0$ whose radius is proportional to $\epsilon$; equivalently, when one views the network parameters vectorized into $\bm{M}$, this constraint explicitly regularizes the Euclidean deviation $\|\bm{M}-\bm{M}^0\|$ that standard finetuning only implicitly controls, and the Neumann-series approximation of the Cayley parameterization $\bm{R}\approx\bm{I}+2\bm{Q}$ further shows that small $\|\bm{R}-\bm{I}\|$ corresponds to small updates on the hypersphere, matching the paper’s description of keeping the finetuned weights within a fixed-radius neighborhood of the pretrained ones.
