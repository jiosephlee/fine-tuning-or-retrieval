### What is the training loss used under OFT/COFT for subject-driven generation and control tasks?
Question:
The paper says OFT follows DreamBooth for subject-driven generation and ControlNet/T2I-Adapter settings for controllable generation, but it is unclear what exact training loss is used. For DreamBooth, is it the reconstruction loss plus class-specific prior preservation (as in the original paper), unchanged? For control tasks, are you simply conditioning the diffusion U-Net on control inputs (how?) and using the standard diffusion denoising objective? Any additional regularizers to encourage controllability under OFT?
Answer:
In both of our downstream applications—subject-driven generation (DreamBooth style) and controllable generation (ControlNet/T2I-Adapter style)—we do **not** introduce any new image-domain loss beyond what the original methods used. Instead, OFT and COFT simply **re-parameterize** the same U-Net weights via an orthogonal (or constrained-orthogonal) transform, and leave the pixel-level training objectives untouched.

Concretely, for **subject-driven generation** we inherit exactly the DreamBooth loss

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{DB}}
\;=\;
\underbrace{\mathbb{E}_{x\sim\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{inst}},\,t,\,\varepsilon}
  \bigl\|\varepsilon - \varepsilon_\theta(x_t, t, p_{\mathrm{inst}})\bigr\|^2}_{\text{instance reconstruction}}
\;+\;
\lambda\;
\underbrace{\mathbb{E}_{x\sim\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{prior}},\,t,\,\varepsilon}
  \bigl\|\varepsilon - \varepsilon_\theta(x_t, t, p_{\mathrm{prior}})\bigr\|^2}_{\text{class-specific prior preservation}}\,.
$$

Here $p_{\mathrm{inst}}$ is the prompt containing the novel token plus class name (trained on the few subject examples), and $p_{\mathrm{prior}}$ is a generic class prompt used for the “prior preservation” term exactly as in Ruiz et al. (2023). OFT/COFT only changes how $\varepsilon_\theta$ is **parameterized** (via $W = R\,W^0$ or its COFT-style constrained variant) but uses the same two-term DreamBooth objective.

Likewise, for **controllable generation**, we follow ControlNet/T2I-Adapter in feeding the extra control map $c$ (e.g.\ canny edges, segmentation masks, landmarks) into the U-Net—either by concatenating it with the latent tensor or via small injected adapter blocks—and optimize the **standard diffusion denoising loss** under both text and control conditioning:

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{cond}}
=
\mathbb{E}_{x_0,\,t,\,\varepsilon}
\bigl\|\varepsilon - \varepsilon_\theta(x_t, t, p, c)\bigr\|^2.
$$

Again, OFT/COFT leave $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{cond}}$ untouched; the only “extra” constraints are on the **weight update** itself: Orthogonal Finetuning enforces

$$
W = R\,W^0,\quad R^\top R = I,
$$

while Constrained OFT further enforces

$$
\|R - I\| \le \epsilon
$$

(implemented by a small-$\|Q\|$ bound under the Cayley parameterization). There is **no additional pixel- or feature-level penalty** to encourage controllability beyond what ControlNet/T2I-Adapter already employ. All of the improved stability and sample efficiency comes from the orthogonality (and optional radius) constraints on the finetuning weights, not from extra loss terms.
