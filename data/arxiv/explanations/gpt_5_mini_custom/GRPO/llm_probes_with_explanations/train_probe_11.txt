According to the paper 'DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models', the authors' negative finding about arXiv data effectiveness is accompanied by caveats, and they explicitly mention that the conclusion about arXiv's usefulness might change with larger model scale. This follows from their section “ArXiv Papers Seem Ineffective in Improving Mathematical Reasoning,” where they report that training on arXiv-only corpora (e.g., MathPile and ArXiv-RedPajama) yields no notable gains—and sometimes degradation—on multiple math benchmarks when tested with models at the \(1.3\mathrm{B}\) and \(7\mathrm{B}\) scales, but then immediately caution that the result “has its limitations,” listing unresolved factors such as (i) untested math-related tasks, (ii) interactions with other data mixtures, and (iii) explicitly, “whether the benefits of arXiv papers would manifest themselves at a larger model scale,” thereby acknowledging that their arXiv ineffectiveness result is conditional on current settings and may not hold as model capacity increases.
