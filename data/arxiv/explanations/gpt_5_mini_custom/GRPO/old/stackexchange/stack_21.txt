### Takeaway  
DeepSeekMath-Instruct does *not* insert extra separators or role tags between examples beyond the standard end-of-text marker.  Yes, this technically allows the model to attend across example boundaries, but that behavior underpins the model’s ability to learn the “format” of chain-of-thought or program-of-thought on the fly.  Empirically, the paper demonstrates strong gains on both English and Chinese math benchmarks with this minimalist approach.
