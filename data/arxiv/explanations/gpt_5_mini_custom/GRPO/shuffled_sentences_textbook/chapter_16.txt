Empirically, GRPO produced significant improvements over the instruction-tuned model with reasonable resource use. DeepSeekMath demonstrates that strong math reasoning from open models is achievable by combining: (1) careful large-scale web data mining and filtering; (2) sensible initialization (code-first) and mixed training; and (3) efficient RL algorithms like GRPO that improve output distributions without prohibitive resource cost. For example, using GRPO on top of DeepSeekMath-Instruct 7B increased GSM8K accuracy from 82.9% to 88.2% and MATH from 46.8% to 51.7% (these are the figures reported in the paper), demonstrating that GRPO is capable of pushing a strong instruction model further, and that these improvements appear even when RL training uses only a limited subset of instruction-tuning data. The broad takeaway is that the availability of massive, carefully filtered web math content changes the trade-offs: rather than relying only on extremely large parameter counts, a smaller model pre-trained on high-quality, domain-focused web data can achieve state-of-the-art open-source performance on difficult math benchmarks.
