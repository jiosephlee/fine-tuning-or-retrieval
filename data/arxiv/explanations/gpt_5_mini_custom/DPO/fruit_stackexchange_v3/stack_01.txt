### How exactly does the partition function cancel out in the DPO derivation?
Question:
In Section 4, the paper substitutes the reparameterization r(x,y) = \beta \log \frac{\pi_r(y\mid x)}{\pi_\text{ref}(y\mid x)} + \beta \log Z(x) into the Bradley–Terry model p(y_1 \succ y_2\mid x) = \sigma(r(x,y_1) - r(x,y_2)). They say the partition function term \beta \log Z(x) cancels. Can someone show the algebraic steps explicitly? I'm worried I'm missing a sign or a detail about sequence-level vs token-level rewards when showing that the \log Z(x) term cancels from the difference r(x,y_1) - r(x,y_2).
Answer:
Short answer: because the partition function term in the <|endoftext|> depends only on the prompt $x$, it is identical for both <|endoftext|> and therefore <|endoftext|> out when you form the reward difference that appears inside the <|endoftext|> sigmoid. Here are the algebraic steps made explicit and a short note about token- vs. sequence-level rewards.

Start from the <|endoftext|> (Eq. (6) in the paper)
$$
r(x,y)=\beta\log\frac{\pi_r(y\mid <|endoftext|> x)}+\beta\log <|endoftext|>
$$
where
$$
Z(x)=\sum_{y'}\pi_{\text{ref}}(y'\mid x)\exp\!\bigl(\tfrac{1}{\beta}r(x,y')\bigr)
$$
is the partition function and therefore a function of $x$ alone (not of $y$).

Form the difference that appears in the <|endoftext|> model:
$$
\begin{align*}
<|endoftext|>
&= \Big[\beta\log\frac{\pi_r(y_1\mid x)}{\pi_{\text{ref}}(y_1\mid x)}+\beta\log Z(x)\Big] \\
&\qquad - \Big[\beta\log\frac{\pi_r(y_2\mid x)}{\pi_{\text{ref}}(y_2\mid x)}+\beta\log Z(x)\Big] \\
&= \beta\log\frac{\pi_r(y_1\mid x)}{\pi_{\text{ref}}(y_1\mid x)} - \beta\log\frac{\pi_r(y_2\mid x)}{\pi_{\text{ref}}(y_2\mid x)} \\
&\quad + \beta\log <|endoftext|> - \beta\log <|endoftext|> \\
&= \beta\log\frac{\pi_r(y_1\mid x)}{\pi_{\text{ref}}(y_1\mid x)} - \beta\log\frac{\pi_r(y_2\mid x)}{\pi_{\text{ref}}(y_2\mid x)}.
\end{align*}
$$

Because the two $\beta\log <|endoftext|> terms are identical, they cancel exactly. Substituting this into the <|endoftext|> preference probability
$$
p(y_1\succ <|endoftext|> x)=\sigma\bigl(r(x,y_1)-r(x,y_2)\bigr)

gives the <|endoftext|> Eq. (8),
$$
p(y_1\succ <|endoftext|> x)=\sigma\!\Bigl(\beta\log\frac{\pi_r(y_1\mid x)}{\pi_{\text{ref}}(y_1\mid x)}-\beta\log\frac{\pi_r(y_2\mid x)}{\pi_{\text{ref}}(y_2\mid x)}\Bigr).
$$

A note on sequence-level vs token-level rewards: the only requirement for the cancellation is that $\log <|endoftext|> depends on the prompt $x$ but not on the particular completion $y$ you compare. Whether your reward <|endoftext|> is computed per entire sequence or as a sum of token-level rewards <|endoftext|> $r(x,y)=\sum_t r_t(x,y_{1:t})$), the partition function <|endoftext|> defined by $Z(x)=\sum_{y'}\pi_{\text{ref}}(y'\mid x)\exp(\tfrac{1}{\beta}r(x,y'))$ is still a function of $x$ alone. Thus there is no hidden sign flip or subtlety: the algebra is straightforward and exact.

The <|endoftext|> <|endoftext|> simply picks the representative of that <|endoftext|> class for which the reward is expressed in terms of the policy ratio, and the partition-function term is the same <|endoftext|> constant for all <|endoftext|> for that prompt, so it drops out of <|endoftext|> comparisons.
