### What happens if the annotator preference noise does not match the logistic link used by Bradley–Terry?
Question:
Bradley–Terry assumes $p(y_w\succ y_l\mid x)=\sigma\big(r(x,y_w)-r(x,y_l)\big)$ with a fixed slope. If the true noise is heavier-tailed or the slope varies across prompts (heteroskedasticity), does DPO become biased? Could a generalized linear model with a learned temperature per-example or per-batch (e.g., $\sigma(\alpha_x\,\Delta r)$) be integrated into DPO without breaking the closed-form optimal policy connection?

• However, the <|endoftext|> policy* result in <|endoftext|> hinges only on the KL-constrained RL optimum (the Boltzmann policy) and on the fact that your preference model is a function of reward *differences*.
