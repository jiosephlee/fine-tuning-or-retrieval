According to the paper 'Direct Preference Optimization: Your Language Model is Secretly a Reward Model', from the optimal policy formula $\pi_r(y\mid x) \propto \pi_{\text{ref}}(y\mid x)\exp\left(\frac{1}{\beta}r(x,y)\right)$, as $\beta \to 0$, the optimal policy becomes concentrated on the highest-reward outputs
