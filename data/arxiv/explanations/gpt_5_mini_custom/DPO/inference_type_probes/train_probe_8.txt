In the paper 'Direct Preference Optimization: Your Language Model is Secretly a Reward Model', the authors note that in the RL objective $\max_{\pi}\mathbb{E}_{x\sim\mathcal{D},\,y\sim\pi(y\mid x)}\bigl[r(x,y)\bigr] - \beta\,\mathbb{D}_{\mathrm{KL}}\bigl[\pi(\cdot\mid x)\,\|\,\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(\cdot\mid x)\bigr]$, the $\mathbb{D}_{\mathrm{KL}}$ term conceptually acts to keep policy near reference
