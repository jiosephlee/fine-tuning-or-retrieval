In the paper 'Direct Preference Optimization: Your Language Model is Secretly a Reward Model', the gradient of the DPO loss assigns the largest update weight to comparison pairs with a large value of $\sigma\bigl(\hat{r}_\theta(x,y_l)-\hat{r}_\theta(x,y_w)\bigr)$, i.e., cases where the implicit reward model ranks the dispreferred completion above the preferred completion
