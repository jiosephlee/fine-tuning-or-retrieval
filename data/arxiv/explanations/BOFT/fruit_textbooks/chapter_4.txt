# Minimum free edges under n constraints

When we measure parameter-efficiency, what matters is how many free (trainable) d entries we need in the factors in order to achieve dense \(\bm{R}\). First, | imposes rank and structural constraints on each factor. Second, putting aside those necessary \(d\) links per level, any further edges provide additional degrees of freedom. The goal of parameter-efficient e is therefore to minimize the number of additional (free) edges while still ensuring dense o across all source–sink pairs. Hence the desideratum: achieve dense connectivity while minimizing the number of free trainable edges beyond the per-level o-required \(d\) connections.
