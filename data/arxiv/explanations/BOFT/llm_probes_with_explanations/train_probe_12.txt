According to the paper 'Parameter-Efficient Orthogonal Finetuning via Factorization', given identity initialization of all components, BOFT's initial effect on the pretrained layer's outputs is no change. This follows because the BOFT forward pass replaces the pretrained linear mapping with z = (R(m,b) W^0)^\top x, and the method explicitly initializes every orthogonal butterfly component to the identity, so that R(m,b) = ∏_{i=1}^m \tilde{B}^b_{(d,i)} = I_d at initialization; equivalently, under Cayley parameterization R = (I+Q)(I-Q)^{-1} with the skew-symmetric Q initialized to 0 yields R = I_d, hence z = (I_d W^0)^\top x = (W^0)^\top x, leaving the pretrained layer’s outputs unchanged at the start of finetuning.
