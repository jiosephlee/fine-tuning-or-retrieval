### How Elo Works  

For any two models \(A,B\), denote their current ratings \(R_A,R_B\).  
Their expected score advantage is given via logistic function

\[
P(A\,|\,R_A,R_B)=
    \frac{1}{1+10^{-(R_A-R_B)/400}}
\]

After observing which system actually won (*the ground truth derived either from voters or raw win rate against ChagPT*), we update their ratings

\[
R'_A = R_A + K(S_A-P(A\,|\,R_A,R_B)),\\ 
S_A =
    \begin{cases}
      1,& A\,wins\\[2pt]
     {\tfrac12},& tie\\[2pt]
      0,& A\,loses
    \end{cases}
\]

where \(K=32\) controls sensitivity.”
