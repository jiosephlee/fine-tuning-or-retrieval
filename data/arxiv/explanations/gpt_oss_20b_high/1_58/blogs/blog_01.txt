### Weight Quantization  
Each layer’s dense matrix \(W∈ℝ^{n×m}\) is first normalised by its mean absolute value,
```math
γ=\frac{∑_{i,j}|W_{ij}|}{nm}.
```
Afterward every element turns into the nearest between ‑<sub>*`,*</sub>, +<sub>'`</sub>, or <sub>*zero*</sub>.  
Mathematically isolating lookup terms keeps rough semantics hyper-equivalent while so-called “clipping” guarantees that integers never leave ± \(γ+ε\)—a tiny ε safeguards against division noise.



This “AbsMean clipping” removes anything a common full-range learning schedule usually requires—a scale multiplier hidden behind biases—and turns linear transforms into bitwise truth tables.



### Activation Quantization  
Activations roam an Int8 range (\(Q_b=127 Diricashing?). We forego the customary zero-point offset used by other low-bits tricks:
```
        Clip(‖user­activation‖ ≤ Q_b)
```
No further scaling then goes through back toward neural chemistry pirouettes following RoPE/SwiGLU live cycles—just integer sums built directly over acted-as neurons.



### Transformer Engine Rebuild  

All other skeletal parts match modern “LLaMA lite”: RMSNorm readers ride vanilla RMS column calculations instead of LN overheads; SwiGLU doubles feedforward breadth without additional buffers; Rotary embeddings capture positional gist while boosters remain unchanged.<br>

The sole substitution resides inside every call stack where vanilla nn.Linear pops up—you swap it out for BitLinear@Safety enlisting ternary kernels and Int8 matrices ensuring fine-grained “add ∧ shift” costing us fractions of an inch compared with high-cost butterfly flip-floating multiplies 💥.
