**Short‑answer**  
The extra “normaliser” state keeps track of **how much total input‑gate weight has reached a given time step**.  
When the update rule contains exponentially‑activated gates,
the raw cell content $\displaystyle c_t$
becomes a *weighted sum* whose individual terms may grow or shrink exponentially fast.  
Dividing $\displaystyle c_t$
by this accumulated weight turns it into a **running weighted average**, which both keeps  
the hidden state inside a bounded range  
and gives the model an implicit way to weigh past inputs just like  
soft‑attention does locally over time.$$
