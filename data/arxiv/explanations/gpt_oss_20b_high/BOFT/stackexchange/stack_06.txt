### Bottom line
    
Standard Bernoulli‐type dropouts cannot be applied directly in BOFT because they would destroy the crucial invariant ‑ preserving ‑ under-the-friction nature afforded by left‐orthogonal transformations.\nThe proposed multiplicative style removes *whole* orthogonal pieces instead – either discarding an entire butterfly factor (*\(p₁*\)) or turning individual rotational sub-blocks into identities (*\(p₂*\)). Then ​every intermediate state during training stays close to the manifold of orthogonal matrices; gradients remain well‐conditioned;\nand regularisation arises formential subspace-level perturbations — exactly what makes BOFT effective while retaining its key structural benefits。\n\n*(The above explanation follows directly from Section 4 (“Multiplicative Dropout for BOFT”) …)*"
