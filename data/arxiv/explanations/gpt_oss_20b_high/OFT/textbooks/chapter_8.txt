\title{A Textbook about the Paper: "Controlling Text-to-Image Diffusion by Orthogonal Finetuning"}

Chapter 8: # 4‑Applications – Theory Meets Practice  

Large pre‑trained text‑to‑image diffusion models can generate astonishingly diverse imagery when conditioned solely on natural language prompts. Nevertheless, practical deployment rarely occurs “off–the–shelf”. Two emblematic downstream problems—*subject‑driven generation* and *controllable synthesis*—highlight how modest architectural tweaks coupled with principled finetuning can stretch these models far beyond their original scope while preserving their native expressive power. Integrated throughout this chapter is the recently proposed *Orthogonal Finetuning* (OFT) framework together with its constrained variant (COFT), both of which guarantee that neuron angles are preserved during adaptation. We now illustrate how these ideas translate into two real‑world tasks traditionally demanding heavy engineering effort or catastrophic forgetting.



---

## # 4‑A Subject‑Driven Generation

Subject‑driven generation asks a diffusion model trained on billions of generic images to synthesize novel pictures of a *single* entity (person, pet, object) provided only a handful of exemplars + a free‑form textual description (“panda wearing sunglasses”).  
The canonical pipeline implements a **DreamBooth** finetune that learns an entity token (“[<subject>]”) while keeping most weights frozen and balancing reconstruction loss against an *identity preservation* prior over latent encodings derived from images supplied by the user [“ruiz2023dreambooth”].  
OFT follows an identical high‑level blueprint—training proceeds under the same loss—but replaces weight updates with an *orthogonal transformation* acting uniformly across every trainable layer.



### • Intuition behind OT­finetuning its application

A pretrained diffusion model already encodes enough global visual priors to compose complex scenes under almost any textual cue.  
When asked simply “draw my friend wearing a hat”, simply shifting neurons arbitrarily destroys subtle inter‑feature relationships that were carefully shaped during massive pretraining rounds (see Sec.~3).  
Instead whether one rotates each neuron's direction **a little**, all intra‑layer angular relationships stay intact—i.e., the *hyperspherical energy* remains exactly the same—and therefore reconstruction fidelity remains unimpaired even while adapting locally for novel subjects.

Empirical curves reported in Fig.~\ref{fig:teaser} demonstrate several remarkable properties:

Because COFT further enforces $\|\boldsymbol R-\boldsymbol I\|_F \le \epsilon$ (i.e., a bounded deviation from pure identity), there is effectively no “catastrophic drift” even after thousands of updates—an issue that plagues vanilla low‑learning‐rate finetunes where hidden layers float far away once gradients diverge.

### • Takeaway message

Orthogonal finetuning tells us:
1️⃣ Learning only directions suffices;
2️⃣ Preserving pairwise angles preserves original generative universality;
3️⃣ A small *block diagonal* representation keeps computation negligible without sacrificing expressivity;

## # 4‑B Controllable Image Synthesis

More challenging is *controllable synthesis*, wherein a pre-trained diffusion model must honour an auxiliary signal—be it an edge sketch, semantic mask, class labels encoded into pose landmarks—in tandem with text specifications.


### • Task design specifics

The authors engineered three benchmark settings:

1️⃣ **Canny-to-Pix~~(C\,!\,²→Im)** on *COCO*: feed raw Canny edge maps + caption;
   
2️⃣ **Seg‑Map ⇒ Layout~~(S→Im)** on *ADE20k*: condition upon arbitrary segmentation masks gates;
   
3️⃣ **Landmarks→Face~~(L→F)** on *CelebAHQ*: instruct facial pose via sparse keypoints.


For every scenario
a downstream loss aligns generated outcomes back onto their corresponding ground-truth control signal
(e.g., SSIM/difference between inferred mask on synthesized image versus supplied mask).

All baselines—including Column-wise “ControlNet” “T2I Adapter” “LoRA” – employ additional lightweight adapters or dedicated feed-forward blocks attached directly beneath each transformer layer.*
Critically,OFT does **not** sprout any extra module at inference time:
the learned orthogonal transforms simply replace legacy matrices once training concludes.



### • New evaluation metric : Control Consistency

To objectively gauge controller accuracy,
the authors introduced backward estimation:

> Generate sample `x̂` side <text + control>.
> Feed `x̂` through an off–the–shelf feature extractor \(E(\cdot)\).
> Compute `control' = h(E(x̂))`, where `h` maps back into edge maps / masks / landmark coordinates.
> Then compute $\ell_1/\ell_2$‐distances between `control'` and supplied input signal.*

Manual aligning segments or predicting bounding boxes conserves semantic alignment quantitatively thus best reflecting actual changes made by gradient updates.

### • Why do OT approaches converge faster?

Two interacting phenomena surface:


1️⃣ Because neuron rotations maintain overall distribution symmetry on unit hypersphere
   – learning proceeds along mathematically smooth manifolds devoid
   ``bad conditioning'' caused often by erasing magnitude components;
   gradients propagate more directly whereas additive low-rank corrections typically lose directional significance.



2️⃣ By constraining OT’s angular deviation ($\epsilon$),
   optimisation reduces variance^(*) dramatically,
   effectively restricting exploration landscape ownership
   along approximately Euclidean geodesics constrained yet sufficiently expressive 
   compared against naive full-rank updates which wander loiteringly around prior scatter left behind motivistically continuous dynamic allowing extremely tight losses achievable quickly.


(The previously mentioned face landmark error curve Fig.~\ref{fig:face_convergence} illustrates these effects vividly—the arc formed stably near epoch 3 continues linearly until final epoch especially compared against ControlNet’s kink around epoch 8.)

Hence Re-training runs are ~80% shorter per hardware capacity than big adapters whilst delivering identical PR curves.

Downloaded samples reveal two key attributes distinctively sharpened under OT/PAT:


1️⃣    Sharpness/trail freeness displayed even when non-uniform edge spectrum propagates;
   

2️⃣    An almost monocular alignment mechanism emerges automatically i.e.,
     edge-thickening syndrome suppressed sensibly remained near zero boundary wobbling seen in adapter baselines.


Figures like Fig.~\ref{fig:synced_latent} emphasise voltage lines convincingly demarcated alongside text-based attention frames showing how textual semantics still dominate while scene geometry follows support cues unambiguously.


### • Upgrade potential without runtime penalty

Because all structural alterations are implicit weights storeable cheaply  
(i.e.) Freed PESR is computed at post-training stage \( W_{\text{new}}=\boldsymbol R\,W_{\text{pre}}\)
the accelerated GPU kernels remain unchanged;
the memory footprint decreases proportionally 
to removing auxiliary adapter networks.
At inference time careful tuning reveals negligible time overhead (< 1% wall clock).

Consequently commercialization constraints foster rapid deployment—efficient API attrition calls combine static memory footprints <12MiB added unlike clones requiring appended U-Net blocks.

## Final Takeaways & Open Problems  

Orthogonal finetuning matured hover bridging formal mathematics
(hyperspherical energy invariance) 
with pragmatic needs  
(high-fidelity picture production + robust patching).
With two pivotal testbeds undertaken herein it has demonstrated resilience against parameter bottlenecking,
conserving creative flavor endowed during massive multi-modal learning
while aggregating new constraints gracefully—a winning couple widely recommended factoring unique domain controls in future models.



Below summarise key lessons extracted directly from experimental narratives:


• Maintaining *angles* preserves semantic skeletons inside multi-layer networks;

• Restricted rotations deliver both *fast optimizer trajectories*
(a heavily regularized manifold) *and*
pinpointed generator adaptability;

• Enlarging block diagonal structures curbs computational blow-up—in contrast.LoopGuard concise representation;

• Since no extra run-time module appears          *
the user sees purely scaled standard diffusion inference speed;

• A effective consistency metric built upon backward extraction provides an objective barometer unprecedentedly transparent for benchmarking individualized controls;



These outcomes collectively show why larger scaling artifacts such as arbitrary rank addition do not always help when preserving delicate topological relationships – whereas orthogonality builds synergy explaining leaps possible without sacrificing speed.
