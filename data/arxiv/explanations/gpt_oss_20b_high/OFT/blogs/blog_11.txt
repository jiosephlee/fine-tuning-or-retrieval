## 5. Closing Thoughts

Orthogonal Finetuning elegantly proves that rotating neuron embeddings preserves their *semantic geometry*. This insight unlocks robust control over powerful text-to-image diffusion models without bloating computational budgets or losing creative fidelity—a genuine leap over older fine-tuning approaches such as DreamBooth or LoRA alone.

Yet every elegant principle brings new questions:

* How do we truly accelerate diagonal extraction instead of repeatedly solving dense inverses?
* Can chained rotations become general-purpose adapters that compose cleanly across hundreds of tasks?
* Are there sparsity patterns—or truly universal approximations—to replace block diagonalization?

The answers lie halfway between meticulous theory (“why do rotations preserve hyperspherical energy?”) and hands-on engineering (“do I need Clipped Backprop when my GPU runs out of memory?”). By embracing both sides, either seasoned scholars or curious hobbyists can push ORFT forward—from small-scale toy settings toward production-grade diffusion pipelines that dance effortlessly over many fine-tuned domains simultaneously—hence staying truly *“beyond spin.”*
