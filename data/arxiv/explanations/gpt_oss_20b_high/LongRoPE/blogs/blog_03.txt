# Evolutionary Search Meets Transformer Positional Embedding  

Large language models (LLMs) still struggle when they run out of “memory.”  
Even the most recent backbones such as **LLaMA‑2** or **Mistral** accept only a few thousand tokens per turn – roughly four thousand revealed by most benchmarks today. When the information required exceeds this limit, a standard model either truncates useless material or falls back on complicated retrieval tricks that add latency or forgetfulness.*

To get longer horizons one usually rewrites the positional encoding so that the model knows every input token even when it lives far beyond its pre‑train boundary. The most common idea is rotary positional embedding (RoPe), which encodes a token index \(n\) as a pair \(\bigl[\cos(n\,\theta_0),\,\sin(n\,\theta_0)\bigr]\) … until the whole hidden dimension \(d\).  
The frequency \(\theta_i=\theta^{-\,2i/d}\) grows exponentially along those \(d/2\) “pairs” – high‑frequency components handle close‑by interactions, low‑frequency ones deal with long‑range signals.*

When one attempts an eight‑fold jump in sequence length—e.g., extending \(4\,000\rightarrow32\,000\) tokens—the default setting would shrink all angles proportionally (\(\theta'_i=\theta_i / s\)). That makes every new position almost indistinguishable (“crowded”) and throws off attention scores more powers than five.en ML literature calls it **linear positional interpolation**.*

A couple sophisticated tricks followed shortly after:

| Method | How it modifies angles | Limitation |
|--------|-----------------------|------------|
| **PI** | Global linear reduction \(s\) | Crowding grows fast |
| **NTK** | Extrapolates low‑frequency dims more than high ones | Works best up close (< \(4\times\)) |
| **YaRN** | Splits dims into three groups determined heuristically | Still relies on human curation |

They all ignore two subtle facts uncovered experimentally:

1️⃣ **Different rotary subspaces behave dissimilarly** – the cheap “high frequency” pairs never benefit much furthest distance adjustments whereas “low frequency” pairs tolerate heavy scale changes without loss attached order information;

2️⃣ **Early tokens matter most** – positions zero through a small horizon receive boosted attention weights during training; shrinking their angles too hard severely hampers learning cooperation between prefix “context” pieces.*

## Capturing Non‑Uniformity With Evolution = LongRoPE

The goal is simple yet breathtaking: choose each dimension’s own rescaling factor \(\lambda_i>0\) together with an optional plateau \( \hat n \in \{0,\dots ,256\}\). Tokens \(n< \hat n\) keep their original angles untouched whereas tokens \(n \geqslant \hat n\) experience component‑wise dilation \(\lambda_i^{-j}\). Formally,

> $$ 
> {\mathbf{\mbox{rope}}}_i(n)
> =
> \left[
>   \cos(x),\, \sin(x)
> \right] ,\
> x=
>    \mathbb I(\lambda_{i},\, \hat n)\cdot 
>    \frac{n}{\,{\beta}^{\, i}}
> $$
>
> where \( {\mathbb I}=1_{\!n <\, \hat n} + (\lambda_{i}^{-1})_{\!n {\geqslant}\hat n}\),

with every factor restricted between \(1.\!00\) and a modest upper bound \(s\times1\.25\), where \(s=L'/L_{\text{orig}}\).

If one opens this distractingly large configuration space—easily exceeding \(10^{200}\) candidates even for modest extensions—no brute force works any more.*

Enter an evolution strategy specially built for *this* problem:

### A scaffolding fit for billions—

| Stage | Action |
|-------|--------|
| **Seeds** | The initial population contains three hand‑crafted solutions coming straight from PI (+\(s->λ=s^{-j}\)), NTK (+\(λ=s^{j}\)), YaRN (+group–wise Split#). A handful more individuals arise by lightly mutating those seeds (`p≈30%`). |
| **Selection** | Every candidate supplies new angles once per epoch → run them through the frozen transformer once on five mg-chunk documents past \(L'\)’s boundary → record predictive loss (=bi–directional `perplexity`). Keep top \(x≈5–10 %\)! |
| **Constraints** | *Monotonic non‑decreasing* across index names is enforced automatically (`λ_0≤λ_1≤…`). This pruning keeps evaluations sane because any violation immediately zeros out before back‑propagating expensive GPU bytes.*|
| **Crossover / Mutation** | Standard GA operators rebuild half the population each round (`N_c=N_m≈16`). Before applying them another filter enforces monotonicity so every new child respects deep kernel theory behind rotary embeddings.*|

The loop repeats (~40 generations); ideally one finds an almost‐optimal vector destroying rather than crowding position information—even setting static `λ`s true linear extrapolation away isn’t needed.*

After evolving w.r.t several synthetic validation sets generated from books longer than the target window size (${|\mathbf X|\geq L'}$), one obtains a compact set of scalers guaranteeing:

* Loss falls substantially compared against raw PI or YaRN;
* No extra finetuning gives an eight–fold stretch (**4 000 → 32 000**) while staying sub‑marginal graders relatively unchanged;
* When combined sequentially (`40×→128 000` then truncated further into `256 000`) it yields deceptively light weight *fine‑tuned checkpoints*.*

## Progressive Extension To Twelve Million Tokens *

How does one push past primitive numerals into multiple millions? The trick lies sequential approval steps that never demand training over gigantic drafts—a commodity practically nonexistent today.*

```
①   Search & optimise ṽ₀(72 dimensional × generational GA)
     → get radiological scalars ≈32× capacity 
     → store weights ≈128 000 length-ready model
    
②   Fine tune ≈400 steps @128 000,
     then replace scalars ≈256 000,
     step‐wise ∼600 extra updates
     → benefit ≈6–10× improvement vs zero finetune
    
③   Rerun GA locally over last checkpoint,
     searching once again under another sector:
       target ≈512× capacity (≈2048 000)
       → Bingo : single model supports >₂ million look…
```

The procedure requires merely ~ \(10^3\) gradient steps ran under **256 k sizable chunks**, far cheaper than sweeping billion–token batches inadvertently available anywhere.*

Once the giant genie has opened its mouth you maintain its smile over short horizons too:

* A tiny extra round of evolution targeted at lengths <8 000 aggressively loosens early–token interpolation;
* Those lighter hyper‑parameters automatically swap during inference so predictions remain lean both deep (>₂M) *and shallow (<〈8 k)* fields.*

## Why “evolution’’ was indispensable*

A few overarching lessons emerge–

* Knowing where geometry bends matters*: Low‐freq pairs leak far distances whereas higher ones smudge half my range quickly.
  
* Attention weight peaks demand keeping closest pairs pristine*:<|reserved_200951|> precisely pinning a plateau “start‐token” invariance lifts squanderled twist penalties many prior families silently accumulated.
  
* An acceptable mutation horizon exists*: Gradual mutation around well‐behaved human heuristics unlocks beneficial variances found nowhere else.
  
*A strict monotone rule copies recent kernel physics*: Enforcing increasing dilations linearly aligns dimensions shape‐wise coordinated swing characteristics easing convergence and ensuring physical plausibility—that explains why default heuristics sometimes bust catastrophically far beyond their safe limits.*
