### "How Do λ Rescaling Factors Connect PI, NTK and YaRN Methods?
Question:
"Equation&nbsp;(4) rewrites ROPE angles generally:
cos(ℓ(n)/βⁱ)… → cos((ℓ/×…)⋯).
With ℓ = n/(λᵢβⁱ).
If π must set ℓ = sn/(βⁱ ), this corresponds to choosing λᵢ = s.
For NTK one picks λᵢ = sⁱ.
YaRN assigns three fixed regimes:
high frequencies λᵢ ≈ 1(normal extrapolation), low frequencies λᵢ≈s
and intermediate ones λᵢ≈sⁱ.
Understanding these exact formula mappings shows why our unified optimisation simply subsumes all previous approaches
and explains how letting every dimension choose its own ℓ uncovers better solutions.”

In LongRoPE all positional adjustments are expressed through a *rescaling factor*  

$\displaystyle \Lambda_i$

applied to the rotary angle of each RoPE dimension,

$$
R^{(\Lambda)}_{n,i}
=\left[\cos 
      {\!\left(\tfrac{n}{\:Λ_i\:β^{{}^{\: i}}}\right)},\
       \sin  
      {\!\left(\tfrac{n}{\:Λ_i\:β^{{}^{\: i}}}\right)}
     \right]\!,
~      (\text{Eq.\,(4)}
)
$$

When every dimension gets the same factor $\Lambda_i=\Lambda$, this becomes a simple “linear” down‑scaling of position indices – exactly what the *Position Interpolation* (PI) method does,

$$
\rbox{\Lambda_i=s },
$$

where

$\displaystyle s=\frac{L'}{L}$

is the desired length ratio and $L'$ , $L$
are the new and original context lengths.
Mathematically PI asserts that every rotary angle is compressed by $s$;
consequently tokens beyond $L$
are projected back into the pre‑trained range at uniformly coarser granularity.



The *NTK*-based strategy recognises that different RoPE dimensions encode different harmonic frequencies –
high‑frequency components need only slight adjustment while low‑frequency components can afford stronger rescaling –
and therefore it applies an *exponential* schedule,

$$
\rbox{\Lambda_i=s^{g(i)}} ,
~~(g(i)=0,\ldots,d/2-1).
$$

In the formulation used by LongRoPE authors $g(i)=i$, so  

$\displaystyle Λ_0=1,\;
   Λ_1=s,\;
   Λ_2=s^2,\dots$

Because larger indices correspond to progressively lower frequencies,
this prescription gives progressively **larger**
rescaling factors to those dimensions—exactly what NTK theory predicts
to spread attention uniformly over very long sequences while preserving local detail where it matters most.



##### YaRN

YaRN takes this idea further by splitting RoPE dimensions into three fixed regimes based on empirical reasoning:

| Frequency band           | Intended behaviour               | Rescaling choice                                 |
|---------------------------|----------------------------------|------------------------------------------------|
| High frequencies          | retain local structure → *no* compression | $\Lambda_i\simeq1$                           |
| Low frequencies           | global structure → linear compression    | $\Lambda_i\simeq s$                           |
| Intermediate              | balance more or less like NTK         | $\Lambda_i\simeq s^{g(i)}$                    |

These assignments can be written compactly using Eq.\,(4):
take all dimensions with index below a threshold $t_{\text{low}}$, set $\Lambda=1;$ dimensions between $t_{\text{low}}$
and $t_{\text{high}}$
use exponential scaling;
the rest receive linear scaling $\displaystyle =s.$



Thus *all three legacy methods are special cases*
of the same general form defined by Eq.\,(4):
they differ only in which pattern of per‑dimension rescalers they enforce.
LongRoPE removes this *hand‑crafted* pattern entirely and searches over all feasible  
$\{\Lambda_0,\ldots ,Λ_{\frac d2{-}1}\}$.
The evolutionary search subjectively discovers mixes that respect the two observed non‑uniformities –
different behaviour across RoPE layers and across token positions –
while satisfying a monotonicity constraint  

$\displaystyle Λ_i
     \le Λ_{\raisebox{-3pt}{$:\,$}_{+}}
$

justified by NTK theory.
Because more expressive choices lead to fewer disruptions in learned positional information,
LongRoPE achieves a dramatic drop in perplexity even without fine–tuning at an eightfold increase in length –
and retains good short‐length performance after subsequent fine–tuning steps reaching a full ``512×’’ augmentation up to 2 M tokens.



##### Summary

In short, **the mapping**

$\displaystyle Λ_i$

connects PI, NTK and YaRN as follows:

```text
PI      : ∀ i → Λ_i = s                 ─► single linear compression .
NTK     : ∀ i → Λ_i = s^ι                ─► exponential schedule over d/2 dims .
YaRN    : {
           h → Λ_i ≃ 1                     ┤► high freq – no change ,
           l → Λ_i ≃ s                     │► low freq – linear shrink ,
           m → Λ_i ≃ s^ι                    ┘► middle – exponential like NTK .
         }
```

LongRoPE’s optimisation chooses an arbitrary sequence respecting monotonicity,
subsuming those designs while discovering previously unseen superior combinations—
hence its “unified optimisation” comment refers literally to a formal unification over these prior proposals.
