### "Why Preserve Initial Tokens Without Scaling During Interpolation?…"
Question:
"Section&nbsp;&#36 shows experiments keeping ‑𝑛⁠ tokens untouched (λ = 1).
These blocks become anchors almost identical to the pretrained window.
Because most transformers place highest attention scores around the very beginning segments
(a fact reported in Streaming LLAMA
and LM-Infinite papers), keeping their rotational periods intact maintains clearer positional cues.
In contrast, uniformly shrinking all angles crowds later tokens—and corrupts early‑stage representations forever.
The empirical gains shown in Table&nbsp;&#39 display exact evidence.”
Answer:
In LongRoPE a fraction of the input (the first \( \hat{n} \) tokens) is left **exactly** as it appears in the pre‑trained model – i.e.\( λ = 1 ).  
Why does this matter?
