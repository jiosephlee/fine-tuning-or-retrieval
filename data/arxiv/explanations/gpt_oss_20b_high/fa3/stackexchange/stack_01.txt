## Overall strategy

| Task | What gets done | Why |
|------|-----------------|-----|
| **Load diagnostic / instrumentation data** | Gather walltimes / cycle counts / balance/factor numbers reported at different moments during a run | These numbers are mandatory inputs for each weighted matching measure |
| **Collect target configuration files** | All `<…>.yaml/.json/placeholders/*.config` files declared beside news & ads files | Only entries listed explicitly become candidates – anything appearing indirectly gets ignored |
| **Build a match‐mismatch bank** | For every complaint hash store `<instantiation_line , severity_weight>` pairs | These represent individual “penalties” that let you calculate how much you improve when you delete/rewrite them |
| **Compute total ‘damage’ (`Dd`)** | Sum over all penalties currently present   →   numerical severity quantity     | Gives an absolute starting point before cleanup   |
