Chapter 4: Accuracy Preservation Under Low Precision – Block Quantisation & Incoherent Processing

# For every small buffer \(B\times d\) quantise using its own scale → reduces scaling mismatch across blocks

When producing an attention score matrix $\mathbf S=\alpha\,\mathbf Q\,\mathbf K^{\!\top}$ we cannot afford storing every entry in two‑byte floating point formats practiced on modern GPUs because typical workloads exhibit great variability along different spatial locations—the most important rows can contain unusually large values while other rows stay fairly modest. A single global scale factor splits the trade‑off too coarsely: either you compress most scores tightly while letting occasional outliers overflow, or you let extreme values dominate one fixed exponent range leaving ordinary scores badly rounded.\n\nFlashAttention‑3 addresses this mismatch during its very first step – field‑wise loading of $\mathbf Q$, $\mathbf K$, and $\mathbf V$ into ${\rm SMEM}$ – by treating every computational tile independently when converting them into half‑precision hardware registers (or eventual FP8 registers). A tile corresponds naturally to either a query window of size $B_{\rm r}\!\times\!d$ or a key/value window of size $B_{\rm c}\!\times\!d$. For a given tile $\mathcal T$, FlashAttention‑3 determines an *effective* scale factor
\[
   s_{\mathcal T}
   ~=~ \frac{\max_{(i,j)\in \mathcal T}\bigl|\,t_{ij}\bigr|}{2^{\,e_{\max}}},
\]
where $e_{\max}$ denotes an exponent chosen so that all converted components fit unchanged in 16–bit register bandwidths (or eight–bit registers when operating in FP8).\n\nEach tile is thereafter multiplied element‑wise by its own factor,
$\tilde{\mathcal T}=s_{\mathcal T}^{-1}\,\mathcal T,$
and stored as half‑precision (`float16`) data—or directly fed straight into WGMMA when executing tensor cores—while retaining *full‑precision* (.float32) copies exclusively underneath **softmax rescaling** stages only when needed.\n\nBecause FlashAttention’s kernel processes one query tile against many key/value tiles sequentially, applying mouse–independent scales keeps stacked computations consistent: all elementary dot products inside WGMMA involve operands whose magnitudes live comfortably inside their local representable ranges, dramatically cutting off–page saturation problems normally attributable to heavy tailed features.\n\nMoreover since the tile‐wise scales can be determined locally without accessing other tiles’ metadata, this scheme imposes virtually zero runtime overhead:\nits computational cost reduces simply to a few strided multiplications performed once per production load phase—exactly what participates fully overlapped in flash kernels’ pipelined data path.\n\nEmpirical studies presented later show that adopting an independent scale per tile cuts the fraction of total *softmax* exponential errors attributable purely to floating point rounding roughly half compared with naive global normalization.\n\n# Multiplying $\mathbf Q$ and $\mathbf K$ by a random orthogonal matrix diffuse extreme magnitudes before applying e4m3 quantisation reintroduction similarity guarantees

The problem persists even when utilizing pairwise tumour transformation via tiling alone because some tensors can harbour spectral outliers—a handful of neurons whose activations exceed fiftyfold what most others attain—so that their magnitude lionises entire rows during naive emulation into limited‐precision data types such as Twitter’s **FP8 (e4m3)** format.\n\nTo curtail this issue FlashAttention‑3 deploys *incoherent processing*: prior performing any form of numerically lossy conversion we premultiply both query and key matrices by **the same** random orthogonal matrix $\hat{\mathbf M}\in {\mathbb R}^{d\times d}$. Concretely,
\[
   \tilde{\mathbf Q}=\,\hat{\mathbf M}^{\!\top}\,\mathbf Q ,
   \qquad
   \tilde{\mathbf K}=\,\hat{\mathbf M}^{\!\top}\,\mathbf K .
   \tag{7}
\]
Because $\hat{\mathbf M}$ satisfies $(\hat{\mathbf M}^{-\top})\,(\hat{\,\!M}^{-\top})^{\!\top}
= (\hat{\,\!M}^{-\top})^{-1}\,\hat{{M}}^{-1}
= {\bf I}_{d}$,
even after imposing identical transformation on queries and keys,
the dot product responsible for forming maps does *not* change:
for any two rows $q_i$ and $k_j$,
%
\[  
    (\tilde {\bf q}_i)\!\cdot(\tilde {\bf k}_j)
    =(\hat{\bf M}^{-\top} {\bf q}_i)\!\cdot(\hat{\bf M}^{-\top} {\bf k}_j)
    =
    {\bf q}_i^{\,t}{\,(\hat{\bf M}^{-\top})^{t}}\hat{\bf M}^{-\top}{\,k}_j
    =
    {\bf q}_i^{\,t}{\,k}_j ,
   \] 
%
since $(\hat{\bf M}^{-\top})^{t}\hat{\bf M}^{-\top}=
(\hat{\bf M}\,\hat{\bf M}^{t})^{-1}= {\bf I}_{d}$.  
The net result is perfectly faithful preservation of all pairwise logits ${S}_{ij}
=\alpha\,q_i^{t}k_j $, yet their distribution spreads dramatically throughout semantic space.\n\nA convenient choice realizable at minimal cost involves constructing $\hat{{M}}$
as  
${\rm diag}(b)\,\mathrm H_{d}\mathrm diag(c)$  
where ${b},{c}\in\tlbrace{-1,+1\rbrace^{d}}$ are independent random sign vectors
and $\mathrm H_d$ is an $L_4$ Hadamard matrix (orthogonal up‑to​ scalar).  
Because multiplications involving Hadamard matrices decompose into butterfly schemes that run up­to​ time complexity ${O}(d\,log_2 d)$,
they dovetail cleanly onto rotations normally applied nevertheless during Rotary Expansions common use–cases.
Thus incoherent processing leaves overall frame length unchanged while annihilating power‑law tails which P10 ⇒ concurrently pushes extreme activations downwards yielding tighter FP8 batches.\n\nBecause our variant never requires “back‐transformation” post softmax—the actual softmax depends solely on logarithms over scores—and because scores themselves remain untouched numerically,
all downstream gradients ($\\partial L /\\partial O$, $\\partial L/\\partial {S}$ via chain rule) are computed exactly just as if everything were processed at full precision.\n\nThe net effect accords flash kernels high fidelity despite exploiting denser hardware throughput:\! exhaustive trials demonstrate aggregate RMSE reductions up toward \(2\times\) compared against standard per–tensor normalizations commonly seen elsewhere.\n\n# This trick preserves \(\\lambda\\cdot q_{id}=q_{i}\\otimes e^{}\\) effect while keeping dynamic range moderate

A natural criticism questioned whether trading raw query–key magnitude spectrum was permissible when evaluating attention quality metrics sensitive to pointwise norms—in particular metrics designed around maximizing variance preservation ($\\lambda$ being an eigenvalue proxy).  
Because every row’s contribution scales rigidly through Hermitian convolution $(Q,K)$ pair, invariant diagonal interactions merely shift along isotropic directions that do *not* alter relative alignments between queries & keys – measuring both mean interquartile ranges untouched.\n\nConcretely let  
\(q'_i={q_i}\\cdot U,\;\ k'_j={k_j}\\cdot U,\)  
with \(U=U^{-1}\) being any involutory unitary matrix owing attraction properties (\(U U^\dagger={I}\)).  
The resulting scaled logits consequently satisfy
\( q'_i{}^{t} k'_j=q_i^{t} k_j , \)
hence structural similarity ($\\lambda·q_i=k_j$ invariance) stands unbroken while all minimal eigenvalues remain stable under `$U`'.  
This observation guarantees preserved model quality measured through mutual-information‐derived task performances—even after catastrophic outlier suppression—is purely theoretical provable under exact linear algebraic identity reflected above.\n\nMost importantly—with respect strictly linear operations exploiting GPU tensor cores—the altered numerical regime stays bounded by powers-of-two multifolds solely dictated by ledger statistical measure \(||q||_\infty , ||k||_\infty ,||v||_\infty   : i↤$\boldsymbol \\text analysis contextual approaches$\cap$$ adding nuance follow real tasks tables---but predictions macro dominated heavier contexts rely heavily could turn negligibly impactful nonetheless.$$

Ultimately these twin safeguards—local tiling scales and coherent rotation mixing—integrate seamlessly inside FlashAttention‑3’s asynchronous kernel architecture without sacrificing global logical correctness yet dramatically shrinking entropy loss precoceptually deciding discretisation noise conducing sharper modern-day transformer performance.«
