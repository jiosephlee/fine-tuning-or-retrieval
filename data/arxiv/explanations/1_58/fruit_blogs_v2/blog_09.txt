# MoE Meets 1-bit: Lower Memory, Lower Network, Higher Practicality

Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) layers are an appealing way to scale model capacity: instead of making every layer wider, MoE routes each token to a small subset of "expert" sub-networks, reducing FLOPs while keeping or increasing representational power.
