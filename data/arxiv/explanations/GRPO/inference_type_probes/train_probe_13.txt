According to the paper 'DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models', under outcome supervision the normalized reward is defined as $\widetilde{r}_i = \frac{r_i - \mathrm{mean}(\mathbf{r})}{\mathrm{std}(\mathbf{r})}$; if all group rewards are identical, the normalization leads to division by zero
