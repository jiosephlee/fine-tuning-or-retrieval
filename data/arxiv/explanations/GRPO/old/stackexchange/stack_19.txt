### Could this “cross-example attention” leak solutions into subsequent prompts?  
Yes, in principle every token in the context attends to every other token, so the model *can* see the tail of example $N$ and use it when generating an answer for example $N+1$.  If you imagine a stream  
$$
\langle\text{Prompt}_1\rangle \;\dots\; \langle\text{Solution}_1\rangle \;\langle\text{EOS}\rangle \;\langle\text{Prompt}_2\rangle \;\dots
$$  
the Transformer has full visibility over both.
