In the paper 'Direct Preference Optimization: Your Language Model is Secretly a Reward Model', the Bradley-Terry model defines pairwise preference probabilities as $p^*(y_1 \\succ y_2 \\mid x) = \\frac{\\phi(r(x,y_1))}{\\phi(r(x,y_1)) + \\phi(r(x,y_2))}$, which conceptually implies pairwise comparison probabilities are proportional to the exponential of each item's score
