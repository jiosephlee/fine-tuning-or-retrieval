In the paper 'Direct Preference Optimization: Your Language Model is Secretly a Reward Model', the authors note that the Bradley-Terry two-item comparison model, which can be written as $p^*(y_1\\succ y_2\\mid x)=\frac{\exp\bigl(r(x,y_1)\bigr)}{\exp\bigl(r(x,y_1)\bigr)+\exp\bigl(r(x,y_2)\bigr)}$ and equivalently as $p^*(y_1\succ y_2\mid x)=\sigma\left(r(x,y_1)-r(x,y_2)\right)$, is mathematically analogous to the common binary statistical model known as logistic regression
