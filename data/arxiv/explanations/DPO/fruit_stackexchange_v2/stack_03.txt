### Why does DPO sometimes outperform PPO even with ground-truth rewards (PPO-GT)?
Question:
In the sentiment control experiment, the reward–KL frontier for DPO dominates PPO and even PPO-GT (which uses the true reward). Conceptually, if both are optimizing the same KL-regularized objective, why can DPO exceed PPO with ground-truth rewards? Is the gap due to variance/baseline issues in PPO, insufficient exploration, or a mismatch in how the sequence-level KL is targeted? Any intuition on where PPO-GT loses optimality compared to maximizing the closed-form optimum implied by the DPO reparameterization?
