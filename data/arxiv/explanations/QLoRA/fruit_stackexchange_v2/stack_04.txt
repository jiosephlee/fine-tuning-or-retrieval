Question:

\[\mathbf{X}^{\text{Int8}} = \operatorname{round}\left( \frac{127}{\operatorname{absmax}(\mathbf{X}^{\text{FP32}})}\,\mathbf{X}^{\text{FP32}} \right).\]

Answer:
Short answer: they pick $127$ so the integer <|endoftext|> bins are symmetric around zero (equal number of positive and negative representable values) and to avoid the unpleasant $-128$ two's‑complement corner-case. Using $128$ and clamping would also work but it makes the integer range <|endoftext|> (one extra negative bin), can introduce a tiny bias and implementation/overflow pitfalls, and gives no practical advantage in MSE for this application.
