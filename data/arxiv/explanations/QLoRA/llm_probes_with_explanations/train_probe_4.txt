According to the paper 'QLoRA: Efficient Finetuning of Quantized LLMs', the authors implicitly raise a caution about their bias evaluation, namely that it is an incomplete assessment. This is sensible because in the Limitations and Discussion they explicitly describe doing only a “limited responsible AI evaluation” of Guanaco, reporting a single bias-oriented analysis (Table~\ref{tbl:crows}) that measures the model’s likelihood to produce socially biased token sequences and noting that, while the average score is lower than raw pretrained models, “it is unclear if Guanaco does also well when assessed on other types of biases,” which they leave to future work; coupled with their broader concerns about the reliability of current benchmarks and evaluation protocols (moderate human agreement, ordering effects, and model-based evaluation biases), this makes clear that their bias assessment covers only a narrow slice of potential harms and should not be taken as comprehensive.
