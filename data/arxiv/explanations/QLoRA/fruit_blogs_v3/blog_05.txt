# The LoRA Recipe That Matches Full Fine-tuning

Large language models (LLMs) are expensive to finetune. LoRA (Low-Rank Adapters) is a popular parameter-efficient finetuning (PEFT) method that injects small trainable low-rank matrices into linear layers while keeping the pretrained weights frozen. With the right placement and a few training tweaks, LoRA on a quantized 4-bit base model (QLoRA) can match 16-bit full finetuning performance across scales, up to tens of billions of parameters.
