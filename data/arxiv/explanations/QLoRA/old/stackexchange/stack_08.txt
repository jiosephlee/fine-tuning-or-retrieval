### Would updating quantization constants during finetuning help, and how would gradients be handled?
Question:
In QLoRA the base model weights and quantization constants are frozen. Hypothetically, if we wanted to finetune $c_1$ or the quantized $c_2$ (or even the NF4 codebook), how would you compute gradients through the quantization steps? Would a straight-through estimator be appropriate, and would the expected gains outweigh added instability or memory cost?
Answer:
In QLoRA we deliberately freeze both the quantized 4-bit weights and their associated scale factors (the “quantization constants” $c_1$, $c_2$) so that all of our update budget goes into the small LoRA adapters.  But nothing in principle prevents us from making those scales—or even the NF4 code-book values—learnable.  The main issues are (1) how to back-propagate through the non-differentiable rounding steps, and (2) whether the modest reduction in quantization error is worth the extra memory and potential optimization headaches.
