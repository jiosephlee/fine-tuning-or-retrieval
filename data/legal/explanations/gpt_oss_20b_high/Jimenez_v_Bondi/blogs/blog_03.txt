## Introduction – Why This Case Matters

The Tenth Circuit’s decision in *Jimenez v. Bondi* (No. 24‑9573, Oct. 7 2025) touches every practice setting where political activity collides with ordinary civil disputes abroad—and raises fresh questions about how “government unwillingness” is proved in today’s world where private actors wield almost as much power as ministries do on the ground.
