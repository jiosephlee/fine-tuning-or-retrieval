Title: Casebook chapter about the opinion: "Santos v. Kimmel"

# I – Facts and Procedural History  

## Parties Identity  
Former congressman **George Santos** files suit against **Jimmy Kimmel**, comedian and host of *Jimmy Kimmel Live!* (“JKL”); the defendants also include **ABC Television Network**, operator of JKL, and its parent **The Walt Disney Company** (“Disney”).  

## Cameo Requests  
Kimmel employed the subscriber‑video platform **Cameo**, requesting personalized clips from Santos—identifying himself under several fictitious names—to feature on his program. Each request was paid and fulfilled in characteristic remark‑style clips officially labeled “personalized.”  

## Airing on JKL  
Santos’s clips were incorporated into JKL’s recurring segment “Will Santos Say It?” where Kimmel broadcasted them live alongside comedic commentary meant parodying Santos’s persona rather than reproducing them verbatim for commercial gain.  

## Complaint Filed  
Santos sought relief on four grounds—copyright infringement, breach of contract under Cameo’s Terms‑of‑Service, breach of implied contract, and fraudulent inducement—in SDNY’s Southern District filing dated October 6 2025. He alleges unlawful copying and refusal of proper consultation periods essential for collated use adaption specifically tied to wrongful depictions reflected throughout JKL’s broadcast transitions.  

## Rule 12(b)(6) Dismissal
