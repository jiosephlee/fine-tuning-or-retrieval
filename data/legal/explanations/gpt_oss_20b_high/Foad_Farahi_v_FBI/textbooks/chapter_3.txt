## Hardy public declaration establishing compilation for counterterrorism investigations  

David Hardy’s initial *public* statement appeared together with the October 2019 summary‑judgment motion.

Critically his brief packet stretched two issues favored by FOIA precedent: (1) an explicit connection between those probes—which e.g., involved secret surveillance cells and intelligence collecting—and the agency’s core mission “to protect persons against threats,” which satisfies § 552(b)(7)(A)’s first requirement that a record be “compiled *for* law‑enforcement purposes.”
