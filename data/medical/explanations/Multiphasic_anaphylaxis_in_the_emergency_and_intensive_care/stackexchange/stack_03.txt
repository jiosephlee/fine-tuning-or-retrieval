### [Management] What is the recommended initial intramuscular epinephrine dose and site for adults with anaphylaxis, and what specific dose would you give a 104-kg patient?

Recommended initial intramuscular epinephrine for adult anaphylaxis is 0.01 mg/kg (10 µg/kg) up to a practical maximum of 0.5 mg per dose, given into the mid‑anterolateral thigh (vastus lateralis). The thigh provides the most reliable, rapid absorption compared with deltoid or subcutaneous injection and is the recommended site in all major guidelines.

For your 104‑kg patient the weight‑based calculation (0.01 mg/kg) would be 1.04 mg, but you do not give that as a single IM bolus — you cap the initial IM dose at 0.5 mg. Give 0.5 mg IM in the mid‑anterolateral thigh promptly, reassess within minutes, and repeat IM doses (0.3–0.5 mg every 5–15 minutes as needed) or escalate to intravenous therapy/infusion if refractory or hemodynamically unstable despite repeated IM doses.
