### [Clinical Pearls] List the ICBD item weights (oral aphthosis, genital aphthosis, ocular lesions, skin lesions, neurologic signs, vascular signs, pathergy) and the minimum total score that classifies Behçet’s disease.

The International Criteria for Behçet’s Disease (ICBD) use weighted clinical items. Oral aphthosis = 2 points, genital aphthosis = 2 points, ocular lesions (uveitis, retinal vasculitis) = 2 points, skin lesions = 1 point, neurologic signs = 1 point, vascular manifestations = 1 point, and a positive pathergy test = 1 point. A total score of ≥4 classifies a patient as having Behçet’s disease.  

Clinically, the higher weights for oral, genital and ocular lesions reflect their greater diagnostic specificity; the lower weights and the optional pathergy test improve sensitivity across different populations.
