Title: Clinical Q&A about the case report: "Acute neurological Behçet's disease complicated by a syndrome of inappropriate secretion of antidiuretic hormone"

### [Morning Report] What are the diagnostic criteria for Behçet's disease according to the International Criteria, and which clinical features in this patient fulfill those criteria, allowing a definitive diagnosis?

**International Criteria for Behçet’s Disease (ICBD, 2014)**  

| Feature | Points |
|---------|--------|
| Oral aphthous lesions | 2 |
| Genital ulcerations | 2 |
| Skin lesions (e.g., erythema nodosum, pseudofolliculitis, papulopustular rash) | 1 |
| Ocular lesions (uveitis, retinal vasculitis) | 2 |
| Neurologic manifestations (meningo‑encephalitis, focal deficits) | 1 |
| Vascular involvement (venous/arterial thrombosis, aneurysm) | 1 |

**Definite Behçet’s disease** = total score ≥ 4 (no laboratory or imaging requirement).

---

**Clinical features of the patient that meet the ICBD**

| Manifestation | Corresponding ICBD point(s) | Presentation in the case |
|----------------|---------------------------|---------------------------|
| Oral aphthous ulcers | 2 | Multiple painful aphthae noted on admission |
| Genital ulcerations | 2 | Painful scrotal ulcer appeared on day 5 |
| Skin lesions (folliculitis‑like rash on face and trunk) | 1 | Evanescent plaques described on physical exam |
| Neurologic involvement (aseptic meningitis) | 1 | CSF showed lymphocytic pleocytosis and elevated protein; MRI was normal, consistent with neuro‑Behçet’s |

**Score**: 2 + 2 + 1 + 1 = 6 points.  
The patient easily surpasses the ≥4 cutoff, yielding a **definitive diagnosis of Behçet’s disease**.

Other findings—HLA‑B51 positivity, elevated ESR/CRP, and SIADH—are supportive but not required by the ICBD. The fever and hyponatraemia are complications of neuro‑Behçet’s, not part of the diagnostic criteria. This case illustrates how the weighted ICBD allows prompt confirmation of BD even when the disease presents concurrently with neurologic and endocrine manifestations.
