Title: Clinical Q&A about the case report: "Central hypoventilation and choking episodes revealing Chiari malformation type I"

### [Clinical Pearls] What is the defining radiographic criterion for diagnosing Chiari malformation type I on sagittal MRI?

**Defining radiographic criterion for Chiari malformation type I**

On sagittal MRI a patient is diagnosed with Chiari malformation type I when the cerebellar tonsils descend **≥ 5 mm below the foramen magnum** (often measured from the foramen‑magnum plane or bony clivus). The classic example in this case report showed **11 mm tonsillar descent**, well above the diagnostic threshold and confirming the diagnosis.
