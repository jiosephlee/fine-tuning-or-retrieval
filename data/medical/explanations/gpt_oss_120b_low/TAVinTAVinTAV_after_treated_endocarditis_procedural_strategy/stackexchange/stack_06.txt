Title: Clinical Q&A about the case report: "TAV-in-TAV-in-TAV after treated endocarditis: procedural strategy, imaging planning and 1-year outcome"

### [Clinical Pearls] List the Valve Academic Research Consortium‑3 (VARC‑3) criteria used to define procedural success in this case and indicate which of these criteria were borderline or not met immediately post‑procedure.

**VARC‑3 procedural‑success criteria (as applied to this case)**  

| Criterion (VARC‑3) | What it requires | Immediate post‑procedure result in this patient | Interpretation |
|--------------------|------------------|-----------------------------------------------|----------------|
| **Device success** | • Correct valve positioning (≤ 10 %‑15 % of annular circumference mal‑position)  <br>• Single‑step implantation without need for a second valve <br>• No > moderate trans‑valvular or para‑valvular regurgitation  <br>• No device‑related death, coronary obstruction, annular rupture, migration or embolisation | • Acurate neo2 was deployed in the intended supra‑annular position, aligned with the Evolut leaflets; no second valve was required.<br>• Final aortography and immediate TTE showed **grade 0 aortic regurgitation** and no coronary compromise. | **Met** – all device‑success elements were satisfied. |
| **Procedural success** (clinical component) | • Successful implantation of the intended valve **and** <br>• **Hemodynamic criteria**: mean aortic gradient < 20 mm Hg **or** aortic valve area > 1.0 cm² (indexed > 0.6 cm²/m²) **and** <br>• Absence of ≥ moderate patient‑prosthesis mismatch (PPM) (indexed AVA ≥ 0.65 cm²/m² for non‑obese, ≥ 0.55 cm²/m² for obese) <br>• No procedural complications (major vascular, stroke, death, need for emergency surgery, etc.) within 30 days | • **Mean gradient** immediately after implantation: **67 mm Hg** (far > 20 mm Hg).<br>• **AVA**: 0.9 cm² (indexed 0.4 cm²/m²) → severe PPM (indexed < 0.55 cm²/m²).<br>• No major vascular complications, stroke, death, or need for bail‑out surgery reported. | **Borderline / not met** – the hemodynamic thresholds for procedural success were **not achieved** immediately after the procedure (both gradient and indexed AVA were outside the VARC‑3 limits, representing severe PPM). | **30‑day safety endpoint** (part of procedural success) | • No all‑cause mortality, stroke, life‑threatening bleeding, major vascular complication, or new permanent pacemaker implantation attributable to the procedure. | • No deaths, strokes, or major bleeding reported in the 30‑day period. ### Summary for the teaching point  

- **Device success** was fully achieved: the third valve was deployed in the planned position, coronary flow was preserved, and there was **no residual regurgitation**.  
- **Procedural success**, however, hinges on the **hemodynamic component**. In this patient the **immediate post‑implant mean gradient of 67 mm Hg and indexed AVA of 0.4 cm²/m²** signaled **severe patient‑prosthesis mismatch**, thus the VARC‑3 “procedural success” definition was **borderline/not met** at that moment.  
- Importantly, the gradient fell to 27 mm Hg and indexed AVA rose to 0.7 cm²/m² by 1 month, satisfying the hemodynamic thresholds thereafter, but VARC‑3 counts success at the **time of discharge/30 days**, not at later follow‑up.  

**Teaching pearl:** In complex TAV‑in‑TAV‑in‑TAV cases, technical deployment may be flawless, yet the VARC‑3 procedural‑success metric can still be unmet if early haemodynamics reveal severe PPM. Meticulous pre‑procedural CT planning (to optimise valve size, implantation depth and commissural alignment) is essential, but even with ideal planning, residual gradients may initially be high and improve only after remodeling and post‑dilatation. Recognising this distinction helps trainees set realistic expectations and appreciate that “device success” does not automatically equal “procedural success” under VARC‑3 criteria.
