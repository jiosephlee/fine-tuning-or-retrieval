Title: Clinical Q&A about the case report: From dog bite to dialysis: complement-mediated haemolytic uraemic syndrome

### [Differential Diagnosis] Which test should be obtained urgently to evaluate for thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura (TTP) in an adult with MAHA and thrombocytopenia, and what activity threshold effectively rules in or out TTP?

Obtain an ADAMTS13 activity assay urgently — drawn before any plasma exchange or plasma infusion — because the diagnosis of TTP is laboratory-defined by severe ADAMTS13 deficiency. An activity level <10% (some labs use ≤10%) effectively confirms TTP and mandates immediate plasma exchange and immunosuppression, whereas activity well above that threshold (generally >10–15%) makes TTP unlikely and should prompt evaluation for alternative TMAs (HUS, complement‑mediated TMA, DIC, secondary causes). Draw a citrated plasma sample, freeze or send per your lab’s protocol, and use clinical tools such as the PLASMIC score to decide empiric PEX while awaiting results. Remember that prior plasma therapy can raise ADAMTS13 activity, so the sample should be obtained before treatment whenever possible.
