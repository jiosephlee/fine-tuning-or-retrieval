Title: Clinical Q&A about the case report: "Monoallelic PARN mutation presenting as pancytopenia, hepatic fibrosis and idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis"

### [Management] What additional diagnostic test is considered a key confirmatory study in suspected telomere biology disorders beyond next-generation sequencing, and how would its result strengthen the diagnosis in this patient?

A key confirmatory test in suspected telomere biology disorder is **telomere length assessment by flow cytometry–fluorescence in situ hybridization (flow-FISH)**.

In this patient, a **telomere length below the age-adjusted normal range** would strongly support the diagnosis of a telomere biology disorder, especially when interpreted alongside her clinical phenotype: premature greying, cytopenias, hepatic fibrosis, and pulmonary fibrosis. In practice, a markedly shortened telomere length provides the functional evidence that the identified **monoallelic PARN variant** is biologically relevant, helping move the diagnosis from a genetic suspicion based on next-generation sequencing alone to a **confirmed telomere-mediated disorder**.
