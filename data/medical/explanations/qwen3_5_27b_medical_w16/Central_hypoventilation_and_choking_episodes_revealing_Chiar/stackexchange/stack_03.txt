Title: Clinical Q&A about the case report: "Central hypoventilation and choking episodes revealing Chiari malformation type I"

### [Differential Diagnosis] What is the unifying diagnosis for a patient presenting with the triad of chronic occipital headache, neurogenic dysphagia, and unexplained central hypoventilation?

The unifying diagnosis for this patient is **Chiari malformation type I (CMI)**.

This case illustrates that CMI can present with a distinctive triad of chronic occipital headache, neurogenic dysphagia, and unexplained central hypoventilation, mimicking acquired central hypoventilation syndrome (Ondine’s curse). The patient’s 14-year history of occipital headaches following trauma, combined with recurrent choking episodes affecting both solids and liquids, initially led to extensive but unrevealing gastrointestinal and pulmonary evaluations. The diagnosis was ultimately established by MRI confirmation of an 11 mm cerebellar tonsillar descent and the retrospective identification of a crowded foramen magnum on a CT scan performed 14 years prior.

The pathophysiology unifying these symptoms centers on brainstem compression and cerebrospinal fluid outflow obstruction at the craniocervical junction. The caudal displacement of the cerebellar tonsils compresses medullary structures, disrupting lower cranial nerve nuclei to cause oropharyngeal dysfunction and choking. Simultaneously, compression of the respiratory control centres impairs automatic breathing while sparing voluntary drive, resulting in central apnoea and severe oxygen desaturation without the sensation of dyspnoea.

Clinically, this case underscores the importance of considering structural brainstem abnormalities in patients with unexplained respiratory dysfunction, particularly when accompanied by bulbar signs or occipital headaches. Normal pulmonary function tests and endoscopic evaluations should prompt neuroimaging to rule out posterior fossa pathology, as posterior fossa decompression may potentially reverse respiratory symptoms by restoring CSF flow dynamics.
