Title: Clinical Q&A about the case report: "Management of atypical complicated abdominal masses in the second trimester of pregnancy"

### [Morning Report] What intraoperative monitoring is mandatory to ensure fetal viability during non-obstetric abdominal surgery in the second trimester?

To ensure fetal viability during non-obstetric abdominal surgery in the second trimester, it is mandatory to check and document the fetal heart rate before, during, and after the procedure. In the presented case, a laparotomy was performed at 21 weeks’ gestation to address large, symptomatic uterine leiomyomas causing severe pain and dyspnea. To maintain fetal safety during this intervention, the surgical team utilized serial and intermittent transvaginal ultrasounds to monitor fetal viability throughout the operation. 

This monitoring protocol aligns with multicentric study recommendations cited in the discussion, which emphasize that verifying fetal cardiac activity at all stages of surgery is essential. By combining this surveillance with intraoperative progesterone prophylaxis, the team successfully managed the maternal pathology while preserving the pregnancy, resulting in an excellent neonatal outcome.
