Title: Clinical Q&A about the case report: "Autoimmune heparin-induced thrombocytopenia following cardiac surgery"

### [Management] After the initial management with argatroban and IVIg, the patient was switched to therapeutic‑dose fondaparinux. Discuss the rationale for adding antithrombin concentrate before fondaparinux initiation.

**Why an antithrombin concentrate was given before therapeutic‑dose fondaparinux**

Fondaparinux is a synthetic pentasaccharide that inhibits factor Xa **indirectly, via antithrombin (AT)**. In a patient with recent cardiac surgery and severe thrombocytopenia, the plasma AT concentration is often below normal because of consumption during the hypercoagulable phase of HIT and by the surgical inflammatory response. The case report reports that the patient’s AT level was 0.70 U mL⁻¹ (interval 0.80–1.40 U mL⁻¹) just before initiation of fondaparinux.

1. **Potency of fondaparinux depends on AT.**  
   In AT‑deficient plasma, the dose‑response curve of fondaparinux shifts to the right: the same weight‑based dose yields only 50 % of the expected anti‑Xa activity. Consequently, the anticoagulant effect is sub‑therapeutic, exposing the patient to ongoing thrombus formation—especially dangerous in aHIT, where platelet activation is relentless.

2. **Risk of under‑anticoagulation is highest in aHIT.**  
   The patient had evidence of a consumptive coagulopathy (falling fibrinogen, rising d‑dimer, schistocytes) while on argatroban, implying that the HIT‑associated hypercoagulable state was not fully controlled. Switching to fondaparinux without adequate AT could have left the patient inadequately anticoagulated and at risk of further thrombotic events, such as the observed cerebral infarct.

3. **Guidelines and pharmacologic evidence.**  
   The American Society of Hematology recommends that patients with HIT who are switched to fondaparinux receive antithrombin concentrate if the baseline AT activity is <70 % of normal (or <0.7 U mL⁻¹). Several case series and small trials have confirmed that a 1000‑U bolus of pooled AT restores fondaparinux’s full anti‑Xa potency and that omitting AT leads to sub‑therapeutic anti‑Xa levels.

Thus, the addition of 1000 U AT concentrate before the first therapeutic dose of fondaparinux ensured that the synthetic pentasaccharide would exert its full anticoagulant effect, preventing further thrombotic complications in this patient with severe autoimmune HIT.
