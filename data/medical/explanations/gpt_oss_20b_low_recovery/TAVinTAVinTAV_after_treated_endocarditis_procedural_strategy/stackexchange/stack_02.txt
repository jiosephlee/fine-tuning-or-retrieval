Title: Clinical Q&A about the case report: "TAV-in-TAV-in-TAV after treated endocarditis: procedural strategy, imaging planning and 1-year outcome"

### [Differential Diagnosis] In this patient with prior endocarditis and two embolized prosthetic valves, what clinical and imaging criteria were used to decide that a third TAVI was preferable to redo surgery?

**Answer**

In this patient the decision for a third TAVI rather than redo surgery was based on a *multifactorial, multidisciplinary assessment* that integrated clinical risk, infection resolution, and detailed imaging evidence of anatomical feasibility.

| **Domain** | **Key Criteria** | **Evidence from the Case** |
|------------|------------------|----------------------------|
| **Surgical risk** | EuroSCORE II ≥ 25 % → prohibitive | 27.36 %, plus COPD, diabetes, prior GI bleed, 2 prior TAVI‑related vascular injury → “prohibitive surgical risk”. |
| **Infection status** | Complete resolution of endocarditis; negative cultures; normal TEE/CT | 6 weeks IV antibiotics completed, no vegetations on TOE or MSCT, normal hematologic markers. |
| **Hemodynamics** | Severe stenosis (mean gradient ≥ 49 mm Hg, AVA = 0.5 cm²) plus moderate‑to‑severe regurgitation | TTE: mean 49 mm Hg, AVA 0.5 cm², grade 2–3 IR. |
| **Anatomical feasibility (CT‑guided)** | • Minimum lumen > 13 mm in Evolut frame < 19 mm needed for a new valve < 23 mm. <br>• Coronary ostia height & sinus size sufficient to avoid obstruction. <br>• Predicted neo‑skirt clearance > 4 mm. | MSCT: minimum lumen 13.7 mm; coronary‑to‑frame distance > 4 mm; sinus dimensions adequate for Acurate neo2 S (17.5 mm). |
| **Procedural strategy** | • High‑implantation to align with Evolut leaflets, preserving effective orifice area. <br>• Commissural alignment to maintain future coronary access. <br>• Ability to use rapid pacing, balloon pre‑/post‑dilation, no need for coronary protection. | Pre‑procedural CT planning defined high‑implantation depth; fluoroscopic landmarks used for commissural alignment; aortography showed unobstructed coronaries after pre‑dilation. |
| **Expected benefit** | Restitution of forward flow, improved symptoms, avoidance of open aortic root replacement and its morbidity. | Post‑procedure TTE: mean gradient 27 mm Hg, AVA 1.1 cm², EF 66 %; patient asymptomatic at 1 yr. |

**Bottom line:**  
The team judged that “redo surgery would require extensive aortic root replacement in a patient with high operative mortality, while a CT‑guided third TAVI offered a safe, anatomically feasible, and physiologically superior alternative”. The decision was endorsed by the heart‑team, with shared consent, and complied with ESC 2025 guidelines for PVE that advise individualised management when infection is cleared and surgical risk is high.
