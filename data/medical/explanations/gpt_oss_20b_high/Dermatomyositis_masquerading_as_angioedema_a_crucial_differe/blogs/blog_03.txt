#### **Key Take-Aways**

* Neck swelling can masquerade dermatomyositis-driven inflammation rather than classical histamine/bradykinin-mediated angioedema—especially when accompanied by subtle proximal weakness or nailfold changes.*
* An elevated creatine kinase level is mandatory when encountering unexplained cervical edemas—even if rash isn’t prominent initially.*
* Anti‑SAE₁ antibody positivity predicts higher rates of dysphagia (~45%) and interstitial lung disease (~40%), requiring comprehensive malignancy screening according to IMACS high-risk criteria.*
* Prompt aggressive therapy—high-dose pulse steroids followed quickly by steroid-sparing agents (MMF preferred if ILD concerning) +/monthly IVIG ± rituximab—is linked with durable remission despite severe extra-muscular manifestations.*
* A multidisciplinary approach—ENT assessment paired tightly with rheumatology/immunology review—cuts time-to-diagnosis dramatically preventing airway complications.*
