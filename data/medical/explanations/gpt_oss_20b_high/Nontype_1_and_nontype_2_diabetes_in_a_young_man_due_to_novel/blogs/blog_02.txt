## Why Did We Start With Basal Insulin?

### Mimicking Type II  
Heterozygous missense mutations in **HNF¹⁰** are almost synonymous with progressive β‑cell failure that resembles late-onset type II rather than fulminant autoimmune destruction. In practice it is difficult—and sometimes mandatory—to give an unfamiliar drug right away when an agency hasn’t confirmed a monogenic culprit yet:

* **No quick commercial tests for MODY upfront**, so clinicians defaulted to something familiar that controls ↑ postprandial glycaemia aggressively while keeping ketosis off‐side = basal insulines inaugurated & treated suprisingly promptly.
