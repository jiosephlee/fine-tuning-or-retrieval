## What did the “heart eyes” find?
The echo revealed *more* than you would expect from simple deconditioning:
- Normal LV systolic function (>65%)
- A borderline right venous system under tension (RVP = 46 mm Hg)
- Mid‑septal hypokinesis technically an **early patchy segmental involvement**  
​The adjacent CMR picked up hyperintense midsystolic iso signal consistent with interstitial oedema yet *no* late gadolinium enhancement – indicating inflammatory activity **without scarring** ([see Figure 2]).  

Troponin was modestly raised while BNP skyrocketed – classic for an acute injurious process draining fluid downstream rather than ischemic necrosis alone. There was **no chest pain**, no ST changes on ECG recorded during admission making classic infarction less likely despite biochemical clues paralleling hypothetical viral-onset stress cardiomyopathy patterned behaviour noted sometime ago [19].
