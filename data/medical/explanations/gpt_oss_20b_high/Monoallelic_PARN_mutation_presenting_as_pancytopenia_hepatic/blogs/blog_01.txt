#### Take–home pearls

• **Early-onset pancytopenia unresponsive after correcting classic nutritional deficiencies warrants exploration for inherited bone-marrow failure syndromes—including telomere biology disorders (TBDs).**

• **Subtle premature-aging signs (premature greying hair, nail changes even if mild) should flag suspect dermato-mucosal triad absence—not eliminate age-related disorders entirely.**

• **Non-invasive modalities—Fibroscan liver stiffness errand plus chest HRCT/PET showing ground-glass change—are powerful surrogates when ordering biopsies becomes ethically dubious given patient youthliness and comorbidities**

• **A screen via flow-FISH measuring peripheral blood telomere length followed promptly by targeted—or whole-exome—to identify coding variants across TERT/TERC/Rtel/Hh gating gene sets ensures accurate diagnosis while acknowledging VUS status can still change management**

• **Supportive care encompasses careful transfusion plan, rigorous vitamin supplementation strategy tailored per functional deficits plus monitoring criticisms every six months surveillance vital until definitive therapy positions…**
