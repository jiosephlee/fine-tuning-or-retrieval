## What makes this case remarkable  

A healthy woman in her late forties presents four days post dog bite with septic shock, pancytopenia and disseminated intravascular coagulation (DIC). Blood cultures grow *Capnocytophaga canimorsus* after a week’s incubation—a slow‐growing bacterium that rarely makes headlines outside infectious disease circles. Her picture improves once antibiotics are switched to ampicillin/penicillin G against *C.* canimorsus, yet six days later she suddenly loses half her hemoglobin without overt bleeding while her platelets rebound only modestly to ~35 × 10⁹/L. A peripheral smear lately demonstrates schistocytes (>15%), haptoglobin falls below detection limits, LDH rises sharply—all hallmarks of microangiopathic hemolytic anemia (MAHA).  

The differential narrows toward thrombotic microangiopathy (TMA), but two entities sit directly opposite each other on treatment choice spectrum :  
* thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura (TTP), where life-saving plasma exchange (PEX) is mandatory regardless — wait “not‐tests” lag months —  
* complement‐mediated hemolytic uremic syndrome (CM‑HUS), where prompt termination of complement‐overdrive via eculizumab dramatically improves renal recovery.



When an ICU team faces such a tableau—and it almost occurs every night—the *PLASMIC* score plus an instant *ADAMTS13* assay change everything.”

## Rapid triage logic in practice  

### Step 0 – Gather bedside data NOW  
On arrival me obtain buffy‐coat numbers **platelet count**, **Hb/Hct**, **severely elevated LDH (>2×ULN)**, **haptoglobin** (>10 mg/dL → “rule out” strong hemolysis), **reticulocyte count**, **direct antiglobulin test**, **’causes’​/T‐index** (**INR<1½**), all *within four hours*. A prompt manual peripheral smear fills a faster five minutes window versus delayed flow cytometry—there is little room for waiting.\  

### Step 1 – Compute the **PLAT**Smodified MICRObiopathologic Index (**PLASMICS**)

| Variable | Why it matters |
|----------|----------------|
| Platelets <30 ×10⁹/L | Severe consumption signals “classic” endothelial injury |
| Schistocytes ≥1 % / LDH >2×ULN | Objective proof that red cells are being fragmented |
| No evidence of prior AIDS / malignancy / transplantation | These conditions raise background risk faints |
| Serum creatinine ≤2 mg/dL | Acute kidney injury alone does not mandate PEX |
| Normal INR (<1․½) | Point mutation denoting minimal coagulation cascade hyperactivation |
| No ongoing heparin exposure | Heparin‐induced thrombocytopenia would confound counts |
| Age under /*≤65 yrs*/ | Younger cohort shows higher overall predilection |

Score ranges from **0–7** :

* **6–7** → very high pretest probability → *start PEX immediately* even before lab confirmation  
* **3–5** → medium certainty → *send rapid ADAMTS13 assay AND PEX as bridge service unless stability allows delayed*.  
* **≤2** → very low → *monitor closely*, get confirmatory studies ASAP before committing resources toward PEX.\  

> In our dog‑bite patient the calculated score was precisely **4/7**, putting her right smack ‘midway’. The AMB records spelled modest platelet despondency yet did *not* tick the “platelet<30×10⁹/L” bin outright—our clinical gut prompted us either way while awaiting real numbers.\  

### Step 2 – Turn @speed—Send *ADAMTS13 Activity*.  

Most centers now ship protocols enabling <3 hour turnarounds when assays run locally or via national dedicated networks. Even an “intermediate” value such as **≈40 %** immediately tells you you’re solidly away from classic TTP (`<10 %`). When ABDM TS [with verb], ephemeral nuance arises only if you have a pending suspicion strong enough —the next step hinges entirely on those numbers.\  

### Step 3 – Reaction pathways based on adjudicated result  

| Result | Treatment Path             | Rationale |
|--------|----------------------------|-----------|
| `<10 %` | Rapid initiation & continuation of PEX loop | Intravenous replacement removes ultra-large VWF multimers fixed intracellularly – emergent lifesaver |
| `>20 %` & KL : positive STX assay sentinel negative | Complement blockade (`eculizumab`) soon after infectious source resolved + prophylactic meningococcal coverage | IgG autoantibody negativity eliminates immune hemolytic options → the “excess complement activation” narrative dominates |
| `20–50 %` despite deny other grounds | Bilateral assessment–non-TPP paradigm + review hypogeneic triggers school(s); usually MCV/HV bypass routine mania until relevance suffices |

Our patient landed firmly into the `>20 %` bucket once swift enzymatic analysis returned (`43 %`). This effectively ruled out Thrombotic Thrombocytopenic Purpura ‑ a big win since unleashing thousands of plasma units gives absolutely no benefit if there isn’t an amyloid egg-laden VWF substrate.\
