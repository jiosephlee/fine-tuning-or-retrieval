Chapter 5: ## Investigation for Underlying Mast Cell Disorders  
In cases where anaphylaxis recurs more than twice despite adequate initial therapy—especially when trigger identification fails—contemplating an underlying clonal mast‐cell proliferative disorder becomes essential. Systemic mastocytosis (SM) represents the prototypical disease capable of predisposing patients to frequent biphasic or multiphasic reactions through continuous mediator release from tissue‐infiltrating leukemic‐like mast cells.[^1]  

**WHO Diagnostic Framework – Major/Minor Criteria**  
The World Health Organization classifies SM by combining one major criterion—or at least three minor ones—to establish pathology definitively.[^2]  
*Major Criterion:* Dense aggregates (>15 mast cells per high‑power field) identified by combined morphologic estimation and chromogranin A or tryptase immunostaining in bone marrow aspirates or biopsies.[^3]  
*Minor Criteria Include:*  
1) Serum baseline tryptase level >20 µg/L.[^4]  
2) More than two clonally distinct expressions {\displaystyle CD25} or {\displaystyle CD2} surface markers detectable by flow cytometry.[^5]  
3) Identification of activating KIT mutations (usually D816V).  
4) Presence of lesions showing organ damage attributable to excessive tissue infiltration (e.g., hepatomegaly/splenomegaly).[^6]  

In our patient only two minor clues emerged—a persistently raised basal tryptase (>35 µg/L at multiple points) and focal hepatic enlargement on ultrasound—which place her assumption into the “probable” SM category because neither major criterion was fulfilled nor were three independent minor markers demonstrated simultaneously due to limited biopsy material.*

**Tryptase Threshold > 30 µg/L Indicates Probable Disease**  
While <20 µg/L establishes normalcy according to conventional guidelines, a sustained level exceeding 30 µg/L markedly elevates suspicion for clonal proliferation rather than transient post‑reaction elevation.[^7] Importantly, our measurements were taken moments after each recurrence instead of during immediate crisis peaks (<4 h post‑symptom onset), thereby reflecting true basal secretion rather than spurious transient spikes commonly encountered following acute allergic insults.[^8] A persistent basal level >30 µg/L has been associated with near‑cooperative predictive power across multicenter studies,[^9] suggesting that beyond serving as such a marker it could function prognostically by signifying greater tissue burden susceptible for repeated discharges.*

**Liver Imaging – Hepatomegaly Correlation**  
Extra‑cutaneous organ involvement constitutes one ancillary evidence arm within WHO’s framework. Radiologic findings such as hepatomegaly manifest crystalline mast‐cell infiltration—a feature corroborated by serial ultrasound scans obtaining enlarged hepatic dimensions relative to normative age‑sex cohorts.[^10] Beyond satisfying a criterion technically classified under “organ damage”, liver enlargement signals systemic veno‑aggregative activity that predisposes patients toward repeated mediator release upon minimal antigen exposure.*

**Bone Marrow Aspirate Limitations When Incomplete**  
Because dense infiltrates must be visualized microscopically within sufficient cellularity samples[^3], inadequate aspiration limiting cellular harvest effectively eliminates assessment capacity for both the major clustering criterion and many minor markers based on flow cytometry expression phenotypes.[^11] Indeed, our patient’s fragmentary cytology precluded confirming either large aggregate clusters required by WHO’s primary rule nor subtle multi‑lineage surface protein aberrancies among marrows cells despite pathognomonic histolog­ical hints at best available [see Figure 2]. Thus bone‑marrow incompleteness mandates alternate calibration pathways utilizing PCR‐based mutational analyses.*

**KIT D816V Mutation Screening Utility**  
The most common activating somatic transmutation targets exon 17 glycine‑to‑valine change K D 816 V discovered through two divergent approaches:[^12]  

1) **Reverse transcription PCR** from peripheral leukocytes capitalizing high clones population found at ~70–80 % allele burden where feasible during treatment gaps [precludes retesting].  
2) **Next‑generation sequencing panels** that further categorize subclonal load allowing functional correlation regarding therapy thresholds e.g., midostaurin sensitivity sets.^[13]  

Presence confirms clonality fulfilling one minor marker even when morphological features are obscured.* Additional assays help rule out mosaicism which could masquerade as repeat episodic allergic responses.*

* **Rapid re‑assessment windows:** repositioned monitoring intervals from default six hours downwards towards parallel action protocols requiring immediate second‑dose possibility against potential biphasic peaks identified using tracked triglyceride pattern.* 
* **Longer discharge observation period:** maintaining >=48–72 h bedside telemetry if elevation persists.*
