Chapter 3: ## Biphasic versus Multiphasic Anaphylaxis: Definitions & Epidemiology  

**Unified definition**  
A standard working definition separates anaphylactic courses according both onset speed and duration until relapse. Biphasia refers to a single recurrence that arises after complete clinical resolution and occurs within a period ranging from less than five hours up to forty‑eight hours following initial symptom disappearance—the window deemed clinically meaningful because most relapses peak prior to the end‑of‑day error boundary documented in most prospective registries. Multipharismic reactions comprise two or more distinct recurrences separated by complete symptom voids before eventual recovery; these typically extend beyond forty‑eight hours and can span several days, sometimes approaching three‑to‑four day courses if underlying mediator release persists unknottedly—as exemplified by the patient who relapsed repeatedly over twelve hours post‑admission before finally quiescing after twenty‐four hours off treatment.
