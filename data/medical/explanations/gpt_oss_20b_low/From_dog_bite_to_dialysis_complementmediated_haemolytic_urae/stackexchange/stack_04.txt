Title: Clinical Q&A about the case report: "From dog bite to dialysis: complement-mediated haemolytic uraemic syndrome"

### [Differential Diagnosis] Why was thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura (TTP) considered unlikely in this patient, and what clinical score and laboratory value were most decisive?

**Answer**

TTP was considered unlikely because the patient’s *PLASMIC* score was only 4 / 7—below the threshold (>6) that predicts a high pre‑test probability of severe ADAMTS13 deficiency—and the ADAMTS13 activity measured was 43.5 %.  A value above 10 % virtually excludes the severe (<10 %) ADAMTS13 deficiency characteristic of TTP.  Thus, the combination of a low PLASMIC score and an ADAMTS13 activity well above the diagnostic cutoff was the decisive evidence against TTP in this case.
